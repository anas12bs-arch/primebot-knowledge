# IMF Urges RBA to Be Ready to Tighten Further If Prices Worsen

Australia’s monetary authorities should stand ready to raise interest rates further if price pressures are more persistent, the International Monetary Fund said, while calling on the government to deliver on pledges to tighten fiscal policy.
