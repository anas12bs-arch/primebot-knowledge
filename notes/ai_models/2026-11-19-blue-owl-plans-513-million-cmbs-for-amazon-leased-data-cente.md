# Blue Owl Plans $513 Million CMBS for Amazon-Leased Data Center

Blue Owl Capital Inc. is preparing to sell $513 million of commercial mortgage backed securities to fund the acquisition of a Northern Virginia data center that’s fully leased to Amazon.com Inc., according to people with knowledge of the matter.
