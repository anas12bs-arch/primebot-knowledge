# SEC Alleges Fund Adviser Misled Investors in Pre-IPO Schemes

Investment adviser Adit Ventures and co-founder Eric Munson were accused by the US Securities and Exchange Commission of making false claims about investments and overcharging clients with millions of dollars in undisclosed fees to buy shares of companies including SpaceX and Klarna before they were offered to the public.
