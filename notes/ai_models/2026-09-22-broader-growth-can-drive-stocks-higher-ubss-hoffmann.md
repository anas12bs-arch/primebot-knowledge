# Broader Growth Can Drive Stocks Higher: UBS's Hoffmann

Ulrike Hoffmann-Burchardi, global equities CIO at UBS Global Wealth Management, says resilient earnings can drive the next leg higher for stocks as government, corporate and consumer spending support growth. She adds that the AI investment boom may also be underestimated as it expands into robotics and the physical economy. This interview aired Sept. 21, 2026. (Source: Bloomberg)
