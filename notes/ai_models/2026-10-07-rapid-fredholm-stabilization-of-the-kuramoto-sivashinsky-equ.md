# Rapid Fredholm stabilization of the Kuramoto--Sivashinsky equation with unrestricted, spatially-varying anti-diffusion

We develop the first feedback design for rapid stabilization of the Kuramoto--Sivashinsky equation with a spatially varying anti-diffusion coefficient. For constant coefficients, the single-input Fredholm design of Coron and Lü (2015) excludes a discrete set of values at which repeated unstable eigenvalues cause a loss of controllability.
