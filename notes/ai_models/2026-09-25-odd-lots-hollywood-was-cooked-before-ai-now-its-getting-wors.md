# Odd Lots: Hollywood Was Cooked Before AI. Now It’s Getting Worse

Is Hollywood just screwed? The American entertainment business has been on a steady decline since the pandemic and now AI has entered the picture.

On this episode of the Odd Lots podcast, creative director of LA Material, Hayes Davenport, joins Tracy Alloway and Joe Weisenthal at a live recording at the Vermont Hollywood theater to discuss how generative AI is threatening the movie making business and the ways in which Hollywood is pushing back.
