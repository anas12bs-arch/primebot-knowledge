# Is 4-Bit Quantization More Than Noise to a Chat Model's Safety Behaviour? Attributing Behavioural Shifts with Matched Perturbations

Chat models on phones and laptops are usually 4-bit copies of the model that was evaluated, and audits of such copies disagree about what quantization does to refusal. A 4-bit build is one of many weight perturbations of the same size, so a change in its behaviour could come from its specific rounding error or from any perturbation that large.
