# JSE Courts Dangote Refinery Secondary Listing in Expansion Push

Africa’s biggest stock exchange is working with Dangote Group to pave the way for a potential secondary listing of its giant oil refinery in Johannesburg, an outcome the bourse’s chief said would be the ideal next step after its landmark Nigerian initial public offering.
