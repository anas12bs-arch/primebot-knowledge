# Arc’teryx CEO on Product Expansion & Consumer Demand

Stuart Haselden CEO of Arc'teryx discusses the company's aims to nearly double revenue over the next five years by building on two strategic priorities. The company has focused on technical innovation and performance products, while divesting lifestyle products it believed diluted its brand authority. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close."
