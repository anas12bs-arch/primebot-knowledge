# Ascend Sport Said to Mull Listing in Up to $740 Million Merger

Switzerland’s Ascend Sport Technology AG is exploring strategic options, including a reverse merger with a listed shell company that could value the firm between 500 million and 600 million Swiss francs ($740 million), according to people familiar with matter.
