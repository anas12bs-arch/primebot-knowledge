# MIRAGE: Motion Identity Reduction via Autoregressive Generative Encoding for Privacy-Preserving Skeleton-based Motion Data

Skeleton motion identifies people through body proportions and movement style, and skeleton streams are increasingly passed to third-party analytics that run action classifiers trained on raw data. A prior adversarial anonymizer adds a per-frame residual in the raw coordinate space but sees one frame at a time; motion retargeting needs a target skeleton and complete sequences, which rules out online use. We present MIRAGE, a target-free, causal anonymizer that adds an identity-reducing residual
