# Fine-Grained Japanese Clinical Named Entity Recognition: A Controlled Comparison of BERT and Open-Weight Large Language Models

Background: Generative large language models (LLMs) offer an alternative to encoder-based clinical named entity recognition (NER), but their performance on fine-grained Japanese extraction tasks and sensitivity to generation settings remain uncertain. Methods: We compared two fine-tuned BERT-CRF baselines with nine open-weight LLMs on iCorpus, which defines 113 entity categories.
