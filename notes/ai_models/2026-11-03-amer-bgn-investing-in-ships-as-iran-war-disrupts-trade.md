# Amer: BGN Investing in Ships as Iran War Disrupts Trade

BGN is investing in more vessels, including VLCCs, as shipping companies navigate disruptions from the Iran war. Wael Amer, group chief operating officer at BGN, spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar about the company’s investments aimed at helping the energy trader continue reaching customers during difficult market conditions.
