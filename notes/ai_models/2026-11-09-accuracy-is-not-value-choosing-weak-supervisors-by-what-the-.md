# Accuracy Is Not Value: Choosing Weak Supervisors by What the Student Inherits

Weak supervisors are usually judged by their own errors, but what matters is what a student learns from them. For squared loss and any linear learner shared by all supervisors, we show that a supervisor's value is determined by its errors after they pass through the student's learning operator. Consequently, the inner products of the supervisors' raw errors can be compatible with every feasible value of the student's risk, and weighting supervisors by them can be arbitrarily worse than the best
