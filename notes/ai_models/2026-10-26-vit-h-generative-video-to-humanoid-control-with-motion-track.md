# ViT-H: Generative Video-To-Humanoid Control with Motion Tracking Feedback

Yet, existing reconstruction-retargeting-tracking pipelines are limited in system-level scalability by separately optimized intermediate stages and cannot directly optimize reference motion executability using downstream tracking feedback.
