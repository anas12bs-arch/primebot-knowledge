# Dutch Bros CEO Christine Barone on Expansion & Inflation

Christine Barone, President & CEO of Dutch Bros, discussed the company’s aggressive growth strategy, saying the chain aims to reach 2,029 shops by 2029 and expects to add at least 185 locations this year. She said that pace is supported by a strong internal talent pipeline, with more than 500 operators ready to move into new states and markets as the company expands. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
