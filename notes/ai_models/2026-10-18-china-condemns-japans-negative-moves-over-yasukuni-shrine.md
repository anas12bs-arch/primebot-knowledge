# China Condemns Japan’s ‘Negative Moves’ Over Yasukuni Shrine

China strongly condemned what it called Japan’s “negative moves” concerning the Yasukuni Shrine, saying the actions were a provocation against the postwar international order, according to a Saturday statement from the Chinese Embassy in Japan.
