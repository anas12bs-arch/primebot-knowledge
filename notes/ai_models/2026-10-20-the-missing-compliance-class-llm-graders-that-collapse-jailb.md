# The Missing Compliance Class: LLM Graders That Collapse Jailbreak Success, and a Free Check

Jailbreak papers report an attack success rate, the share of responses that deliver the harmful request, and read it as a property of the target model. We hold the target model, the prompts and the responses fixed and change only the grader. Three dense graders of 8B and 9B parameters, from three model families, move compliant responses to the neighbouring class, evasion, instead of scattering them across classes.
