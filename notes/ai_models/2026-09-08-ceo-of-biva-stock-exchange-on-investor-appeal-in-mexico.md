# CEO of BIVA Stock Exchange on Investor Appeal in Mexico

Maria Ariza, CEO of the BIVA Stock Exchange and an organizer of Mexico Investment Week in New York, says the event is meant to keep investors engaged with Mexico even as trade and geopolitical uncertainty rise. She notes that the annual conversations with investors help explain opportunities across Mexican industries and encourage capital to flow into local markets. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
