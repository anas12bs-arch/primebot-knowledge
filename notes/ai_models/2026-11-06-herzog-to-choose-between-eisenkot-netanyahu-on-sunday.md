# Herzog to Choose Between Eisenkot, Netanyahu on Sunday

Israel’s President Isaac Herzog says he will decide Sunday whether Benjamin Netanyahu or election challenger Gadi Eisenkot will get the first chance to form a governing coalition. Eisenkot has secured recommendations from 67 members of the 120-seat Knesset, compared with 46 for Netanyahu. Bloomberg’s Jerusalem reporter Dan Williams reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
