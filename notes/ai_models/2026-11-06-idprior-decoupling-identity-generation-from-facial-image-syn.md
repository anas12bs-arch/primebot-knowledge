# IDPrior: Decoupling Identity Generation from Facial Image Synthesis

Large-scale identity-labeled face datasets are essential for training modern face recognition models, yet collecting and annotating such data is costly and raises privacy and ethical concerns. Existing synthetic face generation methods typically derive identities from predefined class labels, unconditional image generation, or predefined latent sampling strategies, without explicitly modeling the underlying distribution of facial identities.
