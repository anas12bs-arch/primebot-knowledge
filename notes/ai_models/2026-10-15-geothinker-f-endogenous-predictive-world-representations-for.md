Existing geometry-aware Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) increasingly rely on external 3D encoders to provide structural priors, but executing these additional encoder paths at inference introduces substantial latency and computational overhead. We propose GeoThinker-$F$, an efficient framework that internalizes spatiotemporal perception by transforming causal MLLM hidden states into predictive latent world representations.
