# From 1D Benchmarks to Complex 3D Geometries: Can Agents Reliably Specify Problems for Automated PDE Simulation?

Turning an engineering request into a simulation requires consistent geometry, physics, boundary conditions, and numerical settings. Recent agent-assisted systems automate simulation setup, but successful execution alone does not establish whether the specification preserves the intended problem or correctly handles missing information. We study whether agents can reliably construct complete executable JSON specifications for automated geometry creation, meshing, PDE solving, and result export.
