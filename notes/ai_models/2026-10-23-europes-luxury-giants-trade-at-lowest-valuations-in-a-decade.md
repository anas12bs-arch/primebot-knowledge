# Europe’s Luxury Giants Trade at Lowest Valuations in a Decade

Shares in Europe’s biggest luxury firms trade at the cheapest levels in more than a decade, with results from Hermès International SCA and LVMH Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton SE dashing hopes that an industry slump will end anytime soon.
