# Shared Is Not Enough: Predictor Width and Factor Readability Under Masked Latent Prediction

Masked latent prediction methods such as I-JEPA aim to learn predictable features, but what determines which information they retain is less clear. We built a simulator in which every factor behind an image is known, so a probe can measure how readable each factor remains after training. We trained small encoders (0.7M parameters) with I-JEPA, SimCLR and BYOL on the same pairs of views and probed a factor that both views share.
