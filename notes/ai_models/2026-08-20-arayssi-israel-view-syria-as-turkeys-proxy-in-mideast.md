# Arayssi: Israel View Syria As Turkey’s Proxy in Mideast

Tensions between Turkey and Israel flared after Israel launched airstrikes on a shuttered air base in northern Syria following a recent visit by a Turkish delegation. Dania Arayssi, Program Head and Senior Analyst at the New Lines Institute and Professor at Georgetown University spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on the escalating tensions in Syria.
