# PhysioWave-T: Token-Efficient Wavelet Foundation Models for Biosignals

We present PhysioWave-T, a token-efficient wavelet foundation model family for EEG, ECG, and sEMG. Dynamic scale folding recombines J+1 wavelet subbands prior to tokenization, compressing sequence length from (J+1)CS to CS tokens for J levels, C channels, and S temporal patches. Route-specific front ends and learned channel-name embeddings allow one Transformer per modality to handle diverse montages and sampling rates, while unseen setups reuse the backbone via lightweight adapters.
