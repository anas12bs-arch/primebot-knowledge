# Kenya Parks $1.9 Billion Vodacom-Safaricom Cash in Bonds Pending Court Ruling

Kenya has invested some of the $1.9 billion that Vodacom Group Ltd. paid for a stake in the country’s biggest company in government securities as it awaits the outcome of a court petition opposing the transaction, its treasury secretary said.
