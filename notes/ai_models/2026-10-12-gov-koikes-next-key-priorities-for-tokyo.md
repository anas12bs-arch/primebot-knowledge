# Gov. Koike's Next Key Priorities for Tokyo

Tokyo Governor Yuriko Koike has spent the last decade driving transformative reforms—from pioneering climate initiatives and universal childcare support to guiding the metropolis through the COVID-19 pandemic and the 2020 Olympic Games. Looking to the future, Koike sits down with Bloomberg’s Shery Ahn at Bloomberg's Tokyo Studio to exclusively discuss her key strategic priorities. (Source: Bloomberg)
