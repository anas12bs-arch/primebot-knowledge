# India’s Green Aviation Push Gives Waste Cooking Oil New Life

India is looking to used cooking oil to help fuel its green aviation ambition and reduce reliance on imported crude. But an inefficient and fragmented supply chain could pose a challenge as the country prepares to introduce its Sustainable Aviation Fuel mandate. (Source: Bloomberg)
