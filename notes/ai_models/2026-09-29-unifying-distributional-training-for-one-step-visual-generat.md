# Unifying Distributional Training for One-Step Visual Generation

\emph{Distributional training} provides collective supervision for one-step visual generation by matching real and generated features in frozen representation spaces. We introduce \emph{a unified theoretical framework} that separates distribution modeling from matching discrepancy and connects global objectives to pointwise feature updates through Wasserstein gradient flow.
