# Why Tech’s AI Borrowing Binge Is Rattling Markets

The market for credit-default swaps, financial instruments that function much like insurance policies for Wall Street’s debt trades, rarely attracts attention when everything is going well. But when the cost of insuring a company’s debt starts climbing, more often than not it’s a warning that concerns are building beneath the surface.
