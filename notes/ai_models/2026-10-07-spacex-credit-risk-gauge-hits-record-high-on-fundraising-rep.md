# SpaceX Credit-Risk Gauge Hits Record High on Fundraising Reports

A measure of SpaceX’s credit risk surged to a fresh high and the company’s bonds tumbled in the secondary market on Wednesday, following reports the company is in talks with banks and investors to raise $40 billion to buy chips from Nvidia Corp.
