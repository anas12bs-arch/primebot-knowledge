# Gold Falls as Traders Weigh US Inflation

Gold valuation platform Unvault estimates that American households are sitting on $750 Billion worth of gold, largely in the form of unworn jewelry. The company says consumers consistently underestimate their jewelry's spot value by 3x or more because pieces purchased prior to 2010 have more than tripled in value. CEO Nidhi Singhvi joins Businessweek Daily to discuss how her business is being boosting by higher gold prices in 2026. (Source: Bloomberg)
