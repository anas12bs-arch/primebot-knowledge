# ACEA President & Mercedes CEO 'Welcomes' EU Deal with China on Hybrids

The President of the auto lobby ACEA Ola Källenius, who’s also the CEO of Mercedes-Benz Group, welcomed the recent deal to cap the imports of Chinese hybrid vehicles to the EU. He called for a level-playing field and the EU to increase its competitiveness. He spoke with Bloomberg’s Oliver Crook in Brussels. (Source: Bloomberg)
