# James Danly: US Expanding LNG Exports to Lower Prices

US Department of Energy Deputy Secretary James Danly tells Bloomberg's Haslidna Amin that the US is accelerating LNG export approvals and production to lower energy prices globally. He argues that expanding American energy abundance will offset supply disruptions, even as infrastructure limits remain. (Source: Bloomberg)
