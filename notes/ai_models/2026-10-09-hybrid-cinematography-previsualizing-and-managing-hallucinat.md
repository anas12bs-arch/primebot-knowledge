# Hybrid Cinematography: Previsualizing and Managing Hallucination Risk in Generative Video Reshooting

On a film set, the camera move is committed during a take. Generative video reshooting lets filmmakers change it afterward, but may require hallucinating unrecorded content, a gap sometimes discovered only after leaving the set. We present Hybrid Cinematography, a workflow that bridges physical capture and generative reshooting to manage hallucination risk while filmmakers can still act on it.
