# People Trying Hard To 'Outfox' S&P 500: Cutright

Brooks Cutright, portfolio manager at Hedgeye Asset Management, joins Scarlet Fu and Eric Balchunas on "Bloomberg ETF IQ." Index tracking funds have nearly $27 trillion tied to the S&amp;P 500. This Friday the S&amp;P 500 is set to announce who may be added or taken out of the index as part of a rebalancing. Thirty-six companies appear to qualify for entry, while more than 110 existing members no longer meet the index's market-cap hurdle.
