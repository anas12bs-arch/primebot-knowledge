# NBA Suspends Clippers Owner Ballmer After Leonard Salary Probe

The NBA stripped the Los Angeles Clippers of five first-round draft picks and suspended owner Steve Ballmer for one year after finding the team had circumvented salary cap rules by facilitating sponsorship agreements between outside companies and star player Kawhi Leonard. Randall Williams reports on "Bloomberg The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
