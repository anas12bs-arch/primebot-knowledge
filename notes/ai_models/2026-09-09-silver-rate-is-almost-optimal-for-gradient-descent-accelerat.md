# Silver Rate Is (Almost) Optimal for Gradient Descent Acceleration

We study how far gradient descent (GD) can be accelerated by predetermined nonnegative stepsizes in smooth convex optimization. Writing $p_{\mathrm{sil}}=\log_2(1+\sqrt{2})$, we prove an $Ω\left(n^{-p_{\mathrm{sil}}-O(\sqrt{\log\log n/\log n})}\right)$ non-anytime lower bound. In the anytime setting, every infinite nonnegative schedule has infinitely many horizons with error $Ω\left(n^{-\frac{2p_{\mathrm{sil}}}{1+p_{\mathrm{sil}}}-O(\sqrt{\log\log n/\log n})}\right)$.
