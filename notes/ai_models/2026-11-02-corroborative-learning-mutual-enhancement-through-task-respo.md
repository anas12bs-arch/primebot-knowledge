# Corroborative Learning: Mutual Enhancement through Task-Response Reconstruction

In pattern analysis, models trained independently for the same task often develop complementary capabilities through differences in architecture, data, and optimization. Exploiting complementarity while retaining independent deployment is central to collaborative learning. Most methods establish transfer by reducing differences in predictions, relations, or representations between target and collaborator, although these differences also underlie complementarity.
