# Virginia Wants 6-Year Rate Freeze in NextEra-Dominion Deal

Bloomberg's Mark Chediak joins Dani Burger on "Bloomberg Deals." Virginia Governor Abigail Spanberger won’t support NextEra Energy’s $67 billion Dominion Energy takeover without a six-year freeze on residential power rates, raising the bar for a utility mega-deal that’s facing increasing skepticism.
