# Dollar Debasement Is Favoring Singapore’s Currency, Analysts Say

The Singapore dollar is tracking the US currency in an inverse direction more closely than any other in Asia, which analysts say positions it for stronger gains in the region if the “dollar debasement trade” gathers pace.
