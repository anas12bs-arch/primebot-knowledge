# Indonesia Bond Yield Hits Four-Year High as MSCI Risk, Oil Weigh

Indonesia’s 10-year government bond yield climbed to a four-year high and the rupiah weakened toward a record low as elevated oil prices and MSCI Inc.’s threat of a stocks downgrade hurt investor sentiment.
