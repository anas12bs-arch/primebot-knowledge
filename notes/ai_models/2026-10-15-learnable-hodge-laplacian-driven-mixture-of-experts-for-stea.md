# Learnable Hodge-Laplacian Driven Mixture of Experts for Steady-PDE Surrogates on Coarse Unstructured Meshes

Current approaches for steady partial differential equations surrogate models on unstructured meshes apply a single mixing mechanism to the whole solution. This paper proposes an architecture that partitions the solution among vanilla transformer experts, each focused on a different spectral scale. The partition is built from a discrete Hodge Laplacian $L_0=\star_0^{-1} d_0^{\top}\star_1 d_0$, that serves as a fitting layer measuring high-frequency content.
