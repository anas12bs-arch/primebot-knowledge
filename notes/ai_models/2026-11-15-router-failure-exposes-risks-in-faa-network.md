# Router Failure Exposes Risks in FAA Network

Former TSA Administrator and FBI Deputy Director John Pistole is on Bloomberg This Weekend to discuss the nationwide FAA ground stop and the vulnerabilities exposed by a router failure during work on the agency’s aging telecommunications network. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Pistole examines the need for greater redundancy, questions surrounding contractor L3Harris and concerns about whether the system can withstand another disruption as Thanksgiving travel approaches.
