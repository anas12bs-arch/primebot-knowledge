# Robust and Efficient Continual Model Merging via Global Singular Subspace Separation and Restoration

Continual model merging incrementally integrates task-specific models without revisiting past models or data. However, repeated merging entangles past task components and distorts their singular geometry, making balanced task-wise allocation increasingly difficult. We propose Global Singular Subspace Separation and Restoration (GS3R), which augments the merged update with a compact restoration state.
