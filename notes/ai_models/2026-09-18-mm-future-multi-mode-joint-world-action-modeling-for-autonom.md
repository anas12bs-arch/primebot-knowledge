# MM-Future: Multi-Mode Joint World-Action Modeling for Autonomous Driving

Autonomous driving involves coupled decision-making and scene evolution under multi-mode uncertainty. To capture this coupling and uncertainty, we introduce MM-Future, a world-action model that generates multiple paired scene-action hypotheses and models bidirectional interaction within each pair. Each hypothesis is initialized from a structured action prior and an independent future scene source, which are then co-evolved through a modality-aware diffusion Transformer.
