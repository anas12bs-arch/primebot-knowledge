# Smart: Lot of Issues Off the Table in US-China Talks

US & China have agreed to extend their trade-war truce by two months. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced the deal as Chinese President Xi Jinping begins his first state visit to Washington in 11 years. Christopher Smart, founder and managing partner of the Arbroath Group spoke to Bloomberg’s Jennifer Zabasajja on Horizons Middle East & Africa about the significance of the visit.
