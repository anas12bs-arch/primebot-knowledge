# Optimal Low-Rank Quantum State Tomography with Bounded-Sample Joint Measurements

We determine the optimal sample complexity of low-rank quantum state tomography when each measurement may act jointly on at most $t$ samples. For sufficiently small $\varepsilon$, estimating an unknown state on $\mathbb{C}^d$ of rank at most $r$ to trace norm error $\varepsilon$ with constant success probability requires, and is achievable with, $$   Θ\left(   \frac{dr}{\varepsilon^2}   \max\left\{1,\frac r{\sqrt t}\right\}   \right)$$ samples.
