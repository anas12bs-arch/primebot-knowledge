# Why High Bond Yields Aren’t Sinking Stocks

The 10-year Treasury yield, the most important number in global finance, has broken through to its highest level since 2002. Bond yields are assumed to have an inverse relationship with stocks, so a move of this magnitude should be a problem for equities. But it hasn’t been, and Bloomberg Opinion columnist John Authers explains why. (Source: Bloomberg)
