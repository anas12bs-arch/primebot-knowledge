# Humanoids Market Worth $200BN By 2035: Barclays' Todorova

The market for AI-powered robots and autonomous machines has the potential to balloon into a trillion-dollar opportunity by 2035 in the 'decade of the robot', according to Barclays analyst Zornitsa Todorova.
AI-enabled robotics mark a frontier moment in the technology and present a new set of investment opportunities in a world of labor shortages and changing worker preferences.
She spoke to Tom Mackenzie on the latest episode of Bloomberg Tech: Europe. (Source: Bloomberg)
