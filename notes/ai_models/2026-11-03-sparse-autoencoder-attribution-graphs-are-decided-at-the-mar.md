# Sparse Autoencoder Attribution Graphs Are Decided at the Margin

Attribution graphs over the features of sparse autoencoders (SAEs) are used to predict what an ablation does and are compared across training seeds. A TopK SAE keeps the largest preactivations at each token, and a feature's margin is its preactivation's distance to the cutoff. On five public TopK SAE pairs from three Pythia models, zeroing one feature moves a next layer feature across the cutoff in 0.74 of cases or more, and the crossings carry most of the response's energy.
