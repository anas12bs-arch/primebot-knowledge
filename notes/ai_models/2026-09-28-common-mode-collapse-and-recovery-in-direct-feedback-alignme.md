# Common-Mode Collapse and Recovery in Direct Feedback Alignment

Direct feedback alignment (DFA) trains hidden layers through fixed random projections of output error. With tanh hidden units and independent sigmoid outputs, plain stochastic gradient descent can stall near the loss of a constant predictor of class frequencies. We trace this stall to the error's common mode, the component shared across inputs.
