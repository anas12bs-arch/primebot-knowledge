# Charles Elson on Nvidia’s Corporate Governance Structure

Charles Elson, founding director of the University of Delaware’s Weinberg Center for Corporate Governance, says Nvidia’s board should have had a clearer succession plan and stronger oversight in place. He speaks with Romaine Bostick and Jess Menton on “The Close.” (Source: Bloomberg)
