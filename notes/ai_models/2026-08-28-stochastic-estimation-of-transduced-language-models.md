# Stochastic Estimation of Transduced Language Models

Transduced language models (TLMs) compose a pretrained \emph{source} language model with a functional finite-state transducer to induce a language model over \emph{target} strings. Computing the probability of a target prefix under a TLM amounts to summing the source-model probabilities of all source strings that the transducer maps to target strings beginning with that prefix.
