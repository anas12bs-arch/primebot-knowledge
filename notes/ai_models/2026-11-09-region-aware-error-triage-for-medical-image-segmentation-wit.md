# Region-aware error triage for medical image segmentation with groupwise conformal risk control

Segmentation masks are increasingly reviewed rather than redrawn, which requires knowing where a mask is likely wrong. Learned error maps rank pixels by risk, but the number of pixels to warn is chosen ad hoc, and calibrating one warning threshold over all error pixels may leave errors far from the predicted contour under-flagged.
