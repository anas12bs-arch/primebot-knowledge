# JPMorgan AM: We're Getting More Constructive on Duration

Kim Crawford, global fixed income portfolio manager at JPMorgan Asset Management, discusses the outlook for French bond yields and why her firm is getting "more constructive" on longer-term debt. "Bonds typically sell off until something breaks, and something has broken," Crawford tells Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
