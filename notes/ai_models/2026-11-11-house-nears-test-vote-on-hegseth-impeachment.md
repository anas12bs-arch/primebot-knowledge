# House Nears Test Vote on Hegseth Impeachment

The House is set to vote Tuesday evening on whether to advance an effort to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, who cast the move as a politicized attack on US troops and lawful military operations ordered by President Donald Trump.  Wendy Benjaminson, Bloomberg News National Security Team Leader discusses what's next for the  Defense Secretary. (Source: Bloomberg)
