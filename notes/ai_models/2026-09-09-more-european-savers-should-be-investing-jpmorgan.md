# More European Savers Should Be Investing: JPMorgan

Savers across Europe and the UK could be getting a better return on their money if they put more of their cash savings into investment accounts says Patrick Thomson, EMEA CEO of JPMorgan Asset Management. This is particularly important given Europe has an ageing population, so citizens will need pensions that last for longer. Thomson spoke to Bloomberg's Francine Lacqua on 'The Pulse'.
