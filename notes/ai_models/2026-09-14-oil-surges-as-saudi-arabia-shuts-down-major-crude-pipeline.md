# Oil Surges as Saudi Arabia Shuts Down Major Crude Pipeline

Oil surged after Saudi Arabia closed a major crude pipeline that has been used to bypass the Strait of Hormuz during the US-Iran war. Saudi says the pipeline has come under attack and was closed as a precaution. Rob Barnett, Bloomberg Intelligence senior energy analyst, says this is "effectively a three-alarm fire" in the oil space. He's on Bloomberg Radio. (Source: Bloomberg)
