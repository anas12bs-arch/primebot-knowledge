# When Do Histology Foundation Models Need Geometric Test-Time Augmentation?

Test-time augmentation (TTA) aggregates model predictions across multiple transformed views of an input during inference. Because histology images lack a canonical orientation, $D_4$ TTA---pooling across the 8 rotations and reflections of a square---is a standard default in computational pathology. However, the rise of self-supervised histology foundation models (FMs) with inherent rotational invariance calls its continued utility into question.
