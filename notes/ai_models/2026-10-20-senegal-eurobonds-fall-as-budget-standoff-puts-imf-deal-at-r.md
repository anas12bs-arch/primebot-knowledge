# Senegal Eurobonds Fall as Budget Standoff Puts IMF Deal at Risk

Senegal’s dollar bonds weakened after opposition leader Ousmane Sonko threatened to block a revised budget for this year and 2027 spending plan, raising concern this could complicate its debt restructuring and delay a new International Monetary Fund program.
