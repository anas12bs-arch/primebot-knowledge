# TabNSM: Neural Sparse Mixer for Tabular Regression

Large-scale, high-dimensional tabular regression remains challenging: tree-based models are robust but lack end-to-end representation learning, while deep models enable flexible feature learning but often incur costly interaction modeling and sensitivity to noisy or redundant features. We propose TabNSM, a scalable regression framework that extends our earlier sparse-attention and mixer architectures.
