# Restoring Diversity in Distilled Image and Video Generators by Probing Activations

Distilling diffusion models into few-step student models substantially reduces sampling latency, but induces severe diversity collapse: samples generated from different random seeds show minimal variation. In this work, we investigate the root cause of this failure by probing the internal representations of student models relative to their teachers.
