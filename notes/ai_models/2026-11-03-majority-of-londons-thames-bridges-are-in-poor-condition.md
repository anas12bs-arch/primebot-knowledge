# Majority of London’s Thames Bridges Are in Poor Condition

Over half of London’s bridges across the River Thames are in ‘poor’ or ‘very poor’ condition, the worst possible ratings, with the crossing in Chelsea deteriorating after heavy vehicles were recently diverted from a nearby crossing.
