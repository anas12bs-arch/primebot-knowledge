# ScaleWorld: High-Resolution World Generation through Shared Prediction and Refinement

High-resolution generation enables video world models to render fine-grained scene structures and textures, but directly generating high-resolution video incurs substantial computational and memory costs. We present ScaleWorld, a causal video model that unifies low-resolution world prediction and high-resolution refinement within a single shared backbone.
