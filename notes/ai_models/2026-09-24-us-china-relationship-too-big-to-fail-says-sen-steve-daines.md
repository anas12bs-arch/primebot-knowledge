# US, China Relationship 'Too Big to Fail,' Says Sen. Steve Daines

Republican Senator from Montana Steve Daines said that he views the relationship between the US and China as 'too big to fail,' and says that he is appreciative of the President Trump's respectful approach to the relationship as conversations between the two leaders take place behind closed doors in Washington. Daines also discussed AI regulation and said he wants the US to have thoughtful, but fast paced AI governance.  (Source: Bloomberg)
