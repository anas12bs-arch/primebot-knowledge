# Taiwan Drafts Plans for Wealth Fund, Aims for Bill by Year-End

Taiwan is drafting legislation to establish a new sovereign wealth fund with initial capital of about NT$30 billion ($912 million), pushing ahead with the government’s plan to make strategic investments overseas.
