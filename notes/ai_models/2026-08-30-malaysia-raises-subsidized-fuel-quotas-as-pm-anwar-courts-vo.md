# Malaysia Raises Subsidized Fuel Quotas as PM Anwar Courts Voters

Malaysia will restore subsidized fuel quotas to previous levels despite elevated energy prices, as Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim seeks to ease cost-of-living pressures and shore up support after recent electoral setbacks.
