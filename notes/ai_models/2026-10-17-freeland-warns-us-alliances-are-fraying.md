# Freeland Warns US Alliances Are Fraying

Former Canadian Deputy Prime Minister Chrystia Freeland tells Bloomberg This Weekend that Canada and other democracies must become less reliant on the US while remaining open to rebuilding the Western alliance. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Freeland discusses Alberta’s separatist movement, President Donald Trump’s approach to Russia and Ukraine, and lessons from her negotiations with the US that inform her new book, Unreliable Boyfriend.
