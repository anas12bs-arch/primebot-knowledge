# State Transport Routing for Short-horizon Adaptation in Multi-horizon Photovoltaic Forecasting

Recent power measurements provide valuable information for photovoltaic(PV) power forecasting, but directly extrapolating short-term trends can introduce substantial errors over longer forecast horizons. To address this challenge, we propose state transport routing (STR), a lightweight adapter that refines the predictions of a frozen forecasting model.
