# Persistent Oil Prices Threaten Inflation Outlook

Seaport Research Partners Chief Equity Strategist Jonathan Golub tells Bloomberg This Weekend that stocks are not in an AI bubble, arguing that strong earnings and lower valuations show investors remain skeptical even as AI drives much of the market. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Golub says a prolonged Middle East conflict could keep oil and inflation elevated, eventually forcing the Federal Reserve to respond as strains emerge in credit markets.
