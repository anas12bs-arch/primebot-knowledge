# Vietnam’s Vingroup Plans Debut Won Bond After Shares Hit Record

Vietnamese conglomerate Vingroup JSC is in discussions with brokerages about selling its first bond denominated in the Korean won, according to people familiar with the matter, as the company seeks to broaden its investor base after its shares hit a record.
