# Yahua’s Zimbabwe Lithium-Sulfate Plant to Come Online Next Year

China’s Sichuan Yahua Industrial Group Co. plans to start operating a $200 million lithium-sulfate plant in Zimbabwe in the second quarter of next year amid a push by the southern African country to reap more value from its natural resources.
