# Shenkman, Blackstone, GDA Among Lenders to Take Over Vivid

A group of lenders including Blackstone Inc. and Shenkman Capital Management are set to take over Vivid Seats Inc. in a deal that would reduce the ticket reseller’s net debt by more than $200 million, according to people familiar with the matter.
