# Vietnam Picks French-Korean Group for High-Speed Rail Deal

Vietnam has selected a consortium of eight French, South Korean and Vietnamese contractors for a 10.24 trillion dong ($397 million) contract for surveys, feasibility study and design for the North-South high-speed railway, one of the country’s flagship infrastructure projects.
