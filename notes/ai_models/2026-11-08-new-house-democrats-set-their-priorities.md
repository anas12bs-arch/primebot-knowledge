# New House Democrats Set Their Priorities

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Democratic Reps.-elect Paul Barringer of North Carolina and Shannon Taylor of Virginia say voters sent them to Washington with a mandate to address affordability, health care and government accountability. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, they discuss working across the aisle, restoring congressional authority and responding to concerns about tariffs, data centers and the cost of living.
