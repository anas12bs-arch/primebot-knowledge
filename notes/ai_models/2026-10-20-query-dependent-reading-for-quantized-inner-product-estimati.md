# Query-Dependent Reading for Quantized Inner-Product Estimation: A Minimax Separation

We study quantization for inner-product estimation when storage and reading have separate budgets. In the static bit-probe model [Yao, 1981], an encoder stores $w$ bits about $x$ before seeing a query $y$, and a reader probes at most $r<w$ bits to estimate $x^\top y$. Both vectors lie in the $d$-dimensional Euclidean unit ball. Protocols may be randomized, and computation is unrestricted.
