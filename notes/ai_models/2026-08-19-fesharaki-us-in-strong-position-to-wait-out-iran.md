# Fesharaki: US in 'Strong Position' to Wait Out Iran

FGE NexantECA Chairman Emeritus Fereidun Fesharaki tells Bloomberg TV that Iran can only survive a few more months of curtailed income due to the Strait of Hormuz conflict. He adds that the US may consider waiting out the regime for a better deal. (Source: Bloomberg)
