# Rising Fuel Flows Through Hormuz May Help Ease Strained Market

The volume of refined products being loaded within the Persian Gulf is rising, with most of that exiting the Strait of Hormuz and providing some relief to one of the tightest corners of the global oil market nine months into the Iran war.
