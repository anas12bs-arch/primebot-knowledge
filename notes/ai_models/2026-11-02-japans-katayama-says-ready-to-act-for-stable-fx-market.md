# Japan’s Katayama Says Ready to Act for Stable FX Market

Japanese authorities are prepared to respond if currency market movements become disorderly, acting in accordance with a standing pact with her US counterpart, Finance Minister Satsuki Katayama said, one day before the US holds its midterm elections.
