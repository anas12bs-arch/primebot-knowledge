# Jera Sees LNG Spot Prices Rising Ahead of Winter

Yukio Kani, Chairman and Global Chief Executive Officer of JERA, said spot liquefied natural gas prices are likely to continue rising due to low inventories in Europe and supply disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz. He spoke exclusively to Bloomberg's Haslinda Amin on the sidelines of Gastech 2026. (Source: Bloomberg)
