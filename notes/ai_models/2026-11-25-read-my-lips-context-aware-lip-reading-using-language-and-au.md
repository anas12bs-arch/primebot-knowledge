# Read My Lips: Context-aware Lip Reading Using Language and Audio-Visual Models

Real-world speech is often partially observable: audio may be missing or corrupted by noise and competing speakers, while the speaker's lips remain visible. Recovering speech requires combining what can be heard, seen, and inferred from context. We introduce RML (Read My Lips), a collaborative method combining a lip reader, a language model, and an audio-visual generative model. The lip reader provides articulation hypotheses, the language model uses context to resolve ambiguous or missing words
