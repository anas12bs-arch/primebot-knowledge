# Japan's Go President Eyes Expansion in Asia, Robotaxis

Go, the taxi booking app operator that pulled off Japan’s biggest initial public offering this year, is eyeing ways to expand domestically and across Asia, and doesn't see competitors like Uber as a threat. Go President Hiroshi Nakajima spoke exclusively with Bloomberg's Shery Ahn about Japan's unique ride-hailing market and future opportunities like robotaxis. (Source: Bloomberg)
