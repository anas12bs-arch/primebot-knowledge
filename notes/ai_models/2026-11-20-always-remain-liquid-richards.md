Bruce Richards, managing partner and head of CVC Marathon, joins Scarlet Fu and Tom Keene on “Bloomberg Money.” They discuss Richards’ favorite investing advice he has received throughout his career. Richards warns about irrational markets and also says he’s not a fan of leveraged ETF structures. (Source: Bloomberg)
