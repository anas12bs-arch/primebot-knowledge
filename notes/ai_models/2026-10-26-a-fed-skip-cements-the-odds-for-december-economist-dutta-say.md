# A Fed Skip Cements the Odds for December, Economist Dutta Says

“From a risk-management perspective, it probably makes sense to raise rates,” says Neil Dutta, head of US economic research at Renaissance Macro Research, as markets anticipate the Federal Reserve to hold interest rates steady at this week’s meeting. He speaks on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
