# Adaptive Quantization Preserves Fast Rates for TD Learning over Rate-Limited Channels

Motivated by reinforcement learning subject to communication constraints, we study a policy evaluation problem with linear function approximation over a rate-limited channel. In our setup, an agent interacting with an unknown environment (modeled as a Markov Decision Process) transmits quantized estimates of a temporal-difference (TD) learning update direction to a server over a finite-capacity channel.
