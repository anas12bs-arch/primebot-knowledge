# US Economy Can Keep Growing Despite Higher Rates, Says House

Wells Fargo’s Sarah House says Chair Kevin Warsh may use Jackson Hole to focus on longer-term questions facing the Federal Reserve rather than signal the next move on interest rates. She also discusses alternative economic data, the Fed balance sheet, Treasury yields and why the U.S. economy continues to show resilience. House speaks with Bloomberg's Tom Keene and Paul Sweeney on Surveillance Radio. (Source: Bloomberg)
