# AI's Impact on Job-Loss is 'Quite Limited' Right Now, Says Dr. Homkes

Jobless claims continue to suggest economy-wide layoffs remain low. 
Even as companies increasingly use AI and automation to operate with fewer workers, it hasn't translated into a broad pickup in layoffs.
Rebecca Homkes, Lecturer at London Business School, discusses the AI trade and how buildout has impacted jobs and productivity. (Source: Bloomberg)
