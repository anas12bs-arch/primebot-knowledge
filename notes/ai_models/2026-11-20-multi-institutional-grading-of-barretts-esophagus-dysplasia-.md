# Multi-institutional grading of Barrett's esophagus dysplasia without stain normalization using a pathology foundation model and multiple instance learning

Barrett's esophagus dysplasia grading suffers substantial inter-observer variability, particularly for low-grade dysplasia, complicating clinical decision-making. We conducted a retrospective multi-institutional diagnostic accuracy study of a foundation model-based deep learning system for dysplasia grading, using 969 H\&E-stained whole slide images from four institutions.
