# Starbucks CEO Is Focused on Growth Plan, Fundamentals

Starbucks Corp. Chief Executive Officer Brian Niccol expects earnings growth to continue and he says the turnaround plan is ahead of schedule. He says becoming a multi-brand company is a possibility. The chain recently said it was not exploring a takeover of Chipotle. He speaks to Bloomberg's Romaine Bostick. (Source: Bloomberg)
