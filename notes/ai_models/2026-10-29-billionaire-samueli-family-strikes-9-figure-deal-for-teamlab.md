# Billionaire Samueli Family Strikes 9-Figure Deal for TeamLab Museum in Anaheim

The Samueli Family, owners of the National Hockey League’s Anaheim Ducks, struck a more than $100 million deal with the international digital art collective TeamLab to open its first permanent location in the US.
