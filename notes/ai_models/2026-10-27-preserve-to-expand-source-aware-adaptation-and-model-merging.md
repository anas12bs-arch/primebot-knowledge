# Preserve to Expand: Source-Aware Adaptation and Model Merging for Multilingual LLM-Based ASR

Expanding multilingual ASR systems to new languages is challenging when adaptation must preserve previously supported languages without replaying source-language training data. We study this problem in connector-based Speech LLMs, where a trainable connector maps speech representations into a frozen large language model.
