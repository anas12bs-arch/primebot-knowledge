# Guggenheim's Walsh Says Accounting Was 'Appropriate' at GPI Unit

Guggenheim Partners is working with regulators as they probe a business accounting issue at one of its subsidiaries, according to Anne Walsh, chief investment officer of the firm’s asset-management business. Walsh says the firm is cooperating with regulators. She speaks on "Bloomberg Surveillance." (Source: Bloomberg)
