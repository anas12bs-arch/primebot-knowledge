# Citi’s Manthey Sees French Equities Volatile Beyond April Vote

France’s political risk won’t end with April’s presidential election, according to Citigroup Inc.’s Beata Manthey, who says the bigger test for markets is whether any government can command a parliamentary majority and deliver a credible path to lower debt.
