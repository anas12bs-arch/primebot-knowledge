# Nuveen's Cooper Sees Yield Risks Tilted to Upside

Laura Cooper, Nuveen's global investment strategist and macro credit head, discusses yields, the inflation situation and energy markets. "I think the risks is tilted to further upside," Cooper tells Bloomberg Television. She adds inflation expectations could have a "knock-on negative impact" on risk assets.
