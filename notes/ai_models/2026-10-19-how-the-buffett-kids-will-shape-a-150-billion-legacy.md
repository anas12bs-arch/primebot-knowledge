# How the Buffett Kids Will Shape a $150 Billion Legacy

Warren Buffett, chairman emeritus of Berkshire Hathaway, is worth $150 billion, according to the Bloomberg Billionaires Index. Until recently, a longtime favorite of his, Bill Gates, was taking care of giving most of the money away through his behemoth global foundation. Then came the revelations about Gates’ relationship with Jeffrey Epstein. Now, it's Buffett's three children that will inherit the task of distributing his enormous wealth.
