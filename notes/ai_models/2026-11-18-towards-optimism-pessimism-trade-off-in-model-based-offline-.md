# Towards Optimism-Pessimism Trade-off in Model-based Offline-to-Online Reinforcement Learning

Model-based offline-to-online reinforcement learning (RL) enables sample-efficient adaptation by leveraging offline pre-training for online fine-tuning. However, the distribution shifts between offline and online stages often hinder fine-tuning performance. Many existing methods encode the optimism-pessimism trade-off through a single-objective formulation.
