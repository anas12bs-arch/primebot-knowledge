# How Well Does AI Peer Review Work? A Benchmark Built from 100 Planted Errors

One hundred known errors were planted into 10 open-access psychology papers, and the modified papers were then run through frontier models and two commercial AI review tools. The best single system caught 71 of the 100 errors, while the worst caught 30. Pooling every system's output caught 93. Models are only partly correlated in the errors they find, which makes ensembling a big lever for finding issues in papers.
