# Angola Seeks to Raise $228 Million From 34% Standard Bank (SBK) Unit Stake

Angola plans to raise as much as 208.5 billion kwanzas ($228 million) from the sale of a 34% stake in Standard Bank de Angola SA previously held by a jailed former insurance tycoon.
