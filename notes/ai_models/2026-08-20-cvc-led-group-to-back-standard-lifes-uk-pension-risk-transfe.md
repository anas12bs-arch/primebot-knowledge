# CVC-Led Group to Back Standard Life’s UK Pension Risk Transfers

Standard Life Plc agreed to set up a partnership with a consortium led by CVC Capital Partners Plc and Prudential Financial Inc. in the latest tie-up between an insurer and private capital firms looking to enter the country’s booming pension risk-transfer market.
