# Container Shipping Companies Navigate Route Challenges

Lee Klaskow, Senior Logistics Analyst at Bloomberg Intelligence, discussed how container shipping companies are managing disruptions caused by route restrictions and geopolitical tensions. Despite only about a third of typical Suez Canal routes currently in use, companies remain confident that full utilization will resume over time, with alternative routes around Africa available if needed.
