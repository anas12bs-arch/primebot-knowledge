# Canadian Natural to Buy Advantage Energy Amid Oil Price Boom

Canadian Natural Resources agreed to acquire Advantage Energy Ltd. for C$2.255 billion ($1.6 billion), adding to a string of deals in the country’s oil and gas industry as conflicts in the Middle East and Ukraine boost demand for alternative supplies.
