# Next 20 Days Is ‘Mission Critical’ for Retailers: Yih

Adrienne Yih, Barclays Managing Director and Senior Retail Analyst, says consumer spending is holding up despite shoppers’ concerns about high prices. She argues that household income and purchasing power have adjusted alongside inflation and wage growth, helping explain continued spending. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close."
