# China’s Benchmark Crude Oil Futures Have Never Been So Expensive

Yuan-priced crude futures in China rallied to the highest level on record, as the shutdown of an oil pipeline in Saudi Arabia hurt global supplies and refiners in the largest importer bought more cargoes.
