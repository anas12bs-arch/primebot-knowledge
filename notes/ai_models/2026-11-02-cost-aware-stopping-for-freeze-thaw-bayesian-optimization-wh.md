# Cost-Aware Stopping for Freeze-Thaw Bayesian Optimization: When Does One-Step Lookahead Suffice?

Freeze-thaw Bayesian optimization (BO) evaluates candidates in resumable increments but usually stops at a budget or convergence test. We maximize a budget-free utility, the best observed score minus a cost per increment, with a one-step lookahead: advance the candidate with the largest expected improvement (EI) under a fixed surrogate, and stop once no EI exceeds the cost.
