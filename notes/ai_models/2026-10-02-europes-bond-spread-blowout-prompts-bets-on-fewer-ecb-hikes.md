# Europe’s Bond-Spread Blowout Prompts Bets on Fewer ECB Hikes

A surge in euro-area sovereign bond spreads slowed on Friday as traders bet the European Central Bank will be forced to support the region’s more-indebted members by delivering fewer interest-rate hikes.
