# Kazakhstan Encouraged Local Banks to Finance Deal for Gold Miner

Kazakhstan has encouraged domestic banks to lend more than $1 billion to construction tycoon Shakhmurat Mutalip and revised financing regulations to help him buy gold miner AK Altynalmas JSC, according to people familiar with the matter.
