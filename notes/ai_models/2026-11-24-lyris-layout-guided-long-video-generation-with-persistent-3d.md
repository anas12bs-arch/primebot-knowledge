# Lyris: Layout-Guided Long Video Generation with Persistent 3D Memory

Exploring a scene from as little as a single photo requires generating a long, camera-controlled walkthrough that extends far beyond what was captured, yet remains consistent upon return. Memory-based video generation methods preserve what they have already seen, but have no anchor beyond it, where the camera drifts from its path and a text prompt can name objects but not place them. Layout-conditioned methods provide such an anchor and precise control over what appears where, but rely on layout
