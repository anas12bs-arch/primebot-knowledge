# Odd Lots: Weber’s Vision for an Affordability Agenda (Podcast)

The most important word in politics right now is affordability. It was a winning issue in the midterms for Democrats, and one of the reasons why people across Europe are out in the streets protesting: Food, housing, and energy are getting more expensive by the day. During the height of the pandemic, we talked a lot about inflation with Isabella Weber, and her on what governments can do to make life more affordable has evolved into a book called Anti-Fascist Economics: In Defense of Affordability
