# South Africa Antitrust Body Seeks to Revoke Premier-RFG Deal

South Africa’s antitrust commission moved to revoke the approval of a merger between two of the nation’s biggest packaged-food producers, saying they withheld key information about post-deal plans to shutter a fruit-canning plant.
