# CFTC Clears Faster Payouts for Tipster Awards Under $5 Million

The Commodity Futures Trading Commission plans to automatically award successful whistleblowers 30% of any enforcement penalties collected if a tipster is due to receive $5 million or less, the agency said Friday.
