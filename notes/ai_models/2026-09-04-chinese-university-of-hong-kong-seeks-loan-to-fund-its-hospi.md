# Chinese University of Hong Kong Seeks Loan to Fund Its Hospital

Chinese University of Hong Kong is seeking a bank loan of around HK$4 billion ($510 million) to refinance a facility supporting its private hospital, according to people familiar with the matter, as local universities increasingly turn to debt financing for major projects.
