# AirAsia Plunges To Four-Year Low on Route Absorption Report

AirAsia Group Bhd shares plunged as much as 21% to the lowest in almost four years, after reports Malaysia’s government had asked other local airlines to potentially absorb the cash-strapped carrier’s domestic market share.
