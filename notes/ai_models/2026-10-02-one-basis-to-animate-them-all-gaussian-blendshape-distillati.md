# One Basis to Animate Them All: Gaussian Blendshape Distillation for Real-Time Avatars

3D Gaussian avatars support fast rendering, however, their real-time animation is often challenged by the costly neural inference. We address this bottleneck and show that the animation of pretrained avatar models can be closely approximated by a linear combination of identity-independent blendshapes.
