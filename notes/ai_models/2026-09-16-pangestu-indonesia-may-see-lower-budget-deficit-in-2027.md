# Pangestu: Indonesia May See Lower Budget Deficit in 2027

Indonesian National Economic Council Vice Chair Mari Pangestu believes the country may see a lower budget deficit next year. She also expects fiscal discipline from new Finance Minister Suahasil Nazara. She speaks with Paul Allen on "Insight with Haslinda Amin." (Source: Bloomberg)
