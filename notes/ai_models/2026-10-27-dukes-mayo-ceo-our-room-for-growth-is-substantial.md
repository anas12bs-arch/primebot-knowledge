# Duke’s Mayo CEO: Our Room for Growth Is Substantial

E. Yuri Hermida, CEO of Duke’s Mayo Company, discussed the company’s name change from Sauer Brands as it enters a new growth chapter. After divesting its spice business, the company is centering its identity on Duke’s Mayo, whose heritage and recipe date to 1917, while also owning Mateo’s Gourmet Salsa. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
