# Satellite Snapshot Points to Increase in Saudis’ Gulf Oil Flows

Saudi Arabia’s observed oil loadings from inside the Persian Gulf jumped over the weekend, the clearest sign yet of how the kingdom has shifted its exports back toward the Strait of Hormuz following the shutdown of a key cross-country pipeline.
