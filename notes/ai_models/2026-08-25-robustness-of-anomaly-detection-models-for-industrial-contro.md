# Robustness of Anomaly Detection Models for Industrial Control Systems under Training-Time Data Contamination

Machine-learning-based anomaly detection is increasingly used in industrial control systems (ICS), yet most studies assume that detector training data is trustworthy. In practice, training data may be corrupted through compromised logs, labeling errors, manipulated historian records, or unsafe retraining processes.
