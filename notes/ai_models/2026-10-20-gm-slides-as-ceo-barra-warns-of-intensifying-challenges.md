# GM Slides as CEO Barra Warns of ‘Intensifying’ Challenges

General Motors Co. shares fell as shareholders focused on a narrower profit outlook despite plans for another $20 billion in stock repurchases. The company now sees 2026 adjusted earnings before interest and taxes in a range of $14.5 billion to $15.5 billion, compared with the $14 billion to $16 billion range it projected in July. Bloomberg's Craig Trudell joins to discuss. (Source: Bloomberg)
