# Special Bonus Pool for Goldman Executives to Top $500 Million

Goldman Sachs executives including CEO David Solomon and President John Waldron are set to take in a special bonus. Equity awards could top $500 million based on the current share price. Solomon could get $100 million alone. Bloomberg's Sridhar Natarajan reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
