Leonardo SpA, the Italian state-controlled defense company, is aiming to deepen ties with Ukraine’s fast-growing military-technology sector through its new Kyiv unit, seeking partnership, investment or acquisition opportunities, Chief Executive Officer Lorenzo Mariani said.
