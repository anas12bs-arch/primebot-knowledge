# German Pension Reform May Raise €90 Billion a Year, Apollo Says

Germany’s historic pension overhaul could increase annual inflows into the system’s three pillars by €90 billion ($105 billion) annually, according to Apollo Global Management Inc. European Economic &amp; Policy Strategist Huw van Steenis.
