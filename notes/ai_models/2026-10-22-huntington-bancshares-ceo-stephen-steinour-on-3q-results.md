# Huntington Bancshares CEO Stephen Steinour on 3Q Results

Huntington Bank shares were little changed after the company reported revenue below expectations and earnings per share in line. CEO Steve Steinour said the results reinforce confidence in meeting financial targets and delivering sustained earnings growth. He cited growth and the integration of the Cadence and Veritex partnerships, while explaining that stronger competition has made some lending unattractive. He speaks with Romaine Bostick & Jess Menton on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
