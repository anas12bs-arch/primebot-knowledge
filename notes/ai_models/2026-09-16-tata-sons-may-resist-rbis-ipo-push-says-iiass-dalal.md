# Tata Sons May Resist RBI's IPO Push, Says IiAS's Dalal

Hetal Dalal, COO and President of IiAS, a corporate governance and proxy advisory firm, says Tata Sons is expected to "put up a fight" against the RBI's efforts to require a listing. She argues that an IPO could constrain the group's operating flexibility. (Source: Bloomberg)
