# Young Travelers Are Warming Up to Cruises

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg News travel journalist Brittany Chang explains why cruises are attracting more Gen Z and millennial travelers as higher airfare and hotel costs make all-inclusive vacations increasingly appealing. Chang tells hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini that cruise lines are courting younger passengers with mega ships, entertainment, adult-only spaces and nostalgia, with some sailings costing as little as about $30 a day.
