# PrismRestore: A Compact Restoration Toolbox for Agentic Image Restoration

Agentic image restoration composes operations to handle mixed degradations, yet its toolbox often requires storing multiple restoration networks. We propose PrismRestore, which constructs a compact bank of callable restoration actions from a single frozen all-in-one model. Our approach builds on task-dependent yet overlapping channel responses in the shared model.
