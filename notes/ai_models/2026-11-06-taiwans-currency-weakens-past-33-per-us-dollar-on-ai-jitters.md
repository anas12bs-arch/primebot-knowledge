# Taiwan’s Currency Weakens Past 33 Per US Dollar on AI Jitters

The Taiwan dollar weakened past the key psychological level of 33 per US dollar for the first time since April 2025, as a broad retreat in artificial intelligence shares weighed on the island’s tech-heavy equity market.
