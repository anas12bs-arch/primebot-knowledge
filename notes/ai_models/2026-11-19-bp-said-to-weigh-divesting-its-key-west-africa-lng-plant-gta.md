# BP Said to Weigh Divesting Its Key West Africa LNG Plant GTA

BP Plc has been considering divesting its majority stake and operatorship of its liquefied natural gas facility off the coast of West Africa, people familiar with the matter said, as chief executive officer Meg O’Neill aims to narrow the energy major’s portfolio.
