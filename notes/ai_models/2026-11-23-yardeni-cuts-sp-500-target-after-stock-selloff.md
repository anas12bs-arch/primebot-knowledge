# Yardeni Cuts S&P 500 Target After Stock Selloff

Wall Street strategist Ed Yardeni cut his S&amp;P 500 Index year-end forecast for the second time in about two months in the wake of a powerful stock selloff that pushed the benchmark into a technical correction.
The president and chief investment strategist of Yardeni Research Inc. sees the index closing out 2026 at 7,500 points, down from the reduced target of 7,900 he projected in September. That would still represent a recovery of more than 8% for US stocks from where they ended on Friday.
