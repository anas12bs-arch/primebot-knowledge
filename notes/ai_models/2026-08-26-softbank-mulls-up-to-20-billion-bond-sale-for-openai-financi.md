# SoftBank Mulls Up to $20 Billion Bond Sale for OpenAI Financing

SoftBank Group Corp. is talking with investment banks about a potential $10 billion to $20 billion bond offering to help refinance a loan for its investment in US tech giant OpenAI, according to people familiar with the matter.
