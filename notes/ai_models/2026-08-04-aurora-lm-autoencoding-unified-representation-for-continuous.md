# AURORA-LM: Autoencoding Unified Representation for Continuous-Latent Diffusion Language Modeling

Language remains an outlier in generative modeling: while images, video, and audio are increasingly modeled in continuous latent spaces, text generation still relies predominantly on discrete tokens.
