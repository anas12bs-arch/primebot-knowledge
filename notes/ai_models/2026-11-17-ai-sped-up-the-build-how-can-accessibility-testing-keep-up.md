# AI Sped Up The Build: How Can Accessibility Testing Keep Up?

AI has sped up how teams design and build, but quality assurance (QA) testing hasn’t kept pace, and the web may be getting less accessible. Drawing on new research, Andrew Chung explains what separates the teams whose accessibility efforts are keeping up, and how to embed human judgment into AI-enabled workflows without slowing delivery.
