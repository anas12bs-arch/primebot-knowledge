# Bond Yields Slip After Surging to Highest in Decades

Bonds steadied after a selloff that drove global yields to the highest in decades as the oil rally lost steam. The 10-year Treasury yield slipped three basis points to 5.17%, paring a two-day surge of more than 20 basis points. Bloomberg's Alice Gledhill reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
