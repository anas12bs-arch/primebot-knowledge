# Turkish Market Clearinghouse CEO Resigns as Fund Probe Widens

Yunus Arinci, the chief executive officer of Turkey’s settlement and custody bank Takasbank, resigned after being questioned by prosecutors as part of an investigation into the country’s fund crisis, according to people familiar with the matter.
