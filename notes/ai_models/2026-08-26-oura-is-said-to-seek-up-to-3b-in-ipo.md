# Oura Is Said to Seek Up to $3B in IPO

Oura, a maker of smart rings that track health, fitness and sleep, and some of its backers are seeking to raise as much as $3 billion in a US IPO, according to people familiar with the matter. Bloomberg's Scarlet Fu reports on "Bloomberg Deals." (Source: Bloomberg)
