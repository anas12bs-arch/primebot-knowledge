# How Chevron Is Maintaining Prominence in the Permian Basin

With nearly 2 million acres and an interest in one out of every five wells in the Permian Basin, Chevron has recently reached a production plateau and turned its focus to increasing reliability and efficiency in its West Texas operations. On Bloomberg Chief Future Officer, CFO Eimear Bonner shows Annmarie Hordern some of the facilities and technologies that contribute to the company's competitive advantage in the region.
