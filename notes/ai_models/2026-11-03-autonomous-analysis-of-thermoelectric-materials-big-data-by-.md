# Autonomous Analysis of Thermoelectric Materials Big Data by Multi-Agentic AI and Construction of a Knowledge Base and Specialized AI

Extracting knowledge from large databases of experimental materials data requires repeated analyses and reusable records of their choices and results. We developed Multi-Agentic AI (MAA), a multi-agent system in which AI agents with distinct roles analyze experimental thermoelectric data under predefined instructions and quality checks, saving analysis records traceable to their sources.
