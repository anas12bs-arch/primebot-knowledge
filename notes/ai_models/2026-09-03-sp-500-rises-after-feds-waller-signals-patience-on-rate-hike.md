# S&P 500 Rises After Fed’s Waller Signals Patience on Rate Hikes

The S&amp;P 500 Index rose Thursday as investors weighed remarks from Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller, who offered optimism that inflation is showing some signs of slowing. Traders pared bets for interest-rate hikes in September.
