# 01.AI's Kai-Fu Lee on Pacing Frontier AI Development

Kai-Fu Lee, CEO of 01.AI, says he suspects American AI companies may already have developed models that are "really great, but also really scary." He discusses this on "Bloomberg: The China Show" on the sidelines of the BNP Paribas Global Markets APAC Conference. (Source: Bloomberg)
