# The role of locality and weight sharing in convolutional neural networks

The success of convolutional neural networks is often attributed to locality and weight sharing. However, how these two properties separately shape learning, and how their effects depend on the spatial correlations of natural images, remain poorly understood. We address these questions in a teacher-student model with a convolutional teacher acting on $P$ input positions with correlation $c$ between positions.
