# Investment Firm Eurazeo Opens Abu Dhabi Office

European asset management firm Eurazeo has opened its first Middle East office in Abu Dhabi. Mathieu Teisseire, Eurazeo, Head of Investor Relations told Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on Horizons Middle East & Africa on why they decided to expand in the region despite the geopolitical tensions.
