# Top Yen Forecaster Predicts 147 Per Dollar as Rate Gap Narrows

Neil Parker has spent nearly three decades tracking the yen, through financial crises and sharp currency swings. Now, the top dollar-yen forecaster in Bloomberg’s latest rankings sees the Japanese currency rallying toward 147 per dollar next year as Japan’s interest-rate gap with the US narrows.
