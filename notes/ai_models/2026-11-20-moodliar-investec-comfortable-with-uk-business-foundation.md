# Moodliar: Investec Comfortable with UK Business Foundation

Investec shares slid after the lender reported first-half results, declining to the lowest level since March. Investors were disappointed by rising costs and weaker profitability in Investec’s UK operations, even as operating profit in its southern African business surged. Cumesh Moodliar, Investec South Africa CEO spoke to Bloomberg’s Jennifer Zabasajja on Horizons Middle East & Africa on their numbers. (Source: Bloomberg)
