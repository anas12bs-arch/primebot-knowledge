# Stranger Things: When Objects Appear Without Their Typical Neighbors

Visual context is a powerful cue for object recognition: scenes inform what, where, and at what scale objects are likely to appear. Yet over-reliance on context becomes a failure mode when models predict objects based on their typical neighbors rather than the objects' own visual features, which we call typicality bias.
