# Anthropic Deepens Finance Industry Ties With BlackRock

Jaime Magyera, BlackRock senior managing director and head of US Wealth and head of Retirement, said advisers are using artificial intelligence to help parse increasingly complex capital markets after AI company Anthropic announces new ties with Blackrock. Magyera also said that AI is helping create more strategic customization for clients. (Source: Bloomberg)
