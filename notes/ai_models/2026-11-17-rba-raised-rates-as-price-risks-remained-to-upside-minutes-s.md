# RBA Raised Rates as Price Risks Remained to Upside, Minutes Show

A majority of the Australian central bank’s nine-member board voted to raise interest rates two weeks ago on concerns that risks to the inflation outlook remained to the upside and consumer prices had stayed too high for too long.
