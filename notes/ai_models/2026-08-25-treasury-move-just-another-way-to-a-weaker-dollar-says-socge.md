# Treasury Move Just Another Way to a Weaker Dollar, Says SocGen’s Juckes

Kit Juckes, chief FX strategist at Societe Generale, says the US Treasury’s move to increase the purchases of long-dated bonds is “management of the market, rather than yet, something that you might call intervention of the bond market.” He speaks on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
