# Developer Concord Pacific Buys Wind Farm, Plots Data Centers

Concord Pacific Developments Inc. acquired a field of fifty wind turbines from Potentia Renewables Inc. for several hundred million Canadian dollars, deepening the Canadian developer’s long-term bet on renewables, its CEO said, adding that he’s working on a move into data centers too.
