# The Only Edge Was Memorized in Training: LLM Trading Agents Around Earnings and Bad News

Large language model (LLM) trading agents are tested on news, but news reaches stock prices within minutes, and a benchmark that replays headlines at their publication time finds no alpha in trading on them. Money can then remain in two places, before news whose date is known, such as earnings announcements, and after bad news, if the market's fall went too far.
