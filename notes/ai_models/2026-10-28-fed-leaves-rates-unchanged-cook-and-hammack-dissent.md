# Fed Leaves Rates Unchanged, Cook and Hammack Dissent

The Federal Open Market Committee voted 10-2 to hold its benchmark federal funds rate in a range of 3.75% to 4%. Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack were the dissenting votes. Bloomberg's Michael McKee reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
