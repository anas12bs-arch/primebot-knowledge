# Tata Sons’ Listed Shareholders Rally After RBI Renews IPO Push

Tata Group companies with minority ownership of Tata Sons Pvt. jumped following Indian banking regulator’s refusal to exempt the conglomerate’s holding entity from a rule that requires it to go for a public listing.
