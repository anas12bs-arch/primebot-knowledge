# Portugal to Negotiate With Lufthansa, Air France-KLM on TAP

Portugal’s government will enter an additional round of negotiations with Deutsche Lufthansa AG and Air France-KLM over the sale of a minority stake in TAP as it seeks to secure improved offers from both airline groups.
