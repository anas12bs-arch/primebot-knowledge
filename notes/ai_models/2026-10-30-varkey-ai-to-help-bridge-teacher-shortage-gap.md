# Varkey: AI to Help Bridge Teacher Shortage Gap

Ongoing regional conflict is testing the UAE's post-pandemic boom and the new school year is offering another gauge of the country's resilience. Dubai-based private school operator GEMS Education plans to launch a school model next January, which combines AI-powered, self-paced learning with teacher mentoring. Dino Varkey, Group CEO, GEMS Education spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on the AI deployment. (Source: Bloomberg)
