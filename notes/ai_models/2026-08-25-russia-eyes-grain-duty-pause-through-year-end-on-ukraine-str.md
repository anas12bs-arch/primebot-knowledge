# Russia Eyes Grain Duty Pause Through Year End on Ukraine Strikes

Russia is weighing a suspension of the floating export duty on wheat, barley and corn through the end of 2026 as exporters face mounting logistics problems after Ukrainian attacks disrupted shipments through the Black and Azov seas.
