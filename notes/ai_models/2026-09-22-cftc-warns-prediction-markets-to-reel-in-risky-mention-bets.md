# CFTC Warns Prediction Markets to Reel in Risky ‘Mention’ Bets

Prediction market wagers involving bets on what high-profile people might say in public present heightened manipulation risk and can only be offered in limited circumstances, Commodity Futures Trading Commission staff warned in new guidance.
