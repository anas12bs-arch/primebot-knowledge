# Ryanair Initiates Cost Drive to Offset $2 Billion Fuel Surge

Ryanair Holdings Plc will embark on a cost purge to help offset a €2 billion ($2.2 billion) increase in fuel charges next year, as the Middle East conflict continues to drive up oil prices and weighs on consumer willingness to book flights.
