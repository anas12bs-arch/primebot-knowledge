# Stronger UK-EU Ties Would Strengthen Pound, JPMorgan's Gimber Says

JPMorgan Asset Management Global Market Strategist Hugh Gimber comments on the outlook for the UK economy following yesterday's budget, saying "measures that brought the UK closer to the EU would enhance the prospect of strong investment over the years to come." He speaks before a live audience on The Opening Trade's "After the Budget, Before the Bell" — an exclusive event for Bloomberg subscribers in London. (Source: Bloomberg)
