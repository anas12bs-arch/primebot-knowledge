# VStress: Correlation-Aware Auditing and Adaptive Budget Allocation for Repeated Verifiers

Repeated verifier calls are useful only when they contribute conditional information. We introduce VStress, an auditable replay contract, and VStress-CA, a correlation-aware allocation policy that estimates the conditional marginal information of an unqueried verifier on a sealed calibration split, discounts uncertainty, normalizes by call cost, and stops or abstains when the next call is not informative.
