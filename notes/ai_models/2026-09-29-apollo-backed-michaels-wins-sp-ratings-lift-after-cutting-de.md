# Apollo-Backed Michaels Wins S&P Ratings Lift After Cutting Debt

Michaels Cos., the arts-and-crafts retailer owned by Apollo Global Management Inc., received a credit upgrade on Monday from S&amp;P Global. The ratings firm raised Michaels to B from B-, citing recent sales growth and debt paydowns.
