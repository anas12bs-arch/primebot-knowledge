# Gina Rinehart Backed Lynas to Buy Rare Earths Miner Meteoric

Australia’s Lynas Rare Earths Ltd. will acquire Meteoric Resources Ltd. in an all-share deal worth about A$968 million ($672 million), giving it access to a new deposit of the critical minerals in Brazil.
