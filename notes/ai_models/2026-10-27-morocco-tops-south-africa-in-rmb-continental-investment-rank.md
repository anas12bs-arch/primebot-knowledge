# Morocco Tops South Africa in RMB Continental Investment Ranking

Morocco overtook South Africa to become the continent’s fourth-most attractive investment destination as years of industrialization and faster growth propelled it ahead of the region’s biggest economy, FirstRand Ltd.’s Rand Merchant Bank said.
