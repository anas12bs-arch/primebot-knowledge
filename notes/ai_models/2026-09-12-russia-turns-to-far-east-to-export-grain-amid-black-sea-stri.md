# Russia Turns to Far East to Export Grain Amid Black Sea Strikes

Russia is turning to its Far East as one of the alternative ways to export its grain, as Ukrainian strikes disrupt flows from its key Black Sea ports, Agriculture Minister Oksana Lut said.
