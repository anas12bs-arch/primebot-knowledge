# Fed’s Warsh Needs to Launch Operation Twist, Academy’s Tchir Says

Peter Tchir, head of macro strategy at Academy Securities, calls the US Treasury Department’s move to increase its planned purchases of outstanding 10-year to 30-year debt “kind of mediocre” and says the Federal Reserve needs to talk about cutting rates and take hikes off the table.
