# Just Ice Tea Builds on Honest Tea’s Legacy

Just Ice Tea co-founders Seth Goldman and Spike Mendelsohn tell Bloomberg This Weekend how they built the organic tea brand after Coca-Cola discontinued Honest Tea and are expanding with Awaken, a cold-brew tea with 120 milligrams of caffeine. Speaking with host Lisa Mateo, they discuss changing consumer tastes, their approach to organic and fair-trade sourcing and why they see room for a mission-driven brand in the growing ready-to-drink tea market.
