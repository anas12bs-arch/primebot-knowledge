# Bohl: Iran Won’t Succumb to Trump’s Pressure Campaign

Iran has promoted a hardline ex-commander as its top security official, shaking up its highest decision-making body on national defense. It comes after Tehran said it was "very close" to a deal with Oman, over shipping through the Strait of Hormuz but say there has been no negotiations with the US. Ryan Bohl, Senior Middle East and North Africa Analyst at RANE spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on the likely scenario in the impasse. (Source: Bloomberg)
