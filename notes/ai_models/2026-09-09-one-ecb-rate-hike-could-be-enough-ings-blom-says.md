# One ECB Rate Hike 'Could Be Enough,' ING's Blom Says

Marieke Blom, chief economist and global head of research at ING, discusses the outlook for tomorrow's European Central Bank decision on interest rates. "If you look at core inflation, services, wages, and so forth, we don't see that much inflationary pressure," Blom tells Bloomberg Television. "We believe that one rate hike could be enough."
