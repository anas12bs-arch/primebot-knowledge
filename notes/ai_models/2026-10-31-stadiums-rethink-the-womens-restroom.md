# Stadiums Rethink the Women’s Restroom

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Writer and Journalist Benton Graham explains how stadiums and arenas are redesigning restrooms as women’s sports grow and venues recognize that equal restroom space does not necessarily mean equal access. Speaking with host Lisa Mateo, Graham says newer venues are dedicating more restroom capacity to women, while upgrades at older stadiums can carry significant costs. (Source: Bloomberg)
