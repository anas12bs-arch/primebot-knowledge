# European Union Needs to Reduce Dependency on China, Says Spain's Prime Minister

Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez said that the European Union needs to reduce its dependency on China and rebalance its dependence on other economies.  Sanchez, who sat down with Bloomberg's Francine Lacqua, said that one of the EU's strengths its is willingness to open its economies to other regions.  (Source: Bloomberg)
