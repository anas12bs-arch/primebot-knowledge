# Aon Seeks to Raise $13.5 Billion From Bond Sale to Fund USI Deal

Aon Plc is seeking to raise about $13.5 billion from a high-grade bond offering to help fund its planned $17 billion acquisition of USI Insurance Services, marking one of the biggest M&amp;A financing deals to come to market in 2026.
