# Paraguay on Watch for Downgrade at Fitch After Bank Collapse

Fitch Ratings placed Paraguay’s credit rating on watch for a potential downgrade on Wednesday, citing risks to the nation’s finances and economy from a banking crisis that toppled President Santiago Peña and rattled investors.
