# SketchDirector: Complex Scene Sketching via Agentic Layout Reasoning and Detail Refinement

Existing sketch generation methods often rely on image guidance and are largely limited to individual objects or simplified scenes, leaving text-guided complex scene sketching underexplored. We present SketchDirector, an agentic framework that mimics the human sketching workflow for automatic multi-concept scene sketching.
