# Pentagon Deputy Steered Defense Deals to His Goldman Banker

US Deputy Defense Secretary Steve Feinberg reportedly steered companies, including Lockheed Martin Corp. and RTX Corp., negotiating multibillion-dollar weapons deals with the federal government to his longtime Goldman Sachs banker, according to people familiar with the matter. Todd Gillespie reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
