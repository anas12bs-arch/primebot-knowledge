# Hormuz Hostilities Pick Up as Two Tankers Reportedly Struck

Two oil supertankers attempting to exit the Strait of Hormuz were struck late Monday by projectiles in quick succession, maritime security consultant Marisks said, the latest sign of renewed hostilities around the critical waterway.
