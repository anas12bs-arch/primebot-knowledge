# Anthropic IPO Could  Mark End of the AI Trade

Anna Wong, Bloomberg Economics Chief US Economist warns Anthropic’s slice of the token market has narrowed: and that the hyperscaler has surrendered a substantial part of its price premium without strengthening its competitive position. That’s a warning that enterprises may be moving workloads to cheaper models, weakening Anthropic’s pricing power. (Source: Bloomberg)
