# LIV Golf Loses Jon Rahm in Blow to League’s Bankruptcy-Exit Plan

LIV Golf’s top-ranked professional, Jon Rahm, is quitting the bankrupt golf tour and views its proposal to give players a majority ownership stake in the newly established enterprise as “unacceptable,” according to his lawyer.
