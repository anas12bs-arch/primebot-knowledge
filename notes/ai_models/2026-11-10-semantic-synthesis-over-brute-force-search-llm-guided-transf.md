# Semantic Synthesis over Brute-Force Search: LLM-Guided Transformation Design for Efficient Learning

Data transformation pipelines shape the training distribution in computer vision, but designing them is a computationally expensive problem. We investigate whether large language models can enable efficient discovery by synthesizing executable transformation programs outside predefined augmentation spaces.
