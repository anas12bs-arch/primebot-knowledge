# Talabat CEO: Grocery Demand Up Amid Mideast Situation

Food delivery giant Talabat reported its 3Q revenue jumped 18 percent to $1.2 billion. The Dubai-based firm says the growth is driven by robust demand during the summer and back-to-school periods. Talabat CEO Toon Gyssels spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on Horizons Middle East & Africa on the earnings. (Source: Bloomberg)
