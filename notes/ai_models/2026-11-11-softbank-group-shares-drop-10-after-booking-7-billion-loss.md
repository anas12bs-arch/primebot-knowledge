# SoftBank Group Shares Drop 10% After Booking $7 Billion Loss

SoftBank Group Corp. shares dropped as much as 11% after the Japanese tech investor reported its first net loss in almost two years, after a $3.7 billion writedown for its US data-center projects and falling AI valuations.
