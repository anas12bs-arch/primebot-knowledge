# Xi-Trump-Putin Trilateral: A Great Power Stitch-Up?

Trump, China’s Xi and Russia’s Putin will meet for a three-way summit this week.

It’s an unprecedented meeting that’s drawn comparisons with the summit at Yalta near the end of World War II — and raised concerns of a great power stitch-up. Rebecca Choong Wilkins has more. (Source: Bloomberg)
