# South Korea Keeps Fuel Cheap for Holidays as Fiscal Cost Mounts

With millions of South Koreans set to hit the road this week during a three-day public holiday, the government is extending fuel-price caps, cushioning households from an energy shock that has pushed global oil prices above $100 a barrel.
