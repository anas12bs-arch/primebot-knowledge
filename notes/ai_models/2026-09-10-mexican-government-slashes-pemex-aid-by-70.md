# Mexican Government Slashes Pemex Aid by 70%

Bloomberg's Alex Vasquez joins Scarlet Fu on "Bloomberg Deals." Mexicos President Claudia Sheinbaum’s proposed 2027 budget reduces support for state-owned oil company Petroleos Mexicanos, pledging 81 billion pesos ($4.8 billion) to help cover its debt payments, down nearly 70% from the amount allocated this year.
