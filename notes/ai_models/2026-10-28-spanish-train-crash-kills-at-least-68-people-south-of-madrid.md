# Spanish Train Crash Kills at Least 68 People South of Madrid

A high-speed rail service slammed into another train about 60 miles south of Madrid on Wednesday, killing at least 68 people — the second major accident on Spain’s rail network this year and the deadliest crash in over a decade. (Source: Bloomberg)
