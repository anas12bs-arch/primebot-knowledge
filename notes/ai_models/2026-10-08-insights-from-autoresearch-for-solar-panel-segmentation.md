# Insights from Autoresearch for Solar Panel Segmentation

This paper investigates AutoResearch, a protocol in which a coding language model edits a training program under a one-hour GPU budget and retains a change only if validation IoU improves. The protocol is applied to photovoltaic panel segmentation on a frozen real-image split, with DeepLabV3--ResNet-50 held fixed.
