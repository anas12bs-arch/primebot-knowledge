# New Balance CEO Joe Preston on NYC Marathon Partnership

Joe Preston, New Balance President and CEO, discussed the company’s long-running partnership with the New York City Marathon, which he described as a key part of the brand. More than 300 New Balance associates are in the city for race week, including 200 retail staff at the Javits Center expo, where more than 100,000 visitors are expected over three days. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close" on October 29, 2026. (Source: Bloomberg)
