# Nvidia Crisis Complicates Trump’s China Trip

On Bloomberg This Weekend, co-host of Bloomberg Surveillance Annmarie Hordern examines how scrutiny surrounding Nvidia and Jensen Huang is shifting the focus of President Donald Trump’s China trip from trade toward chip export controls and questions about how restricted technology may have reached China. Speaking with host Christina Ruffini, Hordern also discusses mounting pressure on the Commerce Department and how the controversy could lead Congress to pursue tighter controls on advanced chips
