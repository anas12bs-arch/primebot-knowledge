# EngramEdit: Decoupled Knowledge Updates in LLMs through Conditional Memory

Conditional memory architectures such as DeepSeek Engram use input n-grams to look up learned embeddings, expanding the capacity of large language models (LLMs) with limited additional computation. Beyond model scaling, this architecture has demonstrated the potential to decouple factual knowledge storage from general-purpose computation, offering a promising route to updating factual knowledge while keeping the Transformer backbone fixed.
