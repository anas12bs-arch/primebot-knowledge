# Loop Capital CEO on Expanding Athlete Wealth Platform

Loop Capital is buying $450 million in client assets from Lifeline Capital to expand its wealth management business and serve more athletes and entertainers. Founder, Chairman and CEO Jim Reynolds said the firm encourages athletes to invest in companies and gain ownership rather than rely on short-term endorsement payments. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
