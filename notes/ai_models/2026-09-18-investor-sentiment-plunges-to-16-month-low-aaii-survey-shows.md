# Investor Sentiment Plunges to 16-Month Low, AAII Survey Shows

Investor sentiment fell to its lowest point since 2025, a weekly survey from the American Association of Individual Investors showed, as oil prices remained elevated while the Federal Reserve raised interest rates for the first time since 2023.
