# BofA Rejigs Global Credit Trading Businesses to Boost Liquidity

Bank of America Corp. is combining its global credit macro, fixed income exchange-traded fund, and high-grade corporate bond trading businesses into a single platform as the Wall Street firm looks to streamline credit trading for clients and boost the liquidity it can offer.
