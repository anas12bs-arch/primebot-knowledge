# Anthropic Prices at $83 a Share in Record-Sized Offering

Anthropic PBC and its co-founders raised $107.3 billion in an upsized IPO and private placement. The artificial intelligence firm sold 956.4 million shares and the co-founders sold 23.6 million shares for $83 each, according to a statement, above the marketed range. The IPO is the biggest of all time, topping SpaceX’s $75 billion base deal. Sopnendu Mohanty, Group CEO of Global Finance and Technology Network, discusses some of the risks associated with the valuation. (Source: Bloomberg)
