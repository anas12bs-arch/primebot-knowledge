# Souaid: IMF Lebanon Talks Progressing At ‘Good Pace’

Lebanese Central Bank Governor Karim Souaid said talks with the IMF are progressing at a good pace. This comes as the country commenced high level negotiations with the Fund on the sidelines of the IMF-World Bank meetings in Bangkok. Souaid spoke exclusively to Bloomberg’s Avril Hong on Horizons Middle East & Africa. (Source: Bloomberg)
