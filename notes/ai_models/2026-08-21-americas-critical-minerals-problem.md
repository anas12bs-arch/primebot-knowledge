# America’s Critical Minerals Problem

Critical Metals CEO and Executive Chairman Tony Sage joins Bloomberg Open Interest to warn the US is still years away from breaking China’s grip on key rare-earth metals, with processing capacity emerging as the biggest hurdle. He explains the geopolitical risk of that delay and pushes back on concerns surrounding its Greenland project, saying production remains on track for 2029. (Source: Bloomberg)
