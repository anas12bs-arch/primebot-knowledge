# Trump Xi Summit Leaves Big Issues Unresolved

Bloomberg North Asia correspondent Stephen Engle tells Bloomberg This Weekend that President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping bought more time to negotiate over trade while major disagreements involving Taiwan, Iran and technology remain. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Engle explains how competition over AI and unresolved economic and security issues are shaping the next phase of US-China relations.
