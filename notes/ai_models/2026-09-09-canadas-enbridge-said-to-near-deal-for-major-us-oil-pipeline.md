# Canada’s Enbridge Said to Near Deal for Major US Oil Pipeline

Enbridge Inc., the Canadian energy infrastructure giant, is in advanced talks to buy a major crude oil pipeline in the US from Blackstone Inc.’s Tallgrass Energy for about $2 billion, according to people familiar with the matter.
