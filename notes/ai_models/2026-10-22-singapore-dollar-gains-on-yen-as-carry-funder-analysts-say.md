# Singapore Dollar Gains on Yen as Carry Funder, Analysts Say

The Singapore dollar is gaining favor as a funding currency for carry trades as cheap borrowing costs and fading expectations for appreciation make it increasingly attractive relative to the Japanese yen, strategists say.
