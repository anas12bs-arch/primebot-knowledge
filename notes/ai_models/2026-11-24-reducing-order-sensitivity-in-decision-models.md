# Reducing Order Sensitivity in Decision Models

Decision models are emerging as fast components of larger AI systems. Yet recent decision models, such as Jev, process options sequentially, leading to different predictions based on their ordering. Across ten open- and closed-source decision models, we find that \emph{order-sensitivity} is pervasive, causing substantial accuracy degradation and unreliable decision-making.
