# AI Agents Test the Limits of Human Oversight

OpenAI disclosed six cases of unexpected or concerning model behavior as AI companies grapple with how to monitor increasingly autonomous agents and whether stronger independent oversight is needed. Joining David Gura on Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg News AI Reporter Shirin Ghaffary explains that the safety debate is taking on geopolitical urgency as President Donald Trump emphasizes maintaining the US lead over China while resisting calls to slow AI development.
