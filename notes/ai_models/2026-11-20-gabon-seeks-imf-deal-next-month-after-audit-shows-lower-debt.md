# Gabon Seeks IMF Deal Next Month After Audit Shows Lower Debt

Gabon expects to clinch a deal with the International Monetary Fund on a new program by next month and is paying down arrears as a show of good faith while awaiting the lender’s sign-off on a debt audit.
