# Boots Sold for $8.9 Billion as Canada’s Westons Re-Enter UK

The Canadian branch of the billionaire Weston family agreed to buy health retailer Boots for $8.9 billion including debt, in another change of hands for the pharmacy chain and a UK return for the former Selfridges owner.
