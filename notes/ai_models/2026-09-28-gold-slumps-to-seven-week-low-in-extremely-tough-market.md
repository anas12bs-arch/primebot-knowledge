# Gold Slumps to Seven-Week Low in ‘Extremely Tough’ Market

Gold sank to its lowest in more than seven weeks as the standoff between US and Iran over the Strait of Hormuz kept energy costs elevated and reinforced pressure on the Federal Reserve for more interest-rate hikes.
