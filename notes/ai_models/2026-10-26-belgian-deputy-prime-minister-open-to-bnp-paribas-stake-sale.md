# Belgian Deputy Prime Minister Open to BNP Paribas Stake Sale

Belgian Deputy Prime Minister David Clarinval sees the country’s 5.7% stake in BNP Paribas SA as non-strategic and suggested he would be open selling some or all of the holding.
