# MMG Seeks to Allay EU Concerns Over Anglo American Deal

China’s MMG Ltd. is seeking to avoid a potential European Union veto of its planned $500 million Anglo American Plc deal in Brazil with a fresh tranche of remedies aimed at allaying regulators’ concerns over the future supply of ferro nickel to the EU’s stainless-steel industry.
