# Inside a Packaging Industry Mega-Deal

Silgan is teaming up with American Industrial Partners to acquire Trivium Packaging in a deal valued at about $3.4 billion dollars.  Silgan President and CEO Adam J. Greenlee discusses the strategic fit, expected benefits for shareholders and what the deal says about consumer demand and the broader economy. (Source: Bloomberg)
