# Sushi Restaurants Put on Tuna Carving Dinner Shows

Sushi restaurants are swapping soft music and carefully plated fish for the spectacle of breaking down 200-pound bluefin tuna in front of paying customers. Some are charging well into five figures to buy out the restaurant for the night. Sunny Kim reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
