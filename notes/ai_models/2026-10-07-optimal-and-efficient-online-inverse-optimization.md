# Optimal and Efficient Online Inverse Optimization

In online inverse linear optimization, a learner recommends an action and then observes the choice of an expert who maximizes a fixed, unknown linear objective on $\mathbb{R}^{d}$; the goal is to learn to optimize this objective without observing it.
