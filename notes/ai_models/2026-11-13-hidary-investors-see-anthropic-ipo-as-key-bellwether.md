# Hidary: Investors See Anthropic IPO as Key Bellwether

Anthropic’s IPO is said to be about 6 times oversubscribed. The Claude AI Model firm and its co-founders are seeking to raise $64.4 billion in the public listing as well as $26 billion in private placement, making it bigger than Space X’s IPO. Jack Hidary, CEO of SandboxAQ spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on Horizons Middle East & Africa about the listing as well as his deals in the UAE on the sidelines of ADIPEC. (Source: Bloomberg)
