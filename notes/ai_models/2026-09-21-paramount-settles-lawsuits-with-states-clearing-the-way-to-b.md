# Paramount Settles Lawsuits With States Clearing the Way to Buy WBD

Paramount Skydance settles lawsuits with 12 states clearing the way for it to buy Warner Bros. Discovery Inc. The deal limits Paramount's market power and it promises to release 30 movies a year at the combined studios. Charlie Pellett has the breaking news. (Source: Bloomberg)
