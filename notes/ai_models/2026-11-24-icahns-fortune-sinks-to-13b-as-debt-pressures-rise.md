# Icahn’s Fortune Sinks to $1.3b as Debt Pressures Rise

Carl Icahn’s fortune has dwindled to about $1.3 billion as mounting debt squeezes his business empire, leaving the longtime corporate raider and activist investor facing one of the toughest stretches of his career. When Icahn Enterprises reported third-quarter earnings this month, it warned there was no assurance it could refinance $1.4 billion of bonds due in May on acceptable terms — or at all.
