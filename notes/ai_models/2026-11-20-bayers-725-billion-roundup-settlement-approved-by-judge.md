# Bayer’s $7.25 Billion Roundup Settlement Approved by Judge

A Missouri judge approved a Bayer AG proposal to contain its mounting legal costs by paying as much as $7.25 billion to resolve tens of thousands of US lawsuits by people who blame the company’s Roundup weedkiller for their cancers.
