# Philippines’ Main Coal Mine in Limbo Puts Nation’s Energy Supply at Risk

Sheila Potestad has spent two decades working as a welder at the Philippines’ largest coal mine on Semirara Island, on the western side of the archipelago. The mother of two is employed there alongside her husband. Now, more than 200 dump trucks, bulldozers and excavators are idle, seawater has forced a shutdown at one site, and the right to operate the mine is up for auction.
