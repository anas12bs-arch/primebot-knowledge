# Carlyle Buys $7.7 Billion in Consumer Loans as Truist Revamps

Carlyle Group Inc. has agreed to buy $7.7 billion of unsecured personal loans from Truist Financial Corp., among the asset manager’s largest such deals as it expands a push into asset-back credit.
