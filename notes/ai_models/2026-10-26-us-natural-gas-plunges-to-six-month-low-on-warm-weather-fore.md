# US Natural Gas Plunges to Six-Month Low on Warm Weather Forecast

US natural gas futures plummeted as much as 5.4% to the lowest since April as forecasts shifted significantly warmer over the weekend, indicating lower demand for the furnace and power-plant fuel during the early weeks of the winter heating season.
