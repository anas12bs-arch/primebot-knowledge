# Oil Supply Risks Rise After Saudi Pipeline Attack

Saudi Arabia shut its East-West Pipeline as a precaution after multiple attacks, threatening a crucial route for moving crude to the Red Sea while bypassing disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz. Bloomberg Economics' Ziad Daoud says prolonged disruption could tighten global energy supplies further, adding pressure to oil and US diesel prices. (Source: Bloomberg)
