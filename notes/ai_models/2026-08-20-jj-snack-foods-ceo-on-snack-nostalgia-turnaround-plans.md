# J&J Snack Foods CEO on Snack Nostalgia, Turnaround Plans

Dan Fachner, CEO of J&amp;J Snack Foods, discussed the company's strategic focus on the food service segment, which accounts for 60% of its business. He emphasized the strong recovery and growth potential in experiential venues such as theaters, theme parks, and ballparks, where iconic brands like ICEE, Dippin' Dots, and Super Pretzel are popular. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
