# ARG-Avatar: Animation-Rendering Guidance for Efficient Feedforward Avatar Reconstruction

Reconstructing animatable 3D human avatars from images is important for applications such as gaming and augmented reality. Feedforward methods make avatar reconstruction accessible by reducing inference time to a few seconds, but recent approaches rely heavily on scaling model capacity, training data, and computation.
