# Nigeria Exchange CEO Sees Dangote IPO Spurring New Listings

Nigeria’s stock exchange is in talks with several companies about listing and expects some from next year as Dangote Petroleum Refinery and Petrochemicals FZE’s record share sale spurs interest in the nation’s bourse as a capital-raising venue.
