# Iran Shakes Up Security Team Claiming Oman Deal ‘Close’

Iran promoted a hard-line ex-commander as its top security official, shaking up its highest decision-making body on national defense after saying it was “very close” to a deal with Oman over shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Bloomberg's Stuart Livingstone-Wallace shares what we know about the situation. (Source: Bloomberg)
