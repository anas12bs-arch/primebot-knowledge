# Google, Constellation Energy Strike Nuclear Power Deal

Google parent Alphabet Inc. agreed to buy nuclear power from Constellation Energy in a 20-year power-purchase agreement that will drive more than $4.3 billion in investments to upgrade systems at 11 Constellation reactors. Ed Ludlow has more on "Bloomberg Open Interest." (Source: Bloomberg)
