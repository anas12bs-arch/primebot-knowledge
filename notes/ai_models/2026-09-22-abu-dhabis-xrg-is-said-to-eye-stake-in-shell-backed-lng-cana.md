# Abu Dhabi’s XRG Is Said to Eye Stake in Shell-Backed LNG Canada

Abu Dhabi’s XRG is exploring the acquisition of a stake in the Shell Plc-led LNG Canada export project, as the state-owned group pursues plans to become a major global supplier of natural gas, people familiar with the matter said.
