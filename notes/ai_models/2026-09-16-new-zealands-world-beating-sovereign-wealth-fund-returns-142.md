# New Zealand’s World-Beating Sovereign Wealth Fund Returns 14.2%

The New Zealand Superannuation Fund, one of the world’s top-performing sovereign wealth funds, returned 14.2% in the year through June, powered by a rally in global equity markets despite being underweight in outperforming tech stocks.
