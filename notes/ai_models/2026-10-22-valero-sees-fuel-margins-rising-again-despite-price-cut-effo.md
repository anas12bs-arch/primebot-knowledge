# Valero Sees Fuel Margins Rising Again Despite Price-Cut Efforts

Fuel-making margins, which have dipped from a record, are poised to rise once again despite efforts by Washington to curb gasoline and diesel prices, a Valero Energy Corp. executive said on Thursday.
