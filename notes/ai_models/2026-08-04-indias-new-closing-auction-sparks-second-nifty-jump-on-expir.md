# India’s New Closing Auction Sparks Second Nifty Jump on Expiry

India’s first weekly derivatives expiry under the new closing auction system did little to ease concerns about the new mechanism after the benchmark NSE Nifty 50 Index again moved sharply during the auction, after surprising traders on Monday.
