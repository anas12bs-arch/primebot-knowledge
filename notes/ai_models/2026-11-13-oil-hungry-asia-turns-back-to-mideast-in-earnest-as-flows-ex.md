# Oil-Hungry Asia Turns Back to Mideast in Earnest as Flows Expand

Oil refiners in Asia are increasingly turning back toward crude from Middle Eastern producers as flows rebound after months of conflict, lessening the need for imports from alternative suppliers including the US.
