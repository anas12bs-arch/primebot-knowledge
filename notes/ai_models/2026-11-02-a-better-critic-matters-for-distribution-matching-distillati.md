# A Better Critic Matters for Distribution Matching Distillation

Distribution Matching Distillation (DMD) trains a few-step student using the difference between a teacher's score and an online critic's estimate of the student distribution's score. We investigate what the critic needs, in capacity and in information, to provide a reliable training signal.
