# Linear Bandits under Exact Sliding-Window Constraints

We study linear bandits under exact sliding-window constraints, where every consecutive block of actions must belong to a prescribed feasible set. In the offline setting, where the reward function is known, we show that convexity and cyclic-shift invariance make a stationary solution optimal when $w\mid T$ and within an additive $O(w)$ gap otherwise.
