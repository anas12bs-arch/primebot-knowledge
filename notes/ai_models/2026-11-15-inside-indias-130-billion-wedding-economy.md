# Inside India’s $130 Billion Wedding Economy

India’s weddings are a major economic force, generating an estimated $130 billion in annual spending.. For many families, the financial commitment begins years before the ceremony through savings and gold accumulation, while some households borrow against existing jewelry to finance wedding expenses.
