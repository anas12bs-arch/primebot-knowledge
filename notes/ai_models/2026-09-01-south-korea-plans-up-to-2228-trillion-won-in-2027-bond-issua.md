# South Korea Plans Up to 222.8 Trillion Won in 2027 Bond Issuance

South Korea plans to issue up to 222.8 trillion won ($162.7 billion) of bonds in 2027, a total near this year’s record, as President Lee Jae Myung pushes his expansionary fiscal agenda.
