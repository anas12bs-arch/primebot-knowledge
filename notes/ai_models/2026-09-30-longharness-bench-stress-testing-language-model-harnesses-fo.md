# LongHarness Bench: Stress-Testing Language Model Harnesses for Long-Context Reasoning

Language-model (LM) harnesses enable LMs to operate effectively over long contexts using additional compute. However, existing long-context evaluations are insufficient for distinguishing modern harnesses, reflected by saturated accuracy across harnesses and largely similar evaluation costs. In this paper, we introduce a benchmark for evaluating both the effectiveness and efficiency of long-context harnesses.
