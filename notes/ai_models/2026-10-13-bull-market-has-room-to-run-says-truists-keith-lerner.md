# Bull Market Has Room to Run, Says Truist's Keith Lerner

Stocks bounced from session lows as speculation the Federal Reserve won’t be forced to raise rates aggressively offset lingering worries about elevated oil prices and the outlook for artificial intelligence. Keith Lerner, CIO and chief market strategist at Truist Advisory Services, says the four-year-old bull market has room to run, with AI and technology still central to its prospects. He speaks with hosts Carol Massar and Tim Stenovec on Bloomberg Businessweek Daily. (Source: Bloomberg)
