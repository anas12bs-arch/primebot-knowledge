# Philip Morris CEO Is ‘Optimistic’ About Zyn Potential

Jacek Olczak discusses Zyn's strong demand and asking whether those issues have now been resolved. The conversation also focused on Philip Morris International’s year-end market share target for Zyn and whether offering larger cans with 15 to 20 pouches could boost revenue as well as margins. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
