# ProtoVis-Nav: Prototypical Visual Imagination for Visual-Spatial Aligned UAV Navigation

Vision language navigation (VLN) is very challenging for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) since small errors in 3D motion prediction can lead to navigation errors and collisions. Unable to fathom execution outcomes in 3D environments, existing methods often struggle to predict accurate navigation waypoints from language instructions.
