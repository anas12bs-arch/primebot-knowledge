# BofA's Cabana: Front-End Yields Face Repricing Risk

Mark Cabana, Co-Head of Global Rates Research at BofA Global Research, says the front end of the global yield curve could reprice higher as central banks move to reduce accommodation. He spoke on the sidelines of the BofA APAC Conference in Hong Kong.
