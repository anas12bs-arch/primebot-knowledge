# Treasury Revamps Debt-Sale Guidance, Holds Auctions Steady

The US Treasury revamped the guidance on sales of notes and bonds it has used since early 2024, with new language in the so-called quarterly refunding announcement that may open the door to cuts in the issuance of the longest-dated debt in 2027. Michael McKee reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
