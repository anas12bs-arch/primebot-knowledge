# Northeast Storm Brings Wind, Rain and Flooding

Bloomberg News Meteorologist Craig Allen tells Bloomberg This Weekend that the slow-moving nor’easter is weakening but will continue bringing rain and flooding risks to parts of the Northeast after strong winds and coastal flooding battered the region. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Allen says New York through Boston has borne the brunt of the storm, with additional rain possible even as the strongest winds subside.
