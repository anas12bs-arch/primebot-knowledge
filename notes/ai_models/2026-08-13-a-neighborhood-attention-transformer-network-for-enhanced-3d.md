# A Neighborhood Attention Transformer Network for Enhanced 3D Segmentation of the Left Anterior Descending Artery

Background: Accurate segmentation of the Left Anterior Descending (LAD) artery in 3D free-breathing, non-contrast CT is critical for cardiac dose sparing in thoracic radiotherapy. The LAD is extremely small, has poor soft-tissue contrast, and varies substantially across patients; even manual contours show limited inter-observer agreement, underscoring the ambiguity of the vessel boundaries. Purpose: To develop a transformer-based framework that improves LAD delineation in low-contrast, imbalance
