# Younan: Regional Tensions Not New for Sovereign Funds

Strategic partnerships are becoming increasingly important for sovereign wealth funds in the Gulf region. Despite recent geopolitical volatility, Gulf SWFs have continued investing actively, in an effort to prioritize long term value creation and reduce risk. Wael Younan, Sovereign Wealth Management Head at TCW Group spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar about the strategy of these funds in light of geopolitical tensions. (Source: Bloomberg)
