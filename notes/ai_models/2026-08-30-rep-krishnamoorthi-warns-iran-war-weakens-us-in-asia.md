# Rep. Krishnamoorthi Warns Iran War Weakens US in Asia

Representative Raja Krishnamoorthi (D) says the Iran war is depleting key US munitions and pulling military assets from the Indo Pacific, potentially weakening deterrence against China as concerns over Taiwan grow. While on Bloomberg This Weekend, the Illinois Democrat also criticized the administration’s trade fight with Canada and Venezuela oil policy while calling for more US assistance to Nepal following catastrophic flooding.
