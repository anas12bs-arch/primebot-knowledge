# MEMIG: Self-evolving Memory Construction for Antibody Optimization

Memory has become central to self-evolving LLM agents, which store and retrieve experience from past rollouts to guide future decisions. Existing systems represent this experience as free-form natural language and maintain it by rewriting, which fits tasks whose experience is narratable. However, in scientific discovery and optimization, experience is often a quantity measured by an oracle under a specific context.
