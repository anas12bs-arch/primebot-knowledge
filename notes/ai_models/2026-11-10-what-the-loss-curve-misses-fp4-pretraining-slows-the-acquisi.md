# What the Loss Curve Misses: FP4 Pretraining Slows the Acquisition of Rare Facts

Low-precision pretraining is usually judged by validation loss. We ask whether matching loss also means learning rare facts as efficiently. We train transformers from scratch with emulated 4-bit matrix multiplications on synthetic biographies with controlled exposure counts, on their own and mixed into web text.
