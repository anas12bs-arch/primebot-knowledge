# Harry, Meghan Head Back to Britain After Six Years

Prince Harry and Meghan Markle plan to return to Britain with Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet after six years in the US, with their children set to begin school there in September. Bloomberg News Reporter Tiwa Adebayo explains to David Gura and Christina Ruffini on Bloomberg This Weekend that the surprise move brings Harry closer to King Charles as he undergoes cancer treatment, while questions remain over the family’s security and Harry’s strained relationship with Prince William.
