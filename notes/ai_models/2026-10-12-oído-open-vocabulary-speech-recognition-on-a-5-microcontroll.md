# Oído: Open-Vocabulary Speech Recognition on a $5 Microcontroller without a Neural Accelerator

Speech recognition on low-cost microcontrollers is, in practice, limited to keyword spotting or a fixed list of commands. We present Oído, an inference engine that runs open-vocabulary English speech recognition entirely on an ESP32-S3 (240 MHz dual-core Xtensa LX7, 512 KB of SRAM, 8 MB of PSRAM, 16 MB of flash, no neural accelerator).
