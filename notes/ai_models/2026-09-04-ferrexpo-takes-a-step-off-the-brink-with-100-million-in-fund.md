# Ferrexpo Takes a Step Off the Brink With $100 Million in Funding

Ferrexpo Plc, an iron ore miner in Ukraine working to stave off insolvency, raised about $100 million from investors through a long-awaited fundraising program that it had called essential for its continued survival.
