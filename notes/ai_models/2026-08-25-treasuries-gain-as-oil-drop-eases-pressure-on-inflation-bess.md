# Treasuries Gain as Oil Drop Eases Pressure on Inflation, Bessent

Treasuries gained as a decline in crude oil eased inflation concerns and the pressure on Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, who has been moving to halt a months-long selloff that pushed the longest-dated yields to the highest in almost two decades.
