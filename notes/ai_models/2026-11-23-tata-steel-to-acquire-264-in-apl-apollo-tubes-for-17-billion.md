# Tata Steel to Acquire 26.4% in APL Apollo Tubes for $1.7 Billion

Tata Steel Ltd. has agreed to acquire a 26.4% stake in APL Apollo Tubes Ltd. for as much as 155.1 billion rupees ($1.7 billion), deepening its presence in India’s structural steel products market.
