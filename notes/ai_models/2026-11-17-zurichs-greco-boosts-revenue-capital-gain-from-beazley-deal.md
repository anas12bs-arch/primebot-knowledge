# Zurich’s Greco Boosts Revenue, Capital Gain From Beazley Deal

Zurich Insurance Group AG said it now expects increased revenue of around $1.5 billion per year in the medium term as a result of the integration of Beazley Plc, the specialty insurer it bought earlier this year.
