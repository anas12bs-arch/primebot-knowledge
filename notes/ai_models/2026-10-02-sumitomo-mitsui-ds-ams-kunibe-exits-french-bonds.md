# Sumitomo Mitsui DS AM’s Kunibe Exits French Bonds

Sumitomo Mitsui DS Asset Management’s Shinji Kunibe said that funds in his global fixed-income group have sold their entire holdings of French government bonds amid concerns about the nation’s fiscal situation, shifting funds to German bunds and short-term Japanese government bonds.
