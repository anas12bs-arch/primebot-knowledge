# Fall in China Demand May Signal Need for Easing: Analyst

BofA Global Research Chief Greater China Economist Helen Qiao said the Chinese economy could see a further deterioration of domestic demand. China’s growth weakened across the board in October as consumption and industrial output undershot estimates while investment unexpectedly worsened, in a sign Beijing’s latest stimulus measures have yet to feed through to the economy.
