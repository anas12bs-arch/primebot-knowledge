# ByteDance Draws ADIA, Coller to Primavera’s Continuation Fund

Middle East sovereign wealth fund Abu Dhabi Investment Authority and private equity firm Coller Capital are in talks to back a continuation vehicle anchored by a stake in Chinese tech giant ByteDance, according to people familiar with the matter.
