# Represented but misread: Post-training changes how language models read out what they represent

Does post-training change what a language model represents, or how this representation is read out into an answer? We compared linear probes with the models' own answers, analyzed with signal detection theory, in six open-weight base models and their post-trained versions. For judgments of whether German texts were written by a human or an AI, probes and word n-gram classifiers separated the classes well, but the answers did not and often ordered the texts inversely.
