# Bawag’s €1.6 Billion PTSB Deal Rejected by Irish High Court

The Irish High Court declined to approve Bawag Group AG’s takeover of Irish lender Permanent TSB Group Holdings Plc, saying that the Austrian bank must also seek approval of minority shareholders.
