# Anthony Scaramucci's "All the Wrong Moves"

Former Trump Administration official and Founder of Skybridge, Anthony Scaramucci, is out with a new book "All the Wrong Moves: How Three Catastrophic Decisions Led to the Rise of Trump." He joins Bloomberg Businessweek Daily to discuss the lessons he learned from his time on Wall Street to his short-lived time in the White House and extended exposure to the Trump-world. He tries to make the case for what bipartisan money mistakes lead to today's "political crisis" and how we can get out of it.
