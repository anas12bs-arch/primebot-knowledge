# How Valterra's Forging the Future of Platinum | Chief Financial Officer

Chief Financial Officers now play a critical role in shaping corporate strategy and positioning organizations to meet future challenges. This episode profiles Valterra Platinum CFO Sayurie Naidoo, who leads the world's largest company producing platinum group metals. One year after its demerger from Anglo American, the South African firm is looking ahead to develop new markets for PGMs as the adoption of battery electric vehicles threatens their primary area of demand.
