# Pirelli Falls as Sinochem Sells €455 Million Stake in Tiremaker

Pirelli &amp; C. SpA shares fell after Chinese state-owned Sinochem sold a €455 million ($500 million) stake in the Italian tiremaker at a discount, extending a series of disposals aimed at reducing its influence over the company.
