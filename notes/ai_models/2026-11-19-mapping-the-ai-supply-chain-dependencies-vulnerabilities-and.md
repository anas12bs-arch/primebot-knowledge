# Mapping the AI Supply Chain: Dependencies, Vulnerabilities, and Market Concentration in the AI Ecosystem

AI systems are built across many organizations that contribute models, data, compute, capital, talent, and other assets. While the layers of the AI stack are relatively well understood, the relationships between actors and components across layers remains sparsely documented. In this work, we focus on the organizations that make up the AI supply chain: the complex network of actors that contribute to the development and deployment of AI.
