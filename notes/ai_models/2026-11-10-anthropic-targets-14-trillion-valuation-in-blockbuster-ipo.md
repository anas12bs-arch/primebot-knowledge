# Anthropic Targets $1.4 Trillion Valuation in Blockbuster IPO

Anthropic is seeking a valuation of about $1.4 trillion in its initial public offering, a bid to make history as the first frontier artificial intelligence lab to go public. The company’s IPO is set to price on Wednesday, Nov. 18 and trade the following day. Bailey Lipschultz has more. (Source: Bloomberg)
