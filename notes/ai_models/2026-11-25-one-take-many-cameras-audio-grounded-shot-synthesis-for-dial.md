# One Take, Many Cameras: Audio-Grounded Shot Synthesis for Dialogue Re-Cinematography

Creating a dialogue scene typically requires cutting between multiple views (e.g., close-ups, medium shots, and over-the-shoulder shots) of the actors. Yet capturing such coverage is costly and in many settings unavailable. We study dialogue re-cinematography: synthesizing additional shots from a single wide-angle dialogue video.
