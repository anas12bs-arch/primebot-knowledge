# Wall Street’s Leveraged ETF Push Gets New Twist

Bloomberg's Isabelle Lee joins Scarlet Fu and Eric Balchunas on "Bloomberg ETF IQ." A corner of the ETF market drawing regulatory scrutiny is pushing for still shorter-term speculation. Defiance ETFs has submitted paperwork to the US Securities and Exchange Commission for a series of leveraged funds that would seek to double the moves of some individual stocks — over periods measured in just hours, rather than days.
