# Brazil’s Patria Sells Colombia Solar Park to Isagen

Brazilian alternative asset manager Patria Investments Ltd and minority partner Ashmore Colombia agreed to sell one of Colombia’s largest solar parks to power generator Isagen SA for an undisclosed amount, completing an investment cycle that took the project from early-stage development to operation.
