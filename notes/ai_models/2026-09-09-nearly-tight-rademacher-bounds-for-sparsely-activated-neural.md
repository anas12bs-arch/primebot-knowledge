# Nearly Tight Rademacher Bounds for Sparsely Activated Neural Networks

An input may activate few hidden units even when different inputs collectively use an entire network. We study the statistical complexity of this input-dependent sparsity in the one-hidden-layer ReLU model of Awasthi et al. (COLT 2024). For width $s$, at most $k$ active units per input, and effective weight and bias bounds $W,B$, every size-$m$ sample in the class's fixed radius-$R$ input domain satisfies $\mathcal{R}(S)\le CWR\min\{k,\sqrt{sk/m}\log^{3/2}(2m)\}+kB/\sqrt m$.
