# Venezuela Plans to Ship Aluminum to US in Mercuria-Heeney Deal

Venezuela is preparing to ship aluminum to the US under a deal with Mercuria Energy Group Ltd. and Heeney Capital, diversifying beyond oil in a quickening revival of commercial ties following years of economic and diplomatic isolation.
