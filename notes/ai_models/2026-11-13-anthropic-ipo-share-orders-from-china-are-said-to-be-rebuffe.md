# Anthropic IPO Share Orders From China Are Said to Be Rebuffed

Institutional investors based in mainland China and Hong Kong have been excluded from placing orders in Anthropic PBC’s initial public offering, according to people familiar with the matter, highlighting the sensitivities around an industry both Washington and Beijing consider strategic.
