# Hurricane Isaias Slams Florida Panhandle

AccuWeather Correspondent Bill Wadell joins hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini on Bloomberg This Weekend from Pensacola Beach, Florida, where Hurricane Isaias made landfall as a Category 2 storm, bringing damaging winds, flooding and widespread power outages across the Gulf Coast. Wadell says the storm has weakened as it moves inland, but downed power lines, debris and continued flooding remain concerns as crews begin restoring electricity and assessing the damage.
