# Active Feature Acquisition by Distilling an Oracle Teacher

Acquiring every feature of an instance is often costly: a measurement can carry a monetary price, a time delay, or a privacy cost. Active Feature Acquisition (AFA) addresses this by learning a policy that decides, per instance, which features to acquire under a budget. Existing AFA methods train this policy by optimizing a self-constructed utility target rather than distilling an explicit teacher. We construct this teacher explicitly.
