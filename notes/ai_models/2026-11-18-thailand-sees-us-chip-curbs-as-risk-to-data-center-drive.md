# Thailand Sees US Chip Curbs as Risk to Data Center Drive

New US restrictions on the export and access to top-end semiconductors risks denting Thailand’s ambitions to be a hub for data centers, the foreign minister said.
Foreign Minister Sihasak said in a BTV interview with Haslinda Amin that the new rules, announced this week just as President Donald Trump traveled to Asia, could impact its ambitions and that “we have to be more careful now.” (Source: Bloomberg)
