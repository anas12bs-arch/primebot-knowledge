# JPM’s Bob Michele Says Entire Yield Curve Is Oversold

Bob Michele, global head of fixed income at JPMorgan Asset Management, expects the bond market to settle down after this week's US economic data and says, “if we see some stabilization here, we have our next buy level, we’ve got plenty of buying left to do.” He speaks on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
