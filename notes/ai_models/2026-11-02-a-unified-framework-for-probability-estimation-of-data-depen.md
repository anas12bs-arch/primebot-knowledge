# A Unified Framework for Probability Estimation of Data-Dependent Events

We study the general problem of estimating $D(\dde(S))$, the probability that an unknown distribution $D$ assigns to an event determined by a sample $S$ of $n$ i.i.d.\ draws from $D$ through a known mapping $\dde$. The estimation algorithm knows $\dde$ and $S$ but does not have access to any additional samples from $D$.
