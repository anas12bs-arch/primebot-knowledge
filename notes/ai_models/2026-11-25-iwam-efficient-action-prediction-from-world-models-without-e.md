# iWAM: Efficient Action Prediction from World Models without Explicit Future Prediction

World-action models (WAMs) build policies on pretrained video-generation models, on the premise that predicting how a scene evolves helps determine how to act. However, generating future scenes is expensive for real-time control. This motivates efficient proxies for future imagination, but how to train such proxies without sacrificing action-prediction quality remains unclear.
