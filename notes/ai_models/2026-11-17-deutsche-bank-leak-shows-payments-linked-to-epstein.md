# Deutsche Bank Leak Shows Payments Linked to Epstein

Deutsche Bank dropped after the Financial Times reported the firm had processed $582 million of suspicious payments linked to the late sex offender Jeffrey Epstein. Internal documents showed that the German bank filed so-called suspicious activity reports in the US in 2019 identifying that amount of money transfers related to Epstein, the FT said in the report on Tuesday, citing a leak of millions of Deutsche Bank documents. Bloomberg's Steven Arons reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
