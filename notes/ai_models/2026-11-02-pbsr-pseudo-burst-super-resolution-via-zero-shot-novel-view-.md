# PBSR: Pseudo-Burst Super-Resolution via Zero-Shot Novel View Synthesis

Burst super-resolution (BSR) achieves higher reconstruction fidelity than single-image super-resolution (SISR) by aggregating sub-pixel information from multiple frames. However, it requires a burst of frames captured under near-static conditions, and such frames are unavailable for single photographs or archival images.
