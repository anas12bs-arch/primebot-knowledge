# US Public Debt Climbs Above $40 Trillion for the First Time

Total US public debt stood at $40.05 trillion as of the close of business Tuesday, a Treasury Department release on Wednesday showed, surging by a third in less than five years. Tyler Kendall reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
