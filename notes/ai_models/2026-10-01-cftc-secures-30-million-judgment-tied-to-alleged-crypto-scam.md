# CFTC Secures $30 Million Judgment Tied to Alleged Crypto Scam

A federal judge in Florida ordered two individuals to pay more than $30 million in restitution and penalties for allegedly defrauding more than 9,000 people in a scam involving crytocurrencies and precious metals, the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission said in a statement Wednesdsay.
