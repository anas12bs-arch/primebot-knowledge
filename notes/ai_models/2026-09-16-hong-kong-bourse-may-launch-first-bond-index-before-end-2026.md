# Hong Kong Bourse May Launch First Bond Index Before End-2026

The Hong Kong stock exchange is considering launching its first-ever bond index before the end of the year, according to people familiar with the matter, as the Asian financial center bets that the benchmark would boost trading activity in the world’s largest offshore yuan market.
