# Finding Stability in Investing: Masters in Business with Seth Bernstein

Barry speaks with Seth Bernstein, Chief Executive Officer of AllianceBernstein. They discuss his tenure at JPMorgan Chase where he was managing director and global head of Managed Solutions & Strategy at J.P. Morgan Asset Management. They discuss understanding the Private Credit industry and the future of Mutual Funds as ETFs become the future of investing. Seth also discusses the importance of in-person collaboration as well as how to invest for retirement.  (Source: Bloomberg)
