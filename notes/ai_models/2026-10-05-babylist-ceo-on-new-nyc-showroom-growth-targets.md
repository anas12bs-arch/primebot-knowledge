# Babylist CEO on New NYC Showroom, Growth Targets

Babylist CEO Jennifer Hyman discusses the company’s new 20,000-square-foot showroom in Manhattan’s SoHo neighborhood, which opened at the end of September and is its largest retail location yet. Hyman says the space is designed to help expecting and new parents research major purchases with expert guidance: visitors can learn about products, try baby carriers and get help installing car seats. She speaks with Scarlet Fu on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
