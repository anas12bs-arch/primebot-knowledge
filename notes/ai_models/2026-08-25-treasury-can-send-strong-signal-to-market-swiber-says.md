# Treasury Can Send Strong Signal to Market, Swiber Says

Meghan Swiber, managing director of US rates strategy at BofA Securities, says the US Treasury can cut issuance at the back end of the yield curve, which she says would send a more impactful message to the market. Swiber also talks about what Fed Chair Kevin Warsh may say at Jackson Hole later this week. She speaks on "Bloomberg Surveillance." (Source: Bloomberg)
