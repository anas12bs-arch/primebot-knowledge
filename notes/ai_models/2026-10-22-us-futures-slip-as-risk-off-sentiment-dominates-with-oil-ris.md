# US Futures Slip as Risk-Off Sentiment Dominates With Oil Rising

Futures were under pressure as escalating concern about Mideast conflict pushed oil futures higher, spurring inflation fears with US midterm elections less than two weeks away. Investors were also parsing the latest batch of tech earnings in a bid to assess the prospects of the artificial-intelligence trade.
