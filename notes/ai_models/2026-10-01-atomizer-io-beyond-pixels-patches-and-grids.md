# Atomizer-IO: Beyond Pixels, Patches and Grids

Most vision architectures assume that observations lie on a regular grid, an effective abstraction for natural images but a restrictive one for sensing data whose channels, temporal sampling, spatial resolution, and geometry can vary. Generic set-based architectures remove the grid, but also remove useful spatial inductive biases.
