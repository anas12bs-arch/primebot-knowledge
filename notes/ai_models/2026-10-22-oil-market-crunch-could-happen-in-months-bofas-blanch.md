# Oil Market Crunch Could Happen in Months: BofA's Blanch

Francisco Blanch, Bank of America Commodities and Derivates Research Global Head, discusses energy markets and the volatile context around oil inventories as conflicts in the Middle East and Russia's invasion of Ukraine continue. "We've been living a little bit on borrowed time," Blanch tells Bloomberg Television. He adds crude oil could face a crunch point in "maybe four, five months."  (Source: Bloomberg)
