Text-to-image models excel at natural image synthesis but struggle with graphic design, where success depends on satisfying precise constraints on typography, layout, color, and visual communication. While prompt optimization offers an attractive alternative to expensive diffusion model fine-tuning, learning prompts for frozen image generators requires informative reward functions despite the entirely non-differentiable generation process.
