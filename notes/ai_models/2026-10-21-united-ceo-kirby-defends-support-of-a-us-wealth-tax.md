# United CEO Kirby Defends Support of a US Wealth Tax

United Airlines CEO Scott Kirby addressed his thoughts on a wealth tax in the United States, saying his view of a fair system would be some way of taxing wealth growth coupled with lower income taxes. He spoke on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
