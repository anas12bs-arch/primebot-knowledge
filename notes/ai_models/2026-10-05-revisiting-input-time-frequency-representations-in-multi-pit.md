# Revisiting Input Time-frequency Representations in Multi-pitch Estimation for Vocal Ensembles

Multi-pitch estimation in vocal ensembles is challenging because singers occupy overlapping pitch ranges and often sing at closely spaced fundamental frequencies, causing their harmonics to overlap in time-frequency representations.
