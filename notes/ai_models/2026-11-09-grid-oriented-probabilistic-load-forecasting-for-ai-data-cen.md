# Grid-Oriented Probabilistic Load Forecasting for AI Data Centers

Probabilistic load forecasts for AI data centers need to represent both uncertainty under changing workloads and temporal dependence to support grid operations. This paper develops an open, grid-oriented benchmark using measured GPU power from a 448-GPU AI cluster. Forecasts are evaluated through four stages: probabilistic prediction, online uncertainty calibration, temporal scenario generation, and horizon-wide reserve sizing.
