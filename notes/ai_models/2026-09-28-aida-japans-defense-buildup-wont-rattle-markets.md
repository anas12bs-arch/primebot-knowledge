# Aida: Japan’s Defense Buildup Won’t Rattle Markets

Takuji Aida, Chief Japan Economist at Credit Agricole and an economic advisor to Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, says any expansion of Japan’s defense spending will likely be spread over many years and shouldn’t become a source of significant market anxiety in any single year. He speaks with Shery Ahn on Bloomberg: The Asia Trade. (Source: Bloomberg)
