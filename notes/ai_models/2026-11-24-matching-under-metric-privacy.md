# Matching Under Metric Privacy

We study how to represent and randomize points in $d$-dimensional Euclidean space to obtain accurate bipartite matchings under metric privacy. Given $n$ private and $n$ public points, we analyze local mechanisms that randomize each private point or its distances to the public points. Using the public points' affine span, we construct a smaller representation that preserves all matching costs.
