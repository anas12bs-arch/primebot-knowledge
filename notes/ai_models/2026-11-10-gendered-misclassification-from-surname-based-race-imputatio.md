# Gendered Misclassification from Surname-Based Race Imputation

Researchers increasingly estimate political behavior based on race and gender from voter files, where race is imputed from surnames and geography. This only works where surnames suggest group membership, an assumption violated where surnames follow norms around marriage. Imputation is therefore biased against women who adopt their husband's surname, especially those who racially inter-marry, an outcome itself endogenous to partisanship.
