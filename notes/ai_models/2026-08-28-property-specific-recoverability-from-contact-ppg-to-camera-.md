# Property-Specific Recoverability from Contact PPG to Camera rPPG under Heterogeneous Observation Conditions

Camera-derived remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) is commonly validated through endpoint accuracy, but endpoint performance does not establish whether other physiological properties of source contact photoplethysmography (PPG) remain preserved recording by recording. We evaluated property-specific PPG-to-rPPG recoverability on 655 recordings from the Multi-Domain Mobile Video Physiology Dataset using CHROM as a fixed camera-rPPG observation pathway.
