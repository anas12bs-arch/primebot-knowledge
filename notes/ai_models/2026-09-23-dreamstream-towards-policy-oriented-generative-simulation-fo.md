# DreamStream: Towards Policy-Oriented Generative Simulation for End-to-End Driving

Faithfully evaluating end-to-end driving policies in simulation requires observations that are not merely photo-realistic, but preserve the scene features a policy relies on to make decisions. Existing platforms, however, exhibit a sim-to-real visual gap that corrupts policy perception, undermining their ability to assess a policy's closed-loop decision-making.
