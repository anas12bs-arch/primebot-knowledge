# Fed’s Bowman Sees No Urgency for Further Action on Rates

Federal Reserve Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman said that although inflation remains above the central bank’s goal, “I think we have some time to wait and see how financial conditions and the economy evolves.” She spoke Wednesday with Institute of International Finance CEO Tim Adams at the IIF Annual Membership Meeting in Bangkok. (Source: Bloomberg)
