# Oil Analyst Sankey Sees ‘Very Elevated’ Market Through End of 2027

Paul Sankey, president at Sankey Research, warns of oil market risk from tightness in US refining capacity as diesel reaches new record highs. Sankey discusses the effects of a potential US diesel export ban and the lingering impacts he sees for the global oil market through the end of this year and into 2027. He speaks on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
