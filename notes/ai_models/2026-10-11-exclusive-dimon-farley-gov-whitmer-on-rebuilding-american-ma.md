# Exclusive: Dimon, Farley, Gov. Whitmer on Rebuilding American Manufacturing

Ford, JPMorgan and the state of Michigan are teaming up to connect manufacturers and startups with capital, customers and government support as the US tries to rebuild supply chains that were moved overseas. Ford CEO Jim Farley says his company will commit $1 billion in purchasing to companies developing capabilities in areas including advanced manufacturing, magnets and rare earths, while JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon says the bank will lend capital through its broader security and resiliency
