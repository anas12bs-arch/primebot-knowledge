# Where to Hide From an AI Crash

JPMorgan’s Gabriela Santos says investors don’t have to abandon the AI boom to protect themselves from the next selloff. She explains why AI-driven volatility is likely to keep returning, and identifies Treasuries, gold, core real estate and European stocks as potential portfolio shock absorbers. (Source: Bloomberg)
