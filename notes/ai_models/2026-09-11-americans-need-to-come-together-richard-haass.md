# Americans Need To Come Together: Richard Haass

Richard Haass, senior counselor with Centerview Partners, president emeritus for the Council on Foreign Relations, and former advisor to Colin Powell and President George H.W. Bush, joins Scarlet Fu on "Bloomberg Money." Sentiment sank among consumers from both political parties, according to a UMich survey, and only 35% of Republicans believe the government is doing a good job with the economy, the lowest reading since President Trump returned to the White House last year.
