# Generalization behavior of OPTQ and the role of regularization

Large neural networks can be compressed by rounding or "quantizing" their weights to numbers that admit representations with fewer bits. One algorithm for quantization, OPTQ, progressively quantizes the weights of a neural network so that the squared quantization error on a specified calibration dataset is as small as possible.
