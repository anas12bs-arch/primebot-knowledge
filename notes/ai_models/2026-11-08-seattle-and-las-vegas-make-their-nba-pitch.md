# Seattle and Las Vegas Make Their NBA Pitch

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg senior sports reporter Randall Williams explains why the NBA’s potential expansion to Seattle and Las Vegas could generate billions of dollars while raising questions about the financial returns for existing owners. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Williams also examines the competition among prospective ownership groups, LIV Golf’s struggles and the labor battle threatening Major League Baseball’s next season.
