# Citi Says European Economic Data, Earnings Going in 'Right Direction'

Beata Manthey, European equity strategy head at Citi, says economic data, earnings revisions and government policy paint a positive picture for European firms. "We argue that actually policymakers are making good steps toward really protecting [the] European economy going forward," Manthey tells Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
