# Saidzoda-Tajik-E5-Large: Fine-Tuning a 560M Embedder for Tajik Retrieval

Finding the passage that answers a question is the first step of most practical systems built on documents, and for Tajik we found no openly described model for it. We present Saidzoda-Tajik-E5-Large, a Tajik text embedder built from multilingual-e5-large-instruct (560M parameters). We asked our own Tajik language model to write questions for passages from Tajik laws, tax documents, Wikipedia and web text, and trained on 235,140 question and passage pairs for one epoch, with hard negatives chose
