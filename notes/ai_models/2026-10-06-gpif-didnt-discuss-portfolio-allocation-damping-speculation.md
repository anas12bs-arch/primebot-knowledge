# GPIF Didn’t Discuss Portfolio Allocation, Damping Speculation

Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund didn’t discuss portfolio allocation at a meeting last month, damping speculation that the $2 trillion fund will respond to calls from Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s administration to increase its purchases of assets in Japan.
