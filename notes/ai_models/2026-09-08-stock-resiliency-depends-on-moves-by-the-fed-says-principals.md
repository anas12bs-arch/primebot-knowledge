# Stock Resiliency Depends on Moves by the Fed, Says Principal’s Shah

“The next couple of months are likely to be pretty rocky with only modest gains to be had for the rest of the year,” says Seema Shah, chief global strategist at Principal Asset Management. Shah says a possible series of hikes by the Federal Reserve can provide some resistance for equities. (Source: Bloomberg)
