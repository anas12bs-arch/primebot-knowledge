# DynMF2: Rigid Motion Factorization by Expectation-Maximization for Monocular 4D Reconstruction and Motion Segmentation

A single casual video leaves a moving scene under-determined: a point seen once from each place can slide toward or away from the camera and still match every frame. We constrain the reconstruction with the structure of the motion itself. Building on DynMF, we model a scene's motion as a handful of SE(3) trajectories, each rigid, and let every point follow one of them alone, never a blend. DynMF2 computes this factorization directly from 3D point trajectories, with no rendering, by Expectation-M
