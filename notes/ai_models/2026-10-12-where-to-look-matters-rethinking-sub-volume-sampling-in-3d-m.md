# Where to Look Matters: Rethinking Sub-Volume Sampling in 3D Medical Self-supervised Learning

Self-supervised learning on 3D medical images commonly relies on random sub-volume sampling during pretraining. However, random cropping implicitly treats all spatial regions as equally worth observing, despite anatomical priors and spatial redundancy that make regions inherently unequal in their value for representation learning.
