# Treasury Official Renews Warning on Wave of Tax-Saving Trades

A senior US Treasury official reiterated concerns Thursday about Wall Street’s tax-efficient investing boom, saying firms should win clients on the merits of their investments, not on how far they’ll go to minimize a tax bill.
