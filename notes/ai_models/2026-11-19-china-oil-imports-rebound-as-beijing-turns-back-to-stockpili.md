# China Oil Imports Rebound as Beijing Turns Back to Stockpiling

China’s appetite for crude is recovering faster than expected in the final months of 2026, as lower oil prices push cash-strapped refiners to use up government-granted import allowances and encourage processors to rebuild stockpiles depleted by months of war.
