# Commerzbank in 'Position of Strength' Ahead of UniCredit Deal, CEO Says

Commerzbank CEO Bettina Orlopp speaks on Bloomberg Television after the lender reported a better-than-expected 45% jump in profit, in one of the last quarterly results as an independent bank before UniCredit SpA takes control of the German rival. "I think we can act out of a position of strength," Orlopp says. (Source: Bloomberg)
