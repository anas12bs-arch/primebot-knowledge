# Inversion-Free High-Dimensional Counterfactual Inference

Counterfactual inference is the task of answering hypothetical "what-if" queries about a unit, given an observational dataset, modeling assumptions, and a causal graph. This enables a modeler to reason about hypothetical scenarios that cannot otherwise be answered without assumptions or a causal graph.
