# Reframe: Observation-Aligned Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Visual Reasoning

Agentic reinforcement learning (RL) and multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) offer complementary routes to post-training. For visual agents, however, tool use presents two related challenges. Models may overlook useful tool-returned observations, while poorly grounded actions can limit the evidence available for subsequent distillation.
