# Japan’s Only High Speed Trader Dharmacapital Moves to Singapore

Japan’s high frequency trader Dharmacapital Co. moved all its staff to Singapore earlier this month, according to people familiar with the matter, effectively leaving Tokyo with no such firms operating out of the city.
