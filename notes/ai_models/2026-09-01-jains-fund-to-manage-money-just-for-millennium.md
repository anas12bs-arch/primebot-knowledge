# Jain's Fund to Manage Money Just for Millennium

Bobby Jain's multistrategy hedge fund made about $1.8 billion in trading profits over two years while managing other clients' capital. Now it's going to manage money exclusively for Millennium Management. Bloomberg's Hema Parmar reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
