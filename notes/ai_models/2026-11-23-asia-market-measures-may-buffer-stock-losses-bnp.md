# Asia Market Measures May Buffer Stock Losses: BNP

Jason Lui, Head of APAC Equity Derivative Strategy at BNP Paribas, says market support measures in China, Taiwan and South Korea may provide a buffer against weak sentiment, but longer-term investment returns will still face long-term uncertainty from US data-center regulations and restrictions on remote access to chips. (Source: Bloomberg)
