# TWG Tea's Bouqdib on El Nino's Impact on Tea, Coffee

TWG Tea Co-Founder Taha Bouqdib examines how El Nino-driven weather disruptions are reshaping global tea and coffee markets, affecting crop yields, supply chains and pricing. He also highlights how TWG Tea is navigating market volatility, investing for long-term growth, and scaling its global footprint. He speaks with Haslinda Amin on "Insight with Haslinda Amin."
