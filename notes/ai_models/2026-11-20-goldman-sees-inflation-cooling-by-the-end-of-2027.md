# Goldman Sees Inflation Cooling by the end of 2027

Goldman Sachs expects inflation to cool to 2.4% by the end of 2027 as tariff, energy and AI-related pressures fade. But Goldman’s Chief US Economist David Mericle says the Fed may still raise rates in December and March, waiting for clearer evidence that inflation is easing. (Source: Bloomberg)
