# IAEA Director on Nuclear Material Left in Iran

IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi joins Bloomberg's David Bura at the UNGA to discuss the nuclear situation in Iran. He says there is still a fairly important amount of nuclear material in Iran and that the IAEA can get on the ground and assess pretty fast if there is the political will for it.
