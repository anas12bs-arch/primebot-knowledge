# Generative Tutorial: Towards Live Contextualized Visual Instructions for Physical Tasks

Visual instructions for physical tasks are typically authored in one context and followed in another, requiring users to translate demonstrated tools, materials, and spatial relationships into their own environment. We introduce Generative Tutorial, a conceptual framework for live visual instruction that depicts intended outcomes and actions within the user's environment and task flow. A formative evaluation of state-of-the-art image and video generation identifies failures and potential benefit
