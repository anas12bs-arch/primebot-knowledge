# Shapoorji Seeks Bond Payment Delay as Tata Listing Takes Shape

India’s Shapoorji Pallonji Group, the conglomerate behind the country’s biggest private credit deal, is in talks with lenders to one of its units to extend a bond payment due at the end of September, according to people familiar with the matter.
