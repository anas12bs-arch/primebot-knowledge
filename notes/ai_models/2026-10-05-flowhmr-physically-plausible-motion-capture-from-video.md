# FlowHMR: Physically Plausible Motion Capture from Video

We present FlowHMR, a framework for recovering physically plausible global 3D human motion from monocular video. Previous learning-based methods typically regress human motion directly from video and train the network with geometric supervision. However, recovering human motion from monocular video is inherently ambiguous in depth, and direct regression tends to collapse toward an averaged solution.
