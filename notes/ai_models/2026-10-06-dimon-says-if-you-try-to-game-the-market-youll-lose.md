# Dimon Says If You Try to Game the Market, You'll Lose

JPMorgan Chase & Co. Chief Executive Officer Jamie Dimon says investors should get used to the fact that markets go up and down. He says if you try to game the market, you are guaranteed to lose money. He speaks exclusively with Tom Mackenzie at JPMorgan’s Tech Stars Conference in London. (Source: Bloomberg)
