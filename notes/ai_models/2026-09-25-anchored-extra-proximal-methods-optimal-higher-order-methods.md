# Anchored Extra-Proximal Methods: Optimal Higher-Order Methods for Monotone Inclusion Problems

We study the deterministic oracle complexity of finding approximate solutions to composite monotone inclusion problems, formed by the sum of a smooth single-valued monotone operator and a maximally monotone set-valued operator, under the tangent-residual criterion.
