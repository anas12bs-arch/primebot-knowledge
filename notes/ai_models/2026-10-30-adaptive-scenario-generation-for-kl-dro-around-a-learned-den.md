# Adaptive Scenario Generation for KL-DRO Around a Learned Density Model

Distributionally robust optimization (DRO) hedges data-driven two-stage stochastic programs against error in the distribution estimated from a finite record, and standard ambiguity sets differ in how they treat realizations absent from that record.
