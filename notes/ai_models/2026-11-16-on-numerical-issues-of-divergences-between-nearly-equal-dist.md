# On numerical issues of divergences between nearly equal distributions

Barron et al. (2026) recently identified numerical issues in the softmax and soft cross-entropy loss. We show that similar issues are present in standard divergence operations between categorical distributions. This includes the forward and reverse Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence and generalized Jensen-Shannon divergence (JSD), as well as Monte Carlo KL estimates and entropy. When the distributions are nearly equal, rounding and cancellation issues affect the forward and gradient computations.
