# High-Fidelity Image Matting with Pixel-Space Diffusion Transformers

Diffusion models have shown strong potential for image matting by transferring generative priors learned from large-scale pretraining. However, existing approaches largely rely on latent diffusion models, whose compressed representations and VAE-based reconstruction introduce a mismatch with the high-frequency precision required for matting.
