# AdaptivePnP: Fast Auto-Tuned Plug-and-Play for Deconvolution

Plug-and-play (PnP) methods reconstruct images by alternating a data-fitting step with an image denoiser. Their performance depends on two parameters: the step size $γ$ and the measurement noise level $σ$. In practice, $γ$ is often tuned by grid search over validation images and must be retuned whenever the problem setting changes, and $σ$ is assumed known or estimated separately.
