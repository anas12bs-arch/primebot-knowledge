# Sinology’s Rothman: China, US Seek Stability in Ties

Andy Rothman, former US diplomat and founder of advisory firm Sinology, says China and the US are willing to maintain stability in bilateral ties. He also urges investors to focus on China’s consumption story. He speaks at CITIC Securities’ International Investors’ Forum in Hong Kong.
