# Video-Conditioned Generative Joint 2D-3D Hand Motion Recovery

Recovering faithful 3D hand motion from video remains challenging due to frequent occlusions and incomplete visual observations, which make frame-wise pose estimates unreliable and temporally inconsistent. To address this problem, we propose JoHan, a unified generative framework that recovers hand motion directly from video sequences without relying on intermediate per-frame pose predictions.
