# Porsche CEO With a First Look at Mission S Sports Car

At a world premiere in Queens, Porsche CEO Michael Leiters told Bloomberg’s Matt Miller that the company’s new sports car will launch a distinct model line positioned well above the 911. The mid-engine platform is designed to support several variants and is flexible enough for either hybrid or combustion-only powertrains. The two speak on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
