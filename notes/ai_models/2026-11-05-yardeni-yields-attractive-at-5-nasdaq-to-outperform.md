# Yardeni: Yields Attractive at 5%; Nasdaq to Outperform

Yardeni Research President, Ed Yardeni, says US 10-year Treasury yields look “very attractive” at around the 5% level with inflation expected to cool. On Bloomberg's Insight with Haslinda Amin, he also told us why the Nasdaq 100 is his preferred asset for the next 24 months. (Source: Bloomberg)
