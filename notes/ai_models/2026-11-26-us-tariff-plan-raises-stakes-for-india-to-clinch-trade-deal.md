# US Tariff Plan Raises Stakes for India to Clinch Trade Deal

The US plans to impose a 17% tariff on Indian goods, lower than regional peers like Vietnam, Malaysia and Bangladesh. The rate would be hiked to 25% if the country fails to secure a deal, according to a statement from the US Trade Representative. This puts pressure on India to agree to a trade deal or face a higher levy, leaving exporters and investors with little clarity on the trade outlook.  (Source: Bloomberg)
