# China's Gree Eyes Europe Winters for Growth

Gree Electric Appliances chairwoman Dong Mingzhu told Bloomberg TV's Avril Hong in a exclusive interview that Europe's cold winters represent the company's next growth opportunity, following record summer heat that nearly doubled air-conditioning revenue in the region. She also discussed Gree's European expansion, energy storage business, and AI's impact on its workforce. (Source: Bloomberg)
