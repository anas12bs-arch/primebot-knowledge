# JPMorgan Dangles 11% Yield on Volta’s $5 Billion AI Loan Sale

JPMorgan Chase &amp; Co. is pitching a yield of about 11% on a $5 billion leveraged-loan sale on behalf of Volta Infrastructure Holdings Ltd., one of the highest borrowing costs seen in the market for risky debt that’s become popular for financing the AI boom.
