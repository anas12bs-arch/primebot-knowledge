# Warsh's Pushback On Guidance Adds Volatility: Lyngen

Ian Lyngen, Head of US Rates Strategy at BMO Capital Markets, and Stephanie Roth, Chief Economist at Wolfe Research, join Scarlet Fu on "Bloomberg Real Yield." US Treasuries fell a day after the Trump administration’s surprise decision to increase buybacks of longer-dated bonds, showing the move did little to counter angst about the surging government debt that pushed some yields to a 19-year high.
