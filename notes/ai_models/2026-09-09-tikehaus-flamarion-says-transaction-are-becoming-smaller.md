# Tikehau's Flamarion Says Transaction Are Becoming 'Smaller'

Tikehau Capital co-founder Antoine Flamarion says there are now "less transactions" in the private equity sphere. He adds that secondary markets are "a massive trend." He speaks with Bloomberg's Guy Johnson from the sidelines of the IPEM Global event in Paris. (Source: Bloomberg)
