# Linking Rapid Learning to Compositional Structure in Neural Networks: Evidence from Additive Analogies in Meta-Learned Parameter Spaces

Humans can learn rapidly from limited experience, while neural networks typically require extensive training. In this work, we investigate what properties of a neural network can enable it to learn rapidly. We study neural networks that have been optimized via meta-learning, a technique that exposes a network to many related functions to facilitate its subsequent learning of similar functions.
