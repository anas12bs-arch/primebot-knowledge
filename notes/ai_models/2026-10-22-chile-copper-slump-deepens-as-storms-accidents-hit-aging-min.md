# Chile Copper Slump Deepens as Storms, Accidents Hit Aging Mines

Chile’s copper production will likely fall 8% to 9% this year, Finance Minister Jorge Quiroz said, worsening his outlook for a slump in the top-producing nation amid a string of deadly accidents and extreme weather.
