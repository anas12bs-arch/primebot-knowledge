# Kennerly: Ultra Wealthy See Long-Term Mideast Gains

Despite heightened uncertainty across the region, a new report from Savills finds that Abu Dhabi and Dubai continue to rank among the world's leading wealth destinations. The report evaluates more than 100 global locations on how well-placed they are to attract the next generation of wealth. Rachael Kennerley, Director of Research at Savills Middle East spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on Horizons Middle East & Africa. (Source: Bloomberg)
