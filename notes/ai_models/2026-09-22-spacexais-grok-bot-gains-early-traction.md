# SpaceXAI’s Grok Bot Gains Early Traction

SpaceXAI’s Grok Bot has topped 400,000 weekly users roughly a month after its launch, offering an early look at demand for Elon Musk’s push into AI agents. The product, which is separate from the flagship Grok chatbot, is designed to operate more like an always-on employee as SpaceXAI competes with OpenAI and Anthropic in the growing market for AI agents. Bloomberg Tech's Ed Ludlow reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
