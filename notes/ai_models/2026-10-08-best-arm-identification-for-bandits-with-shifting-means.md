# Best Arm Identification for Bandits with Shifting Means

We study the best arm identification problem in a stochastic environment with a novel form of adversarial perturbations, which we coin Shifting Means. While classically the mean rewards of the $K$ arms are stable in time, in Shifting Means only the gaps $\boldsymbolΔ$ between mean rewards are stable, while their common shift may be determined adversarially in each round.
