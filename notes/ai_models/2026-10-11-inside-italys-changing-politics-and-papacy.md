# Inside Italy's Changing Politics and Papacy

New York Times Correspondent and Former Rome Bureau Chief Jason Horowitz tells Bloomberg This Weekend how his 25 years reporting from Italy gave him a firsthand view of the Vatican, papal transitions and the country's evolving political landscape. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Horowitz discusses his new memoir, The Italian Job, and how Italy's political experiments, from Silvio Berlusconi to Giorgia Meloni, offer insights into broader global trends.
