# Australia’s Home Prices Fall Further Ahead of Expected Rate Hike

Australia’s home prices tumbled for a seventh straight month and are on track for the biggest fall in 40 years, a private gauge showed, as the Reserve Bank is expected to increase borrowing costs again this week.
