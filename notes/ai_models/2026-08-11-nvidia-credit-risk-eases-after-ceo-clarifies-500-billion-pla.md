# Nvidia Credit Risk Eases After CEO Clarifies $500 Billion Plan

Bond traders dialed back measures of credit risk associated with Nvidia Corp. on Tuesday after the company said it would limit its exposure in a $500 billion plan to finance the type of artificial-intelligence investments that are driving demand for its computer chips.
