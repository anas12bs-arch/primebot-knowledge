# Trump Issues Threat to Oman as US-Iran MOU Expires

President Donald Trump threatened to bomb Oman if it gets in the way of developments with Iran, according to reports from Fox News. A 60-day truce between the US and Iran— already repeatedly violated — will expire on Monday without a deal on how the Stair of Hormuz should be managed. Tyler Kendall reports on Bloomberg Television.
