# Anthropic IPO Explained: Nasdaq Listing and Key Risks

In late 2020, a group of senior OpenAI employees unhappy with what they saw as the company’s failure to adequately prioritize AI ethics and safety left the firm to form Anthropic PBC. Just a few years later, the younger company has become an artificial intelligence behemoth that is expected to hold one of the largest initial public offerings in history.
