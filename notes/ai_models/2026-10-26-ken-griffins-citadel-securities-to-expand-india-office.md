# Ken Griffin’s Citadel Securities to Expand India Office

Billionaire Ken Griffin’s Citadel Securities LLC is moving into a larger India office early next year, according to a filing and a person familiar with the matter, as it expands in one of the world’s fastest-growing financial markets.
