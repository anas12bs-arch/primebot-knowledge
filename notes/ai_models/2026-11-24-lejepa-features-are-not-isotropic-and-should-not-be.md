# LeJEPA Features Are Not Isotropic, and Should Not Be

The SIGReg term in LeJEPA regularizes features to have an isotropic Gaussian distribution. Our first result is to show that this regularizer, in practice, cannot achieve its goal. We design a modified regularizer to enforce the objective better. This leads to slightly improved performance. For example, on ImageNet-100, our modified regularizer reaches the final accuracy of SIGReg in 1.3$\times$ fewer epochs.
