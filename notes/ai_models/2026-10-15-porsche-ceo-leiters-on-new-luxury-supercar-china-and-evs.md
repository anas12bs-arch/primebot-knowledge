# Porsche CEO Leiters on New Luxury Supercar, China and EVs

Porsche AG unveiled a concept previewing a new supercar, the Mission S, that will be positioned above the 911 in its lineup. Speaking with Matt Miller on "Bloomberg The Close," Porsche CEO Michael Leiters discusses the new supercar and also comments on the state of the China market, the future of electric vehicles and the German automaker's storied legacy.
