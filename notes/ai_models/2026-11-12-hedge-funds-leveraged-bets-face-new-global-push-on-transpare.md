# Hedge Funds’ Leveraged Bets Face New Global Push on Transparency

The Financial Stability Board is preparing to launch a consultation on how to gather data on hedge funds’ leveraged bond bets, after early efforts to foster transparency were frustrated by different definitions and restrictions on the cross-border flow of information.
