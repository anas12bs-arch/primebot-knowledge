# 2026 Best Cities for Work Revealed

Indeed Hiring Lab measured 40 metrics across six pillars against hundreds of US metro-areas to determine which cities are the best for workers in 2026. Laura Ullrich, Director of Economic Research at Indeed Hiring Lab, explains that its not about where company's are headquartered or where its best to run a company, but where Indeed is seeing the most career dynamism and opportunity -- so the top cities for workers may surprise you.
