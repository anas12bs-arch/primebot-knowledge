# Soccer Star Mbappé Leaves Nike to Sign With On

French soccer superstar Kylian Mbappé is leaving Nike to sign with Swiss sneaker company On. The 27-year-old striker is the World Cup's all-time goal leader. It's said to be a 10 year deal. On shares rose with the news. Randall Williams reports.  (Source: Bloomberg)
