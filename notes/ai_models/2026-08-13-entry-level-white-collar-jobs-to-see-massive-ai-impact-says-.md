# Entry-Level, White-Collar Jobs to See 'Massive' AI Impact, Says Virginie Maisonneuve

The number of employers who expect entry-level workers to be AI-competent has nearly tripled since last fall, according to the National Association of Colleges and Employers. 
Workers’ anxiety about AI has surged, along with demand for quick training programs.
Virginie Maisonneuve, Founder at Maisonneuve Global Advisors, discusses inflation, rates, and the dual impact of AI on prices and the labor market. (Source: Bloomberg)
