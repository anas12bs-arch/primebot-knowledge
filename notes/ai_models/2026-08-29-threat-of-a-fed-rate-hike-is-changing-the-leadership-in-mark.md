# Threat of a Fed Rate Hike Is Changing the Leadership in Markets, Slimmon Says

Andrew Slimmon, managing director and head of applied equity advisors at Morgan Stanley, says he's happy with what Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh said during his speech at Jackson Hole and he remains consistent. Slimmon says the threat of an interest rate hike is causing a change in leadership in stock markets. He shares more on "Bloomberg The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
