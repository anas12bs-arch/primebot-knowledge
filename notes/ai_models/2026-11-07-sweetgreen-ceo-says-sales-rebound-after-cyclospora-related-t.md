# Sweetgreen CEO Says Sales Rebound After Cyclospora-Related Traffic Drop

Sweetgreen CEO and Co-Founder Jonathan Neman says that sales momentum has improved after a summer Cyclospora outbreak—unrelated to Sweetgreen’s stores but damaging to traffic across the sector. The company reported October comparable sales growth of about 5%, following a return to flat comps in September, and saw further improvement in early November. He speaks with Romaine Bostick & Kailey Leinz on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
