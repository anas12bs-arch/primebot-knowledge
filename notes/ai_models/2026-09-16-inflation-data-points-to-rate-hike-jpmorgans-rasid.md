# Inflation Data Points to Rate Hike: JPMorgan's Rasid

JPMorgan Global Market Strategist Raisah Rasid tells Paul Allen that incoming economic data and rising Treasury yields point to a Fed rate hike. She urges investors to focus on the 12- to 18-month rate trajectory and the central bank's long-term inflation outlook. (Source: Bloomberg)
