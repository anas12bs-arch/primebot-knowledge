# Jio IPO Lifts Angel One Past Zerodha as India’s No. 2 Broker

Jio Platforms Ltd.’s record $3.1 billion initial public offering helped Angel One Ltd. overtake Zerodha Broking Ltd. to become India’s second-largest brokerage by the number of active investors on the National Stock Exchange of India.
