# Treasury Buybacks Won't Fix US Yields, Strategist Says

Lombard Odier Senior Macro Strategist Homin Lee argues that expanded Treasury buybacks won't solve underlying US yield pressures driven by large deficits. While the plan may offer short-term relief, he emphasizes that broader macroeconomic factors will continue to dictate yields. (Source: Bloomberg)
