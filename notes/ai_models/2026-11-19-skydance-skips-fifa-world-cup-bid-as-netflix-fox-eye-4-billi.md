# Skydance Skips FIFA World Cup Bid as Netflix, Fox Eye $4 Billion Deal

Skydance Corp. and Walt Disney Co.’s ESPN have declined to bid for the US media rights for the next FIFA Men’s World Cup tournament in what could be one of the costliest and competitive auctions in the global soccer event’s history, according to people familiar with the matter.
