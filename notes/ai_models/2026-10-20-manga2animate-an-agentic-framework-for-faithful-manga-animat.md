# Manga2Animate: An Agentic Framework for Faithful Manga Animation with Script and Storyboard Planning

Adapting existing manga into coherent animation that stays faithful to the source is a valuable yet challenging task. Existing methods either access the manga mainly through text, losing its characters and art style, or feed manga pages directly into video generation models, which misinterpret the manga's structure and produce incorrect storylines.
