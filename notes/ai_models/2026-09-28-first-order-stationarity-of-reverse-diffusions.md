# First-Order Stationarity of Reverse Diffusions

Recent literature has shown a strong connection between optimization and sampling. We develop the corresponding first-order theory for diffusion models. First, the SDE-based reverse-time flows of overdamped and underdamped Langevin diffusions contract relative Fisher divergences at explicit exponential rates whenever the stationary potential of the forward process is strongly convex---a condition on the noising process one chooses, not on the data.
