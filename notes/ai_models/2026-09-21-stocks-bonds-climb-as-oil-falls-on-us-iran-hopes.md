# Stocks, Bonds Climb as Oil Falls on US-Iran Hopes

Stocks and bonds kicked off the week on a positive note as hopes for diplomatic efforts to end the war in Iran drove oil prices lower, easing inflation anxiety.
The advance in equities erased the S&amp;P 500’s decline for the month, with a renewed rally in chipmakers also buoying sentiment.
Omar Aguilar, CEO and CIO of Schwab Asset Management, joins for a broad look across asset classes for the rest of 2026. (Source: Bloomberg)
