# China Stocks Enter Bear Market as Tech Slumps, Policy Hopes Ebb

Chinese stocks entered a bear market, with $2.4 trillion lost since a peak in May, as a protracted slump in consumption and mounting doubts over the profitability of artificial intelligence companies turn them into one of the world’s worst performers.
