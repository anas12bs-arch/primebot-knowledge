# Hydro-Quebec Forced to Import More Power After Deals With US Utilities

Hydro-Quebec, a Canadian renewable-energy powerhouse with major US export contracts, is stretching an investment plan worth C$200 billion ($143 billion) by six years to 2041. Until then, it anticipates potentially being a net importer of electricity.
