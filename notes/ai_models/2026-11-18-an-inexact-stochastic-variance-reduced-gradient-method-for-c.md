# An inexact stochastic variance-reduced gradient method for constrained convex finite-sum optimization

In this paper, we study a class of constrained convex finite-sum optimization problems. In many practical applications, all or some of the constraints are hard constraints that represent imperative requirements. Consequently, any desirable approximate solution must (nearly) satisfy these constraints.
