# Wealthy Clients Want 'Shiny Star' Investments, Says Rick Pitcairn

Rick Pitcairn, chairman and chief global strategist at family office Pitcairn, said its his job to tap the breaks on wealthy clients who want to put too much of their money in 'shiny star' investments like SpaceX or private markets. Pitcairn said that there is a role for private credit and private markets, but it should be inside a diverse portfolio.  (Source: Bloomberg)
