# EEG Decoding in Context: A Framework for Tuning-Free EEG Foundation Models

Electroencephalography (EEG) decoding is a central route toward brain-computer interfaces for communication, rehabilitation, cognitive assessment, and neurological monitoring. Recent EEG foundation models have substantially advanced reusable signal representations, but their downstream use still typically requires a task-specific decoder, gradient-based adaptation, and validation-driven model selection.
