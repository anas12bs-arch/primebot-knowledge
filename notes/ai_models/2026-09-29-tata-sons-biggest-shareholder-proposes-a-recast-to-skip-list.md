# Tata Sons Biggest Shareholder Proposes a Recast to Skip Listing

Tata Trusts, the majority shareholder of Tata Sons Pvt., proposed that the holding company of India’s biggest conglomerate merge with two of its unlisted units to stave off a listing order.
