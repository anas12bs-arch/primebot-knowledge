# Arizona Senator Mark Kelly to Introduce New AI Workforce Bill

Democrat Senator from Arizona Mark Kelly discussed his plans to unveil a new bill aimed at mitigating AI impact on the workforce and said that it is important to generate revenue from AI companies to 'give people options.' Kelly also weighed in on President Trump's summit with China's President Xi and said he does not expect the president to have a serious negotiation on AI with his Chinese counterpart.
