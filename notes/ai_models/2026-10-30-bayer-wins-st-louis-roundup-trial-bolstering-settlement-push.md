# Bayer Wins St. Louis Roundup Trial, Bolstering Settlement Push

A jury in St. Louis rejected claims by three women who blamed Bayer AG’s Roundup weedkiller for their cancers, bolstering the company’s effort to contain the cost of tens of thousands of similar lawsuits in the US that it has offered to settle.
