# Crypto Firm Coinbase Dropped as Member of UK Finance Lobby Group

US-based cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase Global Inc. has been removed as a member of UK Finance, one of Britain’s most influential financial services lobby groups, even as the digital-asset sector takes a more prominent role in policy debates.
