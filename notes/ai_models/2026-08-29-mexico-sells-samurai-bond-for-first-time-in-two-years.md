# Mexico Sells Samurai Bond for First Time in Two Years

Mexico returned to the Samurai bond market for the first time in two years on Friday, raising ¥282.8 billion ($1.77 billion) amid a rush of issuance as borrowers seek to lock in funding before Japanese interest rates potentially climb further.
