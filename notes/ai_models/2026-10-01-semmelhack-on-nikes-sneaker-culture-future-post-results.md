# Semmelhack on Nike’s Sneaker-Culture Future Post-Results

Elizabeth Semmelhack says Nike’s legacy in sneakers like Jordans, Dunks and Air Force Ones still carries major cultural weight. She noted that the brand helped define sneaker culture for decades and that its presence remains visible across American cities. But she argued the market is now far more crowded than in the 1970s, 1980s and 1990s, with many more brands competing for the same cultural attention. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close."
