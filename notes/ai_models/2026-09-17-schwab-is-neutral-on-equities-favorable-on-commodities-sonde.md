# Schwab Is Neutral on Equities, Favorable on Commodities, Sonders Says

Liz Ann Sonders, Charles Schwab chief investment strategist, says there is no one asset allocation that makes sense for every investor. She says the firm is neutral on equities, less favorable on fixed income, and more favorable on commodities.  (Source: Bloomberg)
