# TRACE: Topological Representations for Anomaly detection through Contrastive Embeddings

Intrusion detectors must recognize evolving malicious behavior even when confirmed attack labels are scarce or incomplete. We present TRACE, a topology-aware graph contrastive learning framework for label-efficient intrusion detection. TRACE learns from benign system-activity graphs by combining message-passing and topological representations, then assigns anomaly scores in the same projected embedding space optimized by the contrastive objective.
