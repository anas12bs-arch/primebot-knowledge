# Italy Extends Diesel Tax Cuts to Shield Voters from Price Hikes

Italy will extend a tax cut on diesel fuel prices until Sept. 10, as Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni’s government continues to shield consumers ahead of a general election expected next year, according to an Italian official.
