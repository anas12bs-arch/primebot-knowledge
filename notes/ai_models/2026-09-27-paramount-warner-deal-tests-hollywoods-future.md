# Paramount Warner Deal Tests Hollywood’s Future

Bloomberg entertainment reporter Lucas Shaw is on Bloomberg This Weekend examining Paramount Skydance’s acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery and the challenge of turning two legacy media companies into a growing business as cable declines and streaming growth slows. Speaking with Lisa Mateo, Shaw says the merger could reshape Hollywood while raising questions about debt, further consolidation and whether streaming companies such as Netflix need new sources of growth.
