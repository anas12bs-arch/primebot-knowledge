# Billionaire Raises $720 Million Private Credit for Megha Buyout

Indian billionaire P.V. Krishna Reddy has secured about 67 billion rupees ($720 million) from global private credit investors to buy out his uncle’s stake in Megha Engineering &amp; Infrastructures Ltd., according to people familiar with the matter, in a layered transaction that is one of the country’s biggest deals this year.
