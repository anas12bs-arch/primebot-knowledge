# UK Debt Chief Sees Volatility Driving Demand for Short Gilts

The UK Debt Management Office is pulling back further from long-dated gilts to meet investor demand for shorter tenors that are less exposed to swings in the bond market, Chief Executive Jessica Pulay said.
