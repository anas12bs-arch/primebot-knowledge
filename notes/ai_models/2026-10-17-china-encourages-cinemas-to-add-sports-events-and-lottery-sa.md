# China Encourages Cinemas to Add Sports Events and Lottery Sales

China is encouraging cinemas to screen major sporting events, host sports activities and sell sports lottery tickets as part of a drive to link moviegoing with sports consumption, according to a notice from the National Film Administration and General Administration of Sport.
