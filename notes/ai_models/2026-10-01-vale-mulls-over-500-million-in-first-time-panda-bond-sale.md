# Vale Mulls Over $500 Million in First Time Panda Bond Sale

Brazilian iron ore giant Vale SA is considering to raise as much as 3.5 billion yuan ($522 million) in its debut issuance in China’s bond market, according to people familiar with the matter, joining a growing number of issuers diversifying funding sources and looking for cheaper options.
