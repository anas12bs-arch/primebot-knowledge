# Why Qatar Airlines Gets Cheaper Jet Fuel Outside of the Middle East

Why does an airline like Qatar Airways need to source jet fuel outside of its own oil-rich region?

David Kang, former group treasurer at Qatar Airways, joins Tracy Alloway and Joe Weisenthal on the Odd Lots podcast to discuss why the company often gets a better deal on jet fuel by looking outside of its own national petroleum provider. (Source: Bloomberg)
