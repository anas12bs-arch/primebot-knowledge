# India’s SEBI Seeks Major Changes to Unpopular Auction System

India’s markets regulator proposed sweeping changes to the closing auction system, including a possible return to the previous method for settling derivatives on expiry days, after sharp price swings and liquidity concerns emerged around the month-old mechanism.
