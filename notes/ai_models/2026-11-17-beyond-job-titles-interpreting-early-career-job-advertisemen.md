# Beyond Job Titles: Interpreting Early-Career Job Advertisements with Sparse Autoencoders and Semantic Clustering

Job titles and job descriptions alone provide limited insight into how technical roles differ in a changing job market. To assess how such roles differ in their work and industry applications, we design and compare two methods based on deep learning and transformer representations using 36,081 early-career job postings collected from LinkedIn in the United States between December 2025 and October 2026.
