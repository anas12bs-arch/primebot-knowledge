# Strategically Diverse Sampling for Self-Training

Many LLM training and inference methods, including RL and test-time scaling, depend on repeated sampling, but benefit only when the responses meaningfully differ. Self-training faces the same challenge: training data is typically constructed by sampling IID responses and filtering primarily for correctness, thereby overrepresenting strategies a model already favours.
