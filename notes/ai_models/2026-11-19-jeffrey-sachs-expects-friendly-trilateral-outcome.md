# Jeffrey Sachs Expects Friendly Trilateral Outcome

Columbia University Professor Jeffrey Sachs says he is happy that the leaders of US-China and Russia have agreed to have a trilateral meeting. Speaking to Bloomberg’s Stephen Engle, he adds that his expectations are that the outcome will be ‘calm’ and ‘friendly’. (Source: Bloomberg)
