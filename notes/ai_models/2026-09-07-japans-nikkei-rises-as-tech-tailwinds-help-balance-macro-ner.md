# Japan’s Nikkei Rises as Tech Tailwinds Help Balance Macro Nerves

Japan’s Nikkei 225 climbed as chip stocks followed their US peers higher after Friday’s rally. Gains on the broader Topix were smaller as concerns about the yen and bond yields limited risk appetite.
