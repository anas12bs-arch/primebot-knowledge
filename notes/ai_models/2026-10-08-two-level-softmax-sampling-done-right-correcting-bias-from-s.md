# Two-Level Softmax Sampling Done Right: Correcting Bias from Size Imbalance and Dispersion

Two-level softmax (2LS) sampling is a popular alternative enabling sublinear-time sampling. Assuming items are partitioned into clusters, 2LS first samples a cluster and then an item within it.
