# ModHarness-RL: Recasting Harnesses as Learnable and Evolving Programs for Agentic RL

Agentic reinforcement learning (RL) increasingly relies on a harness, the scaffold around the model that decides what it sees of its own interaction and what guidance it receives. Most existing approaches train under a hand-written harness that stays fixed throughout. However, an effective harness depends on the domain, the data and the model, and keeps changing as the model trains.
