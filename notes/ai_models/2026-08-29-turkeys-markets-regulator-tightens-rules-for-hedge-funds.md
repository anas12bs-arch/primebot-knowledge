# Turkey’s Markets Regulator Tightens Rules for Hedge Funds

Turkey’s markets regulator announced new rules limiting hedge funds’ exposure to related parties and capping concentrated investments, in a bid to address concerns about abnormal returns and repeated warnings from global index providers.
