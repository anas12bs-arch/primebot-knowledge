# Rise in Real Yields Has Further to Run, Nuveen Says

The rise in real yields, which is outpacing that of nominal and breakeven rates, has "a little bit more room to run," says Laura Cooper, global investment strategist at Nuveen. "The key catalyst going forward will be that August inflation print," Cooper tells Bloomberg Television. "We're going to be driven more by data in the next coming weeks." (Source: Bloomberg)
