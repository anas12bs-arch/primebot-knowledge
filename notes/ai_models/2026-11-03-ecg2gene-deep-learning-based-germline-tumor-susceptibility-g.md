# ECG2Gene: Deep Learning-Based Germline Tumor Susceptibility Gene Risk Assessment Using Electrocardiograms

Electrocardiograms (ECGs) may contain physiological information related to hereditary cancer susceptibility, but its relationship with future cancer risk remains unclear. Here, we present ECG2Gene, a two-stage deep-learning framework that derives digital phenotypes related to germline tumor susceptibility from routine 12-lead ECGs and predicts future cancer risk.
