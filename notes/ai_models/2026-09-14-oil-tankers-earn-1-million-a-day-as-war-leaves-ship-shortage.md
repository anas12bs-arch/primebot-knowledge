# Oil Tankers Earn $1 Million a Day as War Leaves Ship Shortage

The cost of hiring an oil tanker on the industry’s benchmark trade route topped $1 million a day for the first time, as the Iran war leaves too few ships willing to cross the Strait of Hormuz to collect cargoes.
