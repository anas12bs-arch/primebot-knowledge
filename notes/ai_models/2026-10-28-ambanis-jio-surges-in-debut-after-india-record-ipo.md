# Ambani’s Jio Surges in Debut After India-Record IPO

Jio Platforms Ltd. surged in its trading debut on Wednesday after billionaire Mukesh Ambani’s telecom giant raised a record $3.1 billion in India’s biggest initial public offering, defying a poor track record for the country’s mega deals. (Source: Bloomberg)
