# Beyond Scarcity: When Encoder Adaptation Is Worthwhile

Long-tailed recognition often learns a shared representation before correcting the classifier for class imbalance. Once the classifier has been rebalanced, however, final accuracy does not reveal whether further encoder adaptation remains worthwhile. Standard imbalance benchmarks obscure this question because they jointly vary the number of distinct examples available to rare classes and the frequency with which those examples shape the representation.
