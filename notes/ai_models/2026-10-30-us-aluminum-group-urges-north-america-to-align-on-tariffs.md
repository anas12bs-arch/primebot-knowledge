# US Aluminum Group Urges North America to Align on Tariffs

The US aluminum industry’s main trade group is pushing for Canada and Mexico to match Washington’s tariffs on imports of the metal from outside North America, while declining to take a position on whether President Donald Trump’s current 50% levy should be lowered.
