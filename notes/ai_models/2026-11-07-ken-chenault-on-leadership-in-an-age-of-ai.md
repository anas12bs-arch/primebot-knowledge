# Ken Chenault on Leadership in an Age of AI

On Bloomberg This Weekend, General Catalyst Chairman and Managing Director and former American Express CEO Ken Chenault discusses leadership, diversity and responsible AI, arguing that executives need the courage to stand by their values even as political and technological pressures intensify. Chenault tells host David Gura that companies should pursue innovation while protecting safety, broadening opportunity and ensuring AI ultimately improves people's lives. (Source: Bloomberg)
