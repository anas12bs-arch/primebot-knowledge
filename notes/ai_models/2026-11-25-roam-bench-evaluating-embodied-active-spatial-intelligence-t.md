# ROAM-Bench: Evaluating Embodied Active Spatial Intelligence through Evidence Verification

Embodied spatial intelligence requires agents to gather information through exploration and interaction, then use it to reason and act. Existing benchmarks insufficiently distinguish correct outcomes from completion of these task requirements. We introduce ROAM-Bench to evaluate these abilities under robot motion and collision constraints. Independent checks use simulation state and episode records to assess outcomes and task requirements, with stronger checks of geometry, coverage, or placement
