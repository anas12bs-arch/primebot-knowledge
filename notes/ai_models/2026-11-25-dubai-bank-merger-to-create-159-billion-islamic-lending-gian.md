# Dubai Bank Merger to Create $159 Billion Islamic Lending Giant

Dubai Islamic Bank PJSC said it’s buying Emirates Islamic Bank PJSC in a deal that will create one of the world’s largest listed Islamic-banking groups, with assets of about $159 billion.
