# Sale of TAP Airline Moves on With Bids by Lufthansa, Air France

Portugal will move to the final stage of the sale of a minority stake in TAP SA after state holding company Parpública submitted its assessment of binding offers from Deutsche Lufthansa AG and Air France-KLM.
