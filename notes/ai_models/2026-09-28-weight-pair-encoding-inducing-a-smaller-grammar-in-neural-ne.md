# Weight Pair Encoding: Inducing a Smaller Grammar in Neural Network Weights

We show that neural network weights can be explicilty fintuned to admit a smaller grammar. Weight Pair Encoding (WeightPE) does so by placing a lossy Re-Pair compressor inside a straight-through estimator. The int8 weights of the network are flattened into one string, and near-matching Re-Pair patterns are made exactly equal within a global L2 budget.
