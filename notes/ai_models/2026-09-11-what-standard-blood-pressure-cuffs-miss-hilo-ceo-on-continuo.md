# What Standard Blood Pressure Cuffs Miss. Hilo CEO on Continuous Monitoring

The CEO of Swiss health-tech company Hilo says continuous blood pressure monitoring can reveal patterns that traditional cuff readings may miss. Stefan Petzinger points to night-time changes as particularly important and says Hilo’s software is designed to turn sensor data into useful blood pressure insights. Petzinger also says greater engagement with the data can encourage healthier behavior and better outcomes. He spoke to Tom Mackenzie on the latest episode of "Bloomberg Tech: Europe." (Sour
