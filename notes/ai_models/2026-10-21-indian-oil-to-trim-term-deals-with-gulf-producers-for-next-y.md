# Indian Oil to Trim Term Deals With Gulf Producers For Next Year

Indian Oil Corp. plans to reduce the amount of crude purchased under term contracts with Middle Eastern producers next year, according to a person familiar with the matter, as attacks in the Persian Gulf continue to threaten supply routes.
