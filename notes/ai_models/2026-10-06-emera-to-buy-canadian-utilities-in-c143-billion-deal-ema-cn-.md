# Emera to Buy Canadian Utilities in C$14.3 Billion Deal (EMA CN, CU CN)

Emera Inc. agreed to acquire Canadian Utilities Ltd. for about C$14 billion ($10 billion) in stock, creating a sprawling, Canadian-headquartered power and natural gas provider with customers from Florida to Alberta.
