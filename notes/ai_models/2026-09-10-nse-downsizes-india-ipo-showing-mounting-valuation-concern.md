# NSE Downsizes India IPO, Showing Mounting Valuation Concern

National Stock Exchange of India Ltd., the world’s largest derivatives exchange by volume, has downsized its initial public offering, a sign of rising concern over the willingness of investors to buy into the deal at the valuation the firm had been seeking.
