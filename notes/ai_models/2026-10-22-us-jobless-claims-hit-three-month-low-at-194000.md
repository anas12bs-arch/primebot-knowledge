# US Jobless Claims Hit Three-Month Low at 194,000

Applications for US unemployment benefits fell by 6,000 to 194,000 last week, a three-month low. The median forecast of economists polled by Bloomberg called for 200,000. Continuing claims rose to a six-week high of 1.73 million in the previous week. Michael McKee reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
