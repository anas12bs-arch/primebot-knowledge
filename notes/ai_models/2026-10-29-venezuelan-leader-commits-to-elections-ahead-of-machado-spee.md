# Venezuelan Leader Commits to Elections Ahead of Machado Speech

Venezuela will hold elections organized by an overhauled electoral regulator and witnessed by the international community, acting President Delcy Rodríguez said in a rare televised address Thursday, as pressure builds on her interim government after the return of opposition leader María Corina Machado.
