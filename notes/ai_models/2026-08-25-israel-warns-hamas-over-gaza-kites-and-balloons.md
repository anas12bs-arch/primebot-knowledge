# Israel Warns Hamas Over Gaza Kites and Balloons

Israeli officials have accused Hamas of using kites and balloons to plan attacks on its territory. The Iran-backed militant group was given until Wednesday to rein in their use - or face repercussions. Bloomberg's Dan Williams reports from Jerusalem. (Source: Bloomberg)
