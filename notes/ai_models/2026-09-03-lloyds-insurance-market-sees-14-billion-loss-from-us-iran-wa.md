# Lloyd’s Insurance Market Sees £1.4 Billion Loss From US-Iran War

Lloyd’s of London sees losses “arising from Middle East conflict” amounting to about £1.4 billion ($1.9 billion), the insurance marketplace’s first estimate of the US-Iran war’s impact.
