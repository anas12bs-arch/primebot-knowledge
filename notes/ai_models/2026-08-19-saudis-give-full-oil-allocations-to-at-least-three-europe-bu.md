# Saudis Give Full Oil Allocations to at Least Three Europe Buyers

Saudi Aramco has notified at least three European oil refiners that will supply full contractual crude volumes next month, easing concerns about the region’s supply concerns while crude flows from the Persian Gulf and Red Sea are disrupted.
