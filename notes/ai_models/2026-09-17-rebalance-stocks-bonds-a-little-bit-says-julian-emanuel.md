# Rebalance Stocks, Bonds ‘A Little Bit,’ Says Julian Emanuel

Julian Emanuel, chief equity and quantitative strategist at Evercore ISI, discusses the market implications of the Federal Reserve hiking interest rates for the first time in three years. He explains why investors should “rebalance a little bit” from stocks into fixed income. (Source: Bloomberg)
