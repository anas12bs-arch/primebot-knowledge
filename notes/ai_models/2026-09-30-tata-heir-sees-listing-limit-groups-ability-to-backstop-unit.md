# Tata Heir Sees Listing Limit Group’s Ability to Backstop Units

Noel Tata, patriarch of Tata Group’s founding family, said a public listing could limit Tata Sons Pvt.’s ability to support troubled units, making the case in a rare interview weeks after a boardroom fight erupted at one of India’s largest conglomerates.
