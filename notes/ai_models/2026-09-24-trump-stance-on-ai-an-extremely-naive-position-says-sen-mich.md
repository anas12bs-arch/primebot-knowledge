# Trump Stance on AI an 'Extremely Naive Position,' Says Sen. Michael Bennet

Democrat Senator from Colorado Michael Bennet said that the American people want a president more focused on high interest rates and fuel prices rather 'than throwing reporters out of the White House and the glamour of his dinner with the president of China.' Bennet also weighed in on talks around AI safety and said that the Presidents decision to not enact guardrails is an 'extremely naive position.'  (Source: Bloomberg)
