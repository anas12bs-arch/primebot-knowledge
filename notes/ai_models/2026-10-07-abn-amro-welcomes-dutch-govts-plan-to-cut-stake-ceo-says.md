# ABN Amro Welcomes Dutch Govt.'s Plan to Cut Stake, CEO Says

ABN Amro CEO Marguerite Berard discusses the Dutch government's plan to reduce its holding in the lender to 10.5% from the current 20.7%. "The state has always made very clear that its intention was to fully return ABN Amro to the market, and we welcome that direction," Berard tells Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
