# Turkey's Regulators Act to Stem Market Rout

Turkish officials are trying to stop a meltdown at some of the country’s most prominent asset managers from infecting the wider financial industry. Several top finance executives were either detained or given travel bans as part of a widening investigation related to mass outflows from asset managers this week. Bloomberg's Beril Akman reports.
