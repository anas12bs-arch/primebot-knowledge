# A Patient World Model for Early Forecasting of Digital Health Campaign Outcomes: Capabilities and Limits

Digital direct-to-consumer (DTC) health campaigns are usually measured after the fact. In-flight forecasting commonly relies on a separate classifier for every cutoff and horizon. We treat this task as a dynamic-system problem and build a compact patient world model. The architecture maintains a latent state per patient, learns exposure-conditioned state dynamics jointly with a weekly conversion hazard, and rolls forward into future conversion curves.
