# Learning Adaptable Dexterous Skills via Hybrid Dexterous Mimicking

Dexterous multi-finger hands hold promise to unlock diverse skills like functional grasps, in-hand reorientation, and dynamic handover that the human-designed world assumes, yet existing learning recipes force a trade-off.
