# Chevron’s Wirth Warns US Diesel Curbs Could Send Prices Higher

A US ban on diesel exports could result in higher prices in some parts of the country as well as causing issues with other nations that rely on American supplies, Chevron Corp. Chief Executive Officer Mike Wirth said on Tuesday.
