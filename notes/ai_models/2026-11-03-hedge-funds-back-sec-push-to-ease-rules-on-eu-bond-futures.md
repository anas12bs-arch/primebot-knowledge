# Hedge Funds Back SEC Push to Ease Rules on EU Bond Futures

The Securities and Exchange Commission should add the European Union to a four-decade old list of exempted issuers, a move that would ease restrictions on US investor access to the bloc’s new bond futures market, according to a group representing hedge funds.
