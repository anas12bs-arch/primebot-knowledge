# Kyiv Hits Sixth Russian Oil Plant in a Week as Campaign Picks Up

Ukraine attacked a sixth Russian crude-processing plant in the past week in a sign that Kyiv’s campaign against the country’s energy facilities is intensifying. The wave of strikes forced a major refinery to halt operations.
