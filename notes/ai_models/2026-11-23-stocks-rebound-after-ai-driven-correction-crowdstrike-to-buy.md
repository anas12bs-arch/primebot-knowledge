# Stocks Rebound After AI-Driven Correction; Crowdstrike to Buy ZScaler

US equity futures rebound from a rout in the AI trade that sent the S&amp;P 500 into a correction. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent warns that the Federal Reserve risks jeopardizing the US economy by overtightening in response to the latest inflation data. CrowdStrike agrees to acquire cybersecurity provider Zscaler in an all-stock transaction valued at about $43.9 billion. Kamakshya Trivedi of Goldman Sachs joins “Bloomberg Brief” to discuss the latest market recovery. (Source: Bloomberg)
