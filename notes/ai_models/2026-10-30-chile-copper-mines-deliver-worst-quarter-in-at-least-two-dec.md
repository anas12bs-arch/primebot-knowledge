# Chile Copper Mines Deliver Worst Quarter in at Least Two Decades

Chile’s copper output slumped to its lowest quarterly level in at least two decades as accidents and wild weather compounded operational setbacks and the effects of deteriorating ore quality in the top-producing nation.
