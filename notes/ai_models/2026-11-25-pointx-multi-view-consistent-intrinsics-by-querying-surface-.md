# PointX: Multi-View Consistent Intrinsics by Querying Surface Points

The appearance of a scene is determined by the albedo, roughness, metallicity and normal of every point on its surface, and by the light that reaches it, its irradiance. Recovering these intrinsics from images is ambiguous, and methods that predict them per pixel give the same surface point a different decomposition in every view.
