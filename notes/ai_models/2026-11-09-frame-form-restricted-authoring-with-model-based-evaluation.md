# Frame: Form-Restricted Authoring with Model-Based Evaluation

Many professional reports reuse the structure of prior editions while updating the content. We formalize this setting as form-restricted report generation: given a sample report and new grounding documents, generate a report that preserves the sample's form while accurately reflecting the new evidence.
