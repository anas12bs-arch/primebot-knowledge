# Chile’s Long-Term Swaps Defy Stalled Economy to Hit 18-Month High

Chile’s long-dated swaps are at the highest level in 18 months as investors retain faith in the outlook for the economy, despite months of bad news, while the fiscal position remains weak and US Treasury yields soar.
