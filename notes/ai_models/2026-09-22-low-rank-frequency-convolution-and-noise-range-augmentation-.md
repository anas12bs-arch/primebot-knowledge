# Low-Rank Frequency Convolution and Noise-Range Augmentation for Real-Time Pitch Estimation on Edge Devices

The model must be small, it must stay accurate when the input is noisy, and one frame must be produced inside the frame period. In this report the Frequency Convolution Network (FrCN) of our earlier work is factored into a low-rank form.
