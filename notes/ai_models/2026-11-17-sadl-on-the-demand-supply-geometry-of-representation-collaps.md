# SADL: On the Demand-Supply Geometry of Representation Collapse

In Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs), a well-constrained projector output does not by itself prevent representation collapse. We find that, from initialization, the encoder representation is dominated by a component shared by different inputs. A batch-normalized projector removes this component, so regularization after the projector cannot correct it, and the differences between representations shrink until they collapse.
