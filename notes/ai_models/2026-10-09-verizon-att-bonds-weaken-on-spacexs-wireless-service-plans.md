# Verizon, AT&T Bonds Weaken on SpaceX’s Wireless-Service Plans

Bonds issued by Verizon Communications Inc. and AT&amp;T Inc. weakened on Friday, following SpaceX’s announcement that it will buy US spectrum that will help the company become a “major mobile carrier” in the US.
