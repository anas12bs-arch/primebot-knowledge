# Oil Companies Taking Advantage of American Public, Says Rep. Burchett

Representative Tim Burchett, a Tennessee Republican, wants to restrict diesel exports and says 'the American public is getting gouged.' Burchett said that big oil companies are 'compounding the problem' with prices at the pump and taking advantage of the American public because they have a monopoly on the industry.
