# Oil Swings With Focus on Deep Saudi Price Cut

Oil fluctuated in jittery trading, as Saudi Arabia cut prices of its benchmark grade to Asia, the kingdom’s state producer warned about the risk of low stockpiles and as fighting in Yemen intensified.
Martijn Rats, Global Commodities Strategist at Morgan Stanley, discusses upside oil risks amid broader Middle East fighting. (Source: Bloomberg)
