# Why Nordstrom Decided to Go Private

Nordstrom Co-CEO Pete Nordstrom, says that there is "a lot of headroom" in the firm's Rack concept and that he and his brother, Co-CEO Erik Nordstrom, wanted more influence over some of the company's decisions in explaining why they took Nordstrom private in 2025. He speaks with Bloomberg's Romaine Bostick. (Source: Bloomberg)
