# Markets Brace for Sharp Swings No Matter Result of Brazil Vote

Investors are expecting sharp swings in Brazilian markets in the aftermath of Sunday’s elections no matter the outcome, with assets to adjust quickly as the neck-and-neck presidential race prevented traders from taking too strong a view in the lead up to the vote.
