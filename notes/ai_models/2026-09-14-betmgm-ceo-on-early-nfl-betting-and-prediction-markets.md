# BetMGM CEO on Early NFL Betting and Prediction Markets

With Week 1 of the NFL season wrapping up, Adam Greenblatt, CEO of BetMGM, said the season has started enthusiastically and that early activity has been strong. He added that, from the bookmakers’ perspective, Sunday was not a favorable day because the fans came out ahead, leaving bettors happy and operators with an early loss. He speaks with Romaine Bostick & Isabelle Lee on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
