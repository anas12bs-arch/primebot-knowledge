# Trump's Family Seeking National Bank Charter

Donald Trump's family has won preliminary approval to start a national bank, riding a wave of deregulation under the Trump administration. For the president’s family, the goal is to expand the use of USD1, their dollar-backed stablecoin, World Liberty Financial said earlier this month after receiving conditional approval. Bloomberg News Financial Reporter Paige Smith has the details of the move for today's Big Take.
