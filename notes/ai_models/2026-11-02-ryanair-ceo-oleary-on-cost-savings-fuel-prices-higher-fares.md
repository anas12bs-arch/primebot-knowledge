# Ryanair CEO O'Leary on Cost Savings, Fuel Prices, Higher Fares

Ryanair CEO Michael O'Leary speaks on Bloomberg Television after the airline announced it will embark on a cost purge to help offset a €2 billion ($2.2 billion) increase in fuel charges next year. "My advice to consumers now is book early for next summer because the prices are going to rise significantly," O'Leary says. (Source: Bloomberg)
