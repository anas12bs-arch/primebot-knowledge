# Centroid Spectral Coupling: Implicit Alignment and Representation Diversity in Self-Supervised Learning

Self-Supervised Learning (SSL) aims to align augmented views while preserving representation diversity and preventing collapse. Existing non-contrastive methods often address these goals using multiple weighted loss terms. We introduce Centroid Spectral Coupling (CSC), a single-term SSL objective that implicitly couples view alignment, representation diversity, and collapse resistance.
