# Democrats Need to Focus on Corruption, Affordability Says Rep. Morelle

Democratic Representative Joe Morelle of New York said that Democrats need to focus on anti-corruption efforts and setting American families up for success after voters handed the party control of both chambers of Congress. Morelle also said that President Trump's continued focus on his White House ballroom today after voters repudiated his policies 'says volumes about his leadership.' (Source: Bloomberg)
