# Oil Rises as Houthis Attack Two Saudi Ports on Red Sea

Oil extended gains as Iran-backed Houthi rebels continued to target Saudi Arabia, including a refinery at Ras Tanura and the Red Sea ports of Jeddah and Jazan. Meanwhile, the United Arab Emirates said it plans to expand power and water desalination on its eastern coast, beyond the Strait of Hormuz, as war exposes Persian Gulf vulnerabilities. Stuart Livingstone-Wallace reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
