# Visa India’s Next Bet Beyond UPI Dominance

Visa Group India & South Asia Country Manager Suresh Sethi unpacks India’s evolving financial inclusion story in the age of Unified Payments Interface. He discusses Visa’s growth priorities, its strategy to keep cards relevant, and how partnerships and India’s digital public infrastructure could reshape the payments landscape. He speaks with Haslinda Amin from the sidelines of 'Global Fintech Festival Mumbai'. (Source: Bloomberg)
