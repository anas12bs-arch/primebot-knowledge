# Why Finite Intelligence Works in an Infinite World: A Mathematical Theory

An information system often has to learn before it knows what it will be asked. A finite representation can succeed without recording an infinite environment in full when those asks share collective structure, rather than demand unrelated facts scattered arbitrarily across it. We formalize this phenomenon through future-task information, the minimum retained information needed to preserve decision value relative to the available evidence.
