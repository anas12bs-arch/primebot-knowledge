# Steering beyond Language: Improving Multilingual Training-Free Text Embeddings via Paired-Residual Subspace Removal

While deriving text embeddings directly from frozen LLMs has emerged as a promising direction, existing methods focus almost exclusively on English, leaving multilingual settings largely unexplored. In this work, we present the first systematic study of multilingual training-free text embeddings in LLMs. Our analysis reveals a language-conditioned embedding geometry in which semantically equivalent sentences remain misaligned across languages.
