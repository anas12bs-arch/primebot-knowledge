# Trump Plans to Seize Direct Control of Venezuela’s Oil Reserves

President Donald Trump plans to seize direct control of Venezuela’s oil reserves, announcing that a deal had been reached with Venezuela’s interim President Delcy Rodríguez in which the US will control more than 65 billion barrels of oil “through a partnership with private business.” Anthony Di Paola reports on Bloomberg Television.
