# IMF's Georgieva on Warsh's Speech, 'Stubborn' Inflation

IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva says Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh "did a great job" in his Jackson Hole speech. Speaking with Bloomberg's Lisa Abramowicz on "Bloomberg The Close." Georgieva also discusses recent inflation pressures and whether they reflect central bank failure or other forces. (Source: Bloomberg)
