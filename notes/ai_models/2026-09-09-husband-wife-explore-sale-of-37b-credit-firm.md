# Husband & Wife Explore Sale of $37B Credit Firm

Bloomberg's Matt Monks joins Scarlet Fu on "Bloomberg Deals." Palmer Square Capital Management is a $37 billion credit firm run by the husband-wife duo Chris and Angie Long. And the couple is exploring a sale of the business. Under their leadership, the firm has grown to become one of the biggest issuers of collateralized loan obligations. (Source: Bloomberg)
