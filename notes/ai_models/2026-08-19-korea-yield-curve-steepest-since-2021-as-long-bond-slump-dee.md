# Korea Yield Curve Steepest Since 2021 as Long-Bond Slump Deepens

The spread between South Korea’s three- and 10-year government bond yields has widened to the highest in nearly five years amid a global selloff in long-dated securities and expectations that Bank of Korea won’t raise interest rates aggressively.
