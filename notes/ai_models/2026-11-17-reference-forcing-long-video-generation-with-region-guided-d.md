# Reference Forcing: Long Video Generation with Region-Guided Drift Correction

Long video generation should keep the subject and background consistent while supporting rich, natural motion. However, autoregressive models are often distilled with distribution matching distillation (DMD) over short windows, which does not train them to keep this consistency over long ranges. As a result, the subject and background can drift over time. Methods that manage the key-value (KV) cache retain or retrieve history to reduce drift. Yet this history may itself have drifted.
