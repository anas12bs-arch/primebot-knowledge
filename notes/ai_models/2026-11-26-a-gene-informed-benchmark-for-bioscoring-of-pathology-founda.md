# A gene informed benchmark for BioScoring of pathology foundation models

Pathology foundation models are usually compared by their performance on downstream tasks such as classification and retrieval. While such benchmarks measure how useful a model is, they provide no insight into the biology it represents. We introduce BioScore, a benchmark that quantifies how well an image encoder captures cell identities, with spatial transcriptomics of the same tissue as the reference.
