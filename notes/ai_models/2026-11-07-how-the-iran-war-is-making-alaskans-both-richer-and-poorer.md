# How the Iran War Is Making Alaskans Both Richer and Poorer

Oil prices have surged across the United States as a result of the ongoing war in Iran. This means higher costs for households in most states, but in Alaska, the state government is bringing in new revenue from its high oil production to offset those higher costs. Some of the revenue is then redistributed to Alaskans through the state’s unique sovereign wealth fund.
