# Hawkeye: Reading Illegible Text with Vision Language Models

While modern Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have been remarkably successful in general scene text recognition, they consistently struggle with the long-tail distribution of text that is degraded by motion blur or low resolution. These limitations are particularly apparent in egocentric applications like smart glasses, where text is often captured in noisy environments.
