# NereidVLA: Learning to Understand and Navigate the Underwater World

Underwater navigation requires translating visual spatial cues into motion, yet marine vision-language datasets rarely include paired robot trajectories. We present NereidVLA, a vision-language-action model that transfers marine spatial knowledge to language-conditioned 3D navigation. We first train Sea2Know on approximately two million marine examples to learn target grounding, spatial relations, and scene structure without paired robot-motion supervision.
