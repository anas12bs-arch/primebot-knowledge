# BNP’s Asset Unit Shorts China Rate Swaps to Hedge EM Bond Bets

BNP Paribas Asset Management has been hedging its bullish bets on higher-yielding emerging-market bonds by shorting offshore Chinese interest-rate swaps, according to James McAlevey, head of global aggregate and absolute return.
