# KoalaGS: Complexity-Aware Continuous Level of Detail for Gaussian Splatting

Deploying a trained 3D Gaussian Splatting scene under shrinking compute budgets requires one primitive set to remain effective when rendered at smaller prefixes. In this continuous level of detail (LoD) setting, the challenge is to decide which Gaussians should retain capacity as the budget decreases.
