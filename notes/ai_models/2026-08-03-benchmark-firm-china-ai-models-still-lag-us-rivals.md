# Benchmark Firm: China AI Models Still Lag US Rivals

Micah Hill-Smith, CEO of AI model benchmarking platform Artificial Analysis, says his firm's analysis shows that leading Chinese AI models continue to lag their US counterparts by three to nine months, even after the release of breakthrough models. He discusses the findings on "Bloomberg: The China Show." (Source: Bloomberg)
