# NHL Team Futures ETFs Filed, Draw Attention

Bloomberg Intelligence's Athanasios Psarofagis joins Scarlet Fu and Eric Balchunas on "ETF IQ." They discuss how Volatility Shares and Roundhill have filed for 32 ETFs based on each of the 32 teams in the NHL. Each fund will obtain exposure through CME futures tracking a team-specific FutureSports Performance Index. This index will be built from 55 measures -- including wins, losses, goals and other official stats.  (Source: Bloomberg)
