# Private Credit’s Tokenization Revolution

Apex Group CEO Peter Hughes joins Bloomberg Open Interest to explain why family offices need better access to quality private-market deals. He also makes the case that regulated tokenization could transform private credit, bringing 24/7 access, secondary-market liquidity and a potential end to traditional seven-to-10-year lockups.
