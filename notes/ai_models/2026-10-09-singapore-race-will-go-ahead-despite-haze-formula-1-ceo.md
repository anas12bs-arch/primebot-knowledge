# Singapore Race Will Go Ahead Despite Haze: Formula 1 CEO

Singapore’s Formula 1 race will go ahead this weekend despite the haze, says the company’s CEO Stefano Domenicali.

The haze from Indonesian wildfires has choked the city for weeks, pushing it to the top of the global rankings for air pollution. (Source: Bloomberg)
