# We Support Zuckerberg's Vision: Cassidy

Lauren Cassidy, CIO at Founders 100 ETF, joins Scarlet Fu and Athanasios Psarofagis on "Bloomberg ETF IQ." They discuss the Founders 100 ETF (ticker: FFF), the possible merger between Tesla and SpaceX, and how Meta is seeking to raise as much as $95B from selling common and preferred stock.
