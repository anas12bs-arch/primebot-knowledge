# Jio’s IPO Surge Ends Tata’s Reign as Most Valuable India Group

Jio Platforms Ltd.’s blockbuster stock market debut has propelled the digital company as well as its parent Reliance Industries Ltd. into rarified air. It also helped Mukesh Ambani’s conglomerate topple Tata Group as India’s largest conglomerate by market value.
