# India Economic Growth May Beat RBI’s 6.7% Forecast, Gupta Says

India may grow closer to 7% in the financial year through March, stronger than the central bank’s 6.7% forecast, Deputy Governor Poonam Gupta said on Thursday, a pace that would keep it among the world’s fastest-growing major economies.
