# Ordered Policy Optimization

Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) stabilizes policy updates by clipping the importance ratio between the new policy and the data-collecting policy. The standard rule uses one fixed clipping range for all samples. While this choice offers simplicity and effectiveness, it fails to distinguish statistically harmful extreme ratio values from large, yet tolerable changes.
