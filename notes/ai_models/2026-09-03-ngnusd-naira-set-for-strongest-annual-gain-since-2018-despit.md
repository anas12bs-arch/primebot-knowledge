# NGN/USD:  Naira Set for Strongest Annual Gain Since 2018 Despite Elections

Nigeria’s currency is headed for its best performance in almost a decade, as rising dollar inflows from higher oil prices and remittances help insulate the naira from political risks usually associated with its election season, according to analysts.
