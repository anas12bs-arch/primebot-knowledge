# UBS, Europe’s Biggest Stock Bull, Sees 13% Gain By End of 2027

European stocks are set for double-digit gains by the end of next year as a broadening industrial cycle fuels profit growth, according to UBS Group AG strategists whose separate 2026 target is already the most bullish among forecasters tracked by Bloomberg.
