# Ground, Iterate, Trace: Decomposing AI Research Ideation

Large language models have been widely used to generate plausible research ideas, yet it remains unclear what makes automated ideation effective and how generated novel proposals arise from prior scientific work. We introduce the Creative AI Researcher (CAR), an agentic framework combining literature grounding, iterative development and explicit conceptual operators.
