# Tencent-Backed China Chipmaker Enflame Seeks $911 Million in IPO

Shanghai Enflame Technology Co., backed by Tencent Holdings Ltd., is seeking to raise about 6.12 billion yuan ($911 million) in an initial public offering, as the last of the so-called “four little dragons” of leading Chinese artificial intelligence chipmakers taps the public market.
