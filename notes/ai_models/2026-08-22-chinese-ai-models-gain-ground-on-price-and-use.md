# Chinese AI Models Gain Ground on Price and Use

Chinese AI models are rapidly narrowing the performance gap with US rivals while gaining users with lower prices and open-weight technology that lets companies deploy them locally. Bloomberg News Tech Reporter Luz Ding is on Bloomberg This Weekend and explains to hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini that Chinese AI Models capabilities are approaching leading US models at lower cost.
