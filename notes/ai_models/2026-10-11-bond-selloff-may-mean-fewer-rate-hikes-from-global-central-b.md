# Bond Selloff May Mean Fewer Rate Hikes From Global Central Banks

Bond markets are starting to do some of the lifting for central banks around the world by pushing up borrowing costs themselves, potentially reducing the number of benchmark interest-rate hikes needed to get inflation under control.
