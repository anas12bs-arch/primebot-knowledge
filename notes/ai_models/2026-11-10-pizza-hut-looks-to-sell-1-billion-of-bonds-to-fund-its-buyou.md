# Pizza Hut Looks to Sell $1 Billion of Bonds to Fund Its Buyout

Pizza Hut is looking to sell around $1 billion of bonds backed by its franchise agreements and related fees, to help fund its buyout by private equity firm LongRange Capital LLC.
