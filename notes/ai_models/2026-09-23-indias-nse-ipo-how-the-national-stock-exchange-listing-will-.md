# India’s NSE IPO: How the National Stock Exchange Listing Will Work

India’s largest stock exchange, National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. is preparing for its own market debut on Sept. 24, after investors bid for more than five times the shares available in its 226 billion rupees ($2.4 billion) initial public offering.
