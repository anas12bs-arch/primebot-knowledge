# Keeping LLM Agents on Procedure: Executable Finite-State Control for Long-Horizon Instruction Fidelity

Much of the automation handed to language-model agents is procedural: each step needs little intelligence, but a long unattended run must follow a skill or SOP stated once at the start. We show that agents drift from such procedures over long runs, and that restating the instructions or resuming a stopped agent only moves the drift to another shortcut.
