# Does Iced Coffee Pass the Vibe Check?

The very nature of work is in flux right now, with artificial intelligence accelerating the deepening structural shifts. Employers and their young employees are caught in a power struggle over who gets to decide the norms of this new workplace. Iced coffee is just the latest proxy for that tension — how each party feels both misunderstood and deep anxiety about their place in this evolving landscape, Bloomberg Opinion columnist Beth Kowitt says.
