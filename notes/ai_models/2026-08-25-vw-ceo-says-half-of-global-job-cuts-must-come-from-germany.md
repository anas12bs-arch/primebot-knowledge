# VW CEO Says Half of Global Job Cuts Must Come From Germany

Volkswagen Chief Executive Officer Oliver Blume told workers at the main Wolfsburg factory on Tuesday that roughly half of the carmaker’s additional workforce reductions will need to happen in Germany. William Wilkes reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
