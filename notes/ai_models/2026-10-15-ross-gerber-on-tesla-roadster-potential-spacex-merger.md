# Ross Gerber on Tesla Roadster, Potential SpaceX Merger

Ross Gerber discussed Tesla’s anticipated Roadster unveiling, following a Barclays note suggesting Tesla and SpaceX could merge before year-end, potentially ahead of Tesla’s annual general meeting. Gerber said a high-priced car made in low volumes would not materially affect Tesla financially. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
