# Depth Pro 2: Sharp and Temporally Consistent Metric Monocular Depth

We present Depth Pro 2, an approach to estimating dense metric depth for images and video at full resolution. By analyzing prior methods for monocular depth estimation, we find that they commonly trade off temporal consistency and boundary sharpness: multi-frame methods favor consistency, single-frame methods favor sharpness.
