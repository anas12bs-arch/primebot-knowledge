# Turkish Funds With $7.5 Billion Assets Default on Redemptions

Tera Portfoy Yonetimi AS said two of its funds with a combined 366 billion liras ($7.5 billion) in assets failed to meet redemption requests, making it the second Turkish asset manager to announce defaults on some funds this week.
