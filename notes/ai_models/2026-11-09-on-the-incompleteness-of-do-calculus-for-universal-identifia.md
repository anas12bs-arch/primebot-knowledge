# On the Incompleteness of do-calculus for Universal Identifiability

We study the problem of causal effect identification from an arbitrary set of observational and/or interventional distributions, which we call Universal Identifiability (uID). The uID problem generalizes existing classical identifiability (ID) and general identifiability (gID) problems, which considered the identification problem from either mere observational distributions or a specific type of interventional distributions.
