# Nano Nuclear Energy Bring Microreactors to UAE

US microreactor company Nano Nuclear Energy is on the path to deploy its technology to the Middle East after signing the first and only nuclear microreactor partnership with the UAE. The agreement aims to help the country go off grid, power desert data centers and decarbonize oil and gas fields. Jay Yu, the company's founder, executive chairman and president spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on Horizons Middle East & Africa on the deployment in the UAE plus their plans for Mideast expansion.
