# Rubric-CEPR: Self-Evolving Image Editing via Reward-Verified Self-Distillation

Instruction-guided image editors have become highly capable, yet improving them further still depends on human-edited training pairs or external reward models. Such supervision is costly to obtain and can reward plausible failures: a realistic output may leave the requested change undone or alter content that should be preserved.
