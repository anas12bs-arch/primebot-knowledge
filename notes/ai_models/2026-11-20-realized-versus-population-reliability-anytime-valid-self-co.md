# Realized versus Population Reliability: Anytime-Valid Self-Consistency Certification under Correlated Reasoning

Self-consistency certification treats repeated sampling from a language model as sequential evidence accumulation and stops when an anytime-valid e-process crosses a threshold, guaranteeing finite-sample Type I error control by Ville's inequality.
