# Shopify Revenue Miss; Yum Brands Jumps on Taco Bell Sales | Stock Movers

On this episode of Stock Movers:

- Shopify (SHOP) shares are plunging after its quarterly revenue missed analysts’ expectations for the first time in four years.  
- Lattice Semiconductor (LSCC) shares are soaring after the chipmaker’s outlook showed strong visibility, suggesting robust growth into 2028.
- Yum Brands (YUM) shares jumped on "better-than-feared" Taco Bell 3Q sales. RBC says Taco Bell’s 3Q comparable sales drop of 4% is better than the buyside feared. (Source: Bloomberg)
