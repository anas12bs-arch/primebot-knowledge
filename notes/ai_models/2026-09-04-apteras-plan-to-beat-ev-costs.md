# Aptera's Plan to Beat EV Costs

Aptera co-CEO Steve Fambro joins Bloomberg Open Interest to explain why the solar EV startup is betting on China to lower costs and accelerate production, despite tariffs and policy uncertainty. He lays out Aptera’s self-charging promise, its planned California-first rollout, and why he believes a globally sourced model can succeed where other EV startups have struggled. (Source: Bloomberg)
