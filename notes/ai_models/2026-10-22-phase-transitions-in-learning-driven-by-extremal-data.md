# Phase Transitions in Learning Driven by Extremal Data

We study an extremal regression problem, an example of multiple instance learning (MIL), where the predictors are given as multisets, called bags. The bag elements (called instances) possess hidden labels which are pooled to produce an observed bag-level label. Max pooling is common in MIL applications, but gives rise to vanishing gradients.
