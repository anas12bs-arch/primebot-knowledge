# BioLoom: Scaling Biomedical Vision-Language Models by Learning from the Structure of Scientific Articles

The development of biomedical vision-language models (VLMs) with contrastive image-text pretraining has been driven by scaling to increasingly large and diverse image-text datasets. Beyond scaling, further progress can come from learning richer representations from existing data. We hypothesize that the structure of scientific literature itself provides an additional source of supervision: figures, panels, captions, and accompanying article text encode rich relationships across modalities.
