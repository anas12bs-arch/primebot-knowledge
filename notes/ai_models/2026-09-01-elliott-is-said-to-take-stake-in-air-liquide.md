# Elliott Is Said to Take Stake in Air Liquide

Elliott Investment Management has taken a stake in Air Liquide, pushing the industrial gas supplier to improve margins, according to people familiar with the matter. Representatives for Air Liquide and Elliott declined to comment. Bloomberg's Ben Scent explains. (Source: Bloomberg)
