# Natixis Raises Japan Stocks Allocation, Cuts US Equity Exposure

Natixis Investment Managers has raised its allocation to Japanese equities, betting the nation’s economic growth momentum will endure as inflationary pressures push government bond yields higher, its strategists told Bloomberg in a recent interview.
