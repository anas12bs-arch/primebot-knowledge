# Reinforcement Learning Can Be Recovered From Its Own Rollouts

Broadly speaking, reinforcement learning (RL) post-training converts compute into model capabilities by training on model-generated data. Typically, the rollouts are discarded after each update, and the knowledge acquired during RL resides in the model weights. In this work, we investigate how much of this knowledge is retained in the rollouts themselves.
