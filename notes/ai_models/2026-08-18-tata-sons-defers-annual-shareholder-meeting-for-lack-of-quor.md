# Tata Sons Defers Annual Shareholder Meeting for Lack of Quorum

Tata Sons Pvt. deferred its annual general meeting after a key shareholder was unable to participate, according to people familiar with the matter, even as the Indian conglomerate prepares to find a new chairman following Natarajan Chandrasekaran’s resignation last week.
