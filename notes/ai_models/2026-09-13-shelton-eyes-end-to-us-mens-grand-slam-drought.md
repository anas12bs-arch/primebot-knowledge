# Shelton Eyes End to US Men’s Grand Slam Drought

Ben Shelton heads into the US Open final with a chance to become the first American man to win a Grand Slam singles title since Andy Roddick in 2003, potentially giving US men’s tennis a new marquee star. Bloomberg News Business of Sports Reporter Vanessa Perdomo joins David Gura and Christina Ruffini on Bloomberg This Weekend to discuss Shelton and also broader questions across sports ranging from college eligibility rules to the NFL’s push into international markets.
