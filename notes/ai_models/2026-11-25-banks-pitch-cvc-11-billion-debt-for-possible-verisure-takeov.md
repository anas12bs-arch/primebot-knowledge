# Banks Pitch CVC €11 Billion Debt for Possible Verisure Takeover

Banks are offering CVC Capital Partners Plc up to €11 billion ($12 billion) in debt financing for a takeover of Verisure Plc, as the buyout firm weighs a bid to return the home security group to private ownership after a difficult year on the public markets.
