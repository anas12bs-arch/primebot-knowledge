# US Will Not Tackle Debt Until Republicans, Democrats Come Together, Says Rep. Arrington

House Budget Chairman Jodey Arrington said 'Republicans and Democrats are going to have to come together' on a debt reduction plan to counteract the demographic challenges of increasing Social Security and Medicare beneficiaries. Arrington also discussed the pushback against AI data centers around the country, saying that the US has win the AI race but there should be guardrails on drawing on energy and water resources to do so.
