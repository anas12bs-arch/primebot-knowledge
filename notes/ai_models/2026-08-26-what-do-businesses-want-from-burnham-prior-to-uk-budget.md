# What Do Businesses Want From Burnham Prior to UK Budget?

Anna Leach, chief economist at the Institute of Directors, joins Bloomberg's Lizzy Burden on "The Pulse" to discuss the priorities of British businesses in the upcoming UK budget. Leach also talks about the Burnham government's approach to reshaping the Cabinet Office and the creation of a national economic council with devolved powers, aimed at improving policy implementation and fostering economic growth.
