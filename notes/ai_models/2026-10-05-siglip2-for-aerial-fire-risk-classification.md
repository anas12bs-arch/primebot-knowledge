# SigLIP2 for aerial fire risk classification

We examine the transfer of a pretrained SigLIP2 image encoder to seven class fire risk classification from aerial imagery. We introduce a reproducible partition of the public FireRisk training mirror and an implementation that records data provenance, preprocessing and model selection. Two initial runs compare a frozen encoder probe with full model adaptation. On the validation partition, full adaptation reaches 63.05% accuracy and 58.94% macro F1, compared with 55.95% and 50.19% for the probe.
