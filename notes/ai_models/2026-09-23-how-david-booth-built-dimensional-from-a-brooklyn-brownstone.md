# How David Booth Built Dimensional From A Brooklyn Brownstone

David Booth started Dimensional Fund Advisors out of a Brooklyn brownstone in 1981 with an idea that challenged conventional investing: investors should have exposure to smaller companies as part of a diversified portfolio. More than four decades later, Dimensional manages over $1 trillion. Booth is on this week's episode of The David Rubenstein Show: Peer to Peer Conversations to discuss how he built Dimensional and the investment philosophy behind the firm.
