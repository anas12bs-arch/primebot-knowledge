# Uncertainty-Aware Candidate Generation for Entity Linking in Cyber Threat Intelligence

Entity Resolution (ER) pipelines often collapse ambiguity into early hard decisions: upstream Named Entity Recognition (NER) selects a single span and type, and downstream Entity Linking (EL) treats that choice as ground truth. This discards valuable information about the ambiguity of the underlying data (e.g., span boundary disagreement and type confusion), leading to brittle systems that fail unpredictably when faced with uncertainty in inputs.
