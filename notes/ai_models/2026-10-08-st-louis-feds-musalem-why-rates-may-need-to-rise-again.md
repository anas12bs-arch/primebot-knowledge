# St. Louis Fed’s Musalem: Why Rates May Need To Rise Again

St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem warned against a broader resurgence in inflation and signaled that interest rates may need to rise over the next six to nine months, even as the U.S. economy remains strong. Musalem spoke with Bloomberg's Michael Mckee at The Bloomberg Future of Fixed Income Forum in New York. (Source: Bloomberg)
