# How the Oil Price Is Pressuring Treasuries

Bloomberg's Anthony Stephens says the price of oil has become one of the movers of shorter-duration Treasury yields. He speaks as rising oil prices are bolstering expectations that the Federal Reserve will hike interest rates. (Source: Bloomberg)
