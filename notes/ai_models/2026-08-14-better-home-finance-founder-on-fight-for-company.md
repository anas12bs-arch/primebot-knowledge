# Better Home & Finance Founder on Fight for Company

Embattled former Better.com CEO Vishal Garg says he believes that 'the job of making home ownership better for all Americans is not done' after he departed the company on August 3rd. He claims to have the support of a majority of Class A and B shareholders to return to the head of the company, in lieu of interim CEO Daniel Lewis. He speaks to Romaine Bostick on Bloomberg's 'The Close.' (Source: Bloomberg)
