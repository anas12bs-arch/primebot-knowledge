# China Warns Corrupt Officials Can’t Expect a Safe Landing

China’s top anti-graft authorities warned that officials who engage in corruption cannot expect a “safe landing,” regardless of their rank, past contributions or whether they’ve retired, according to a commentary from the CPC Central Commission for Discipline Inspection and National Commission of Supervision.
