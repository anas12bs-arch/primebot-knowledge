# Bessent Defends Buyback Plan, Stops Short of New Bond Measures

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent refrained from any further signals on revamping US debt management on Monday, following a report that his department could draw down some of its cash pile to fund buybacks of higher-yielding older securities. Bloomberg's Garfield Reynolds discusses. (Source: Bloomberg)
