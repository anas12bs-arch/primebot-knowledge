# The Evolving Ways Investors Trade and Access Credit

Credit markets are being reshaped by the growing use of ETFs, credit futures, total return swaps and portfolio trading, expanding the ways investors access liquidity and manage exposure. Jane Street’s Phil Cichlar, Vanguard’s Jacob Riley and CME Group’s Ted Carey spoke with Bloomberg’s Umesh Gajria at the Bloomberg Future of Fixed Income 2026 conference in New York.
