# Private Credit Investors Prefer Being Trapped Than Take Loss

Bloomberg's Olivia Fishlow joins Scarlet Fu on "Bloomberg Real Yield." Bloomberg News reporting that Cox Capital Partners offered instant liquidity for some investors by purchasing their shares, up to $90 million worth, at an average 26% discount. The bids though saw mixed success, with some drawing no offers at all. (Source: Bloomberg)
