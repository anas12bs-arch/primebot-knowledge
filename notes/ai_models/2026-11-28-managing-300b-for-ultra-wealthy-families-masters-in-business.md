# Managing $300B for Ultra Wealthy Families: Masters in Business with Holly MacDonald

Barry speaks with Holly MacDonald, CEO of Bessemer Trust. They discuss her time working in Brazil and how investing in emerging markets shapes her investment philosophy. They also discuss the enduring strength of the US dollar, and how Bessemer evolved from a single family office to a $300 billion enterprise. They also discuss how they navigate building generational wealth and moderating family conflicts.  (Source: Bloomberg)
