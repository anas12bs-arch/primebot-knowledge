# TVP: TV is Almost All You Need for Speculative Decoding

Speculative decoding accelerates LLM inference with a drafter whose proposed tokens are verified by the target model in parallel. Under lossless speculative sampling, a draft token is accepted with probability one minus the total variation (TV) distance between the target and draft distributions, yet drafters are often trained with cross-entropy (CE) or KL.
