# How AI Assistance Affects Human Skill Development: A Study of Learning with Logic Puzzles

While AI assistance can improve human task performance in the short term, it may also undermine the development of skills in the longer term. We examine this tension in a controlled logic-puzzle experiment involving on-demand AI assistance, where participants complete tasks before, during, and after AI is available.
