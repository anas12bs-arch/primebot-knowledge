# Nonmaximal sums of maximally monotone operators under Rockafellar's constraint qualification

We construct counterexamples to Rockafellar's sum conjecture in which two maximally monotone operators satisfy the interior-domain condition but their sum is not maximally monotone. We give one counterexample on $c_0$ and another on $\ell^1$ with its usual norm.
