# JOOPITER Head of Sales on Michael Jordan Jersey Auction

Caitlin Donovan, the Global Head of Sales at JOOPITER, discusses the rapid growth of sports memorabilia as an alternative asset class, saying the category is one of the fastest-growing parts of the collectibles market. JOOPITER, the digital auction house founded by Pharrell Williams in 2022, has put a game-worn Michael Jordan jersey from Game 3 of the 1998 NBA Finals on the market with an opening bid of $10 million. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
