# DynSurvX: Time-Resolved Explanations for Dynamic Survival Predictions

Recent survival models integrating longitudinal data achieve strong predictive performance, but their predictions are often difficult to interpret, limiting their clinical use. We propose DynSurvX, a model-agnostic explainability framework for dynamic survival prediction with mixed static and longitudinal covariates.
