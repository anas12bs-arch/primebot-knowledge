# Ethan Allen CEO Out After Four Decades

Ethan Allen has named former Neiman Marcus Group executive Stefanie Tsen Ward as its interim CEO and expanded its board to nine people to include four more directors, after activist investor DGB Investments gained majority control of the board last week. Mengqi Sun reports on "Bloomberg Open Interest." (Source: Bloomberg)
