# Exact Global Minimax Risk near Nonidentification

We study squared-error estimation when a nuisance parameter is unidentified on a submodel but affects a target that vanishes there. For models with higher-order profile expansions, we give sufficient conditions under which the largest critical-path minimax risk is the exact asymptotic minimax risk over the full parameter space.
