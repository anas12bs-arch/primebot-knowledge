# Legendary Entertainment Makes Bid for Lionsgate Studios

Legendary Entertainment has revived talks and made a takeover offer for Lionsgate Studios Corp. according to people familiar with the matter. Lionsgate is the studio behind movies such as The Hunger Games and John Wick. Chris Palmeri reports.  (Source: Bloomberg)
