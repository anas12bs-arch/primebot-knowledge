# Lufthansa to Eliminate 1,000 Jobs At Struggling Flagship Airline

Deutsche Lufthansa AG will cut 1,000 jobs at its namesake airline to turn around the unprofitable mainline unit, as high fuel costs put more pressure on Europe’s largest aviation group.
