# Communicate the Change: Efficient Expert Parallelism via Activation Compression

Scaling has driven the success of large language models (LLM), with sparse mixture-of-experts (MoE) architectures enabling greater parameter capacity without a proportional increase in computation. At large scales, MoE models rely on expert parallelism to distribute experts across devices. Each MoE layer requires two synchronous collective communication steps during inference: dispatching hidden states to the activated experts and combining the resulting expert outputs.
