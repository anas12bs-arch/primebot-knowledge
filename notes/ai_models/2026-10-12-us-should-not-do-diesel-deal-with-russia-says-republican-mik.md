# US Should Not Do Diesel Deal With Russia, Says Republican Mike Lawler

Representative Mike Lawler, a New York Republican, said he's opposed to any US deal with Russia that benefits 'vile dictator' Vladimir Putin. Lawler said that the answer to US energy supply issues lies in shoring up US domestic energy production and building new refineries.  (Source: Bloomberg)
