Singing emotion recognition relies predominantly on deep learning models that achieve strong performance but offer limited insight into the vocal characteristics driving emotion, of- ten overlooking the singing voice as a distinct signal. We propose an interpretable singing emotion recognition framework using a compact set of singing-specific descriptors across prosody, spec- tral features, phonetics, and vocal technique.
