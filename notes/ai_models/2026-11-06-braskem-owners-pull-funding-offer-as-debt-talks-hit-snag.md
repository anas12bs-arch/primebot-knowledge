# Braskem Owners Pull Funding Offer as Debt Talks Hit Snag

Talks between Brazil’s Braskem SA and its creditors regarding an out-of-court debt rework have soured, with shareholders taking a proposed capital injection key to a deal off the table if talks stand as they are, according to people familiar with the matter.
