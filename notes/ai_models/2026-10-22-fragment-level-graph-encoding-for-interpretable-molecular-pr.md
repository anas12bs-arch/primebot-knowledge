# Fragment-Level Graph Encoding for Interpretable Molecular Property Prediction

Graph neural networks (GNNs) are among the standard tools for molecular property prediction, but the atom-level explanations of their predictions are hard for medicinal chemists to act on. We propose a change of input representation rather than a new model: molecules are encoded as fragment-level graphs from BRICS decomposition, where each node is a synthetically meaningful fragment whose features are the element-wise sum of its atoms' features.
