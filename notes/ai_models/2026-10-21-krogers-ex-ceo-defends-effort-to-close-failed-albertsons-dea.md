# Kroger’s Ex-CEO Defends Effort to Close Failed Albertsons Deal

Kroger Co.’s former chief executive officer testified in court that the grocery giant did what it was required to do to secure regulatory approval for a $24.6 billion merger with Albertsons Co., which is now demanding damages after the deal was blocked for antitrust reasons.
