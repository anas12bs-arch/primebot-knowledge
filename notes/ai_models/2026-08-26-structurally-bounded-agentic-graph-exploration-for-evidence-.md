# Structurally-bounded Agentic Graph Exploration for Evidence-Grounded Scholarly DeepSearch

We present Crase, a bounded and inspectable alternative to deep research agents for scholarly search. Instead of an open-ended search loop, Crase queries a search engine once for seed papers, expands them along their 1.5-hop citation neighborhood, prunes citation edges whose claims lack entailment support, and ranks the remaining papers with a recency-aware random walk. This makes the candidate set, the reason each paper is kept, and the stopping condition explicit and fixed before inference. On
