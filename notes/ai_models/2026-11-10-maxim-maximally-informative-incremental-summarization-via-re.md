# MaxIM: Maximally Informative Incremental Summarization via Reinforcement Learning

AI systems must often maintain bounded memory of unbounded interaction histories. However, optimizing the memory representation, and its updates, for downstream task utility is difficult for standard reinforcement learning (RL) due to severe credit assignment bottlenecks induced by sparse, delayed rewards.
