# AI May Transform How Humans Understand Animals

Researchers are using AI to analyze vast collections of animal vocalizations, looking for patterns that could help humans better understand how species such as crows communicate. Bloomberg Weekend Senior Writer Morgan Meaker is on Bloomberg This Weekend and says the technology could reshape humans’ relationship with animals, but researchers warn it also raises ethical questions about privacy, exploitation and how those discoveries might be used.
