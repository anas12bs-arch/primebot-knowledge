# India Stocks Top Indonesia as Asia’s Least-Favored in BofA Poll

India has replaced Indonesia as Asia’s least-preferred stock market in a survey of fund managers by Bank of America Corp., signaling growing caution toward a market that’s among the world’s worst performers this year.
