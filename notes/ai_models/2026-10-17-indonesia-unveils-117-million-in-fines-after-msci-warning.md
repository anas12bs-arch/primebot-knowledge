# Indonesia Unveils $117 Million in Fines After MSCI Warning

Indonesia announced about $117 million in fines tied to manipulative market practices involving three companies, hours after global index provider MSCI Inc. said it was weighing a downgrade of the country’s stock market over investability concerns.
