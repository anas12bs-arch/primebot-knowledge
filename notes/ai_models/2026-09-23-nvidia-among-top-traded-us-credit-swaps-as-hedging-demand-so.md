# Nvidia Among Top Traded US Credit Swaps as Hedging Demand Soars

Derivatives tied to Nvidia Corp. are now among the most traded in the US credit default swaps market, as investors seek to hedge their exposure to the firm’s debt in the wake of June’s $25 billion bond sale.
