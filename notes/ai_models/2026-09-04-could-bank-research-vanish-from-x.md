# Could Bank Research Vanish From X?

Morgan Stanley is cracking down on X users sharing its research, filing copyright complaints against at least 16 posts and temporarily locking a prominent strategist’s account. The unusual move is raising fresh questions about fair use, and whether Wall Street’s favorite forum for sharing charts, research and market ideas could be forced to change. (Source: Bloomberg)
