# Angola Plans to Cut Fuel Subsidies Without Price Increases

Angola, sub-Saharan Africa’s second-largest oil producer, plans to cut spending on fuel subsidies in 2027 without significant increases in pump prices, relying instead on other measures to reduce costs.
