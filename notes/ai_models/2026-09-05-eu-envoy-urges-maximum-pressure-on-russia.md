# EU Envoy Urges Maximum Pressure on Russia

EU Ambassador to the United States Jovita Neliupšienė joins Bloomberg This Weekend and discusses with hosts Christina Ruffini and Jeff Mason that Europe must keep supporting Ukraine while applying “maximum pressure” on Moscow because Russia has not changed its war aims. The comments come as US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner visit Moscow before heading to Kyiv in an effort to revive stalled peace negotiations. (Source: Bloomberg)
