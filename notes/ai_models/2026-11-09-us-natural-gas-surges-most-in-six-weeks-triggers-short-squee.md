# US Natural Gas Surges Most in Six Weeks, Triggers Short Squeeze

US natural gas futures jumped by as much as 8.3%, the most since September, after forecasts for cooler weather along the Atlantic seaboard gave prices an upward push and forced hedge funds to unwind bearish bets.
