# Apollo Is Focused on Investment Grade, De Giorgi Says

Diego De Giorgi, partner and head of EMEA at Apollo Global Management, says the firm is focused on the global industrial renaissance and is particularly focused on investment-grade and the short end of curve. Speaking to Anna Edwards at Bloomberg's Global Credit Forum in London, he also says he expects a higher rate environment in 2027 alongside what he describes as the largest capex cycle in history.  (Source: Bloomberg)
