Hollywood is having its best year since 2019 as moviegoers return to theaters in force. Global ticket sales are up 15% from last year, with five films topping the billion-dollar mark. Daniel Loria, Senior Vice President at The Boxoffice Company, discusses what’s driving the box office comeback and whether the momentum can last. (Source: Bloomberg)
