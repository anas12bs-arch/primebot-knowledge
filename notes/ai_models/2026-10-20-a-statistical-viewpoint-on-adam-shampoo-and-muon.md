# A Statistical Viewpoint on Adam, Shampoo and Muon

We provide new statistical insights into designing stochastic optimization methods. By modeling the gradient via a normal distribution, we argue that the stochastic gradient should be whitened using its covariance. We give an online method for estimating the mean and variance of the gradients based on regularized likelihood or, equivalently, through a maximum a posteriori estimate.
