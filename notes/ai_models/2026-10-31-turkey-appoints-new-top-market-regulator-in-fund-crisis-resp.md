# Turkey Appoints New Top Market Regulator in Fund Crisis Response

Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan appointed Muhsin Atci, head of the nation’s tax inspection board, as chief of Turkey’s market regulator to help deal with an investment fund crisis that rocked financial markets.
