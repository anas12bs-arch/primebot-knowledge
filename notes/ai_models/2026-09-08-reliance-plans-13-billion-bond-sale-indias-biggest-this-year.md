# Reliance Plans $1.3 Billion Bond Sale, India’s Biggest This Year

Billionaire Mukesh Ambani’s Reliance Industries Ltd. is planning to raise as much as 125 billion rupees ($1.3 billion) via local-currency bonds as early as next week, people familiar with the matter said, which would make it the largest listed deal in the market this year.
