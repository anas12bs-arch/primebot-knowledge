# Jersey Mike's CEO on 3Q Earnings, Expansion Plans

Jersey Mike’s shares rose after the company beat third-quarter earnings expectations. CEO Charlie Morrison, discussing the brand’s first full earnings report since going public, said same-store sales increased about 3% and he believes the growth is sustainable. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close" on October 28, 2026. (Source: Bloomberg)
