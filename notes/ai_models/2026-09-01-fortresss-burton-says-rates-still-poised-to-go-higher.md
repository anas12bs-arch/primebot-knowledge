# Fortress’s Burton Says Rates Still Poised to Go Higher

Global bonds slumped as rising oil prices fueled inflation fears and bets on Fed rate hikes. Fortress Chief Strategist Elizabeth Burton says rates are still poised to go higher, arguing the underlying problem won’t be fixed without a fiscal response. She speaks on Bloomberg Open Interest.
