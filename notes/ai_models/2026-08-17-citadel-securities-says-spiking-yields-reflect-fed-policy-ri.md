# Citadel Securities Says Spiking Yields Reflect Fed Policy Risk

The Federal Reserve’s reluctance to tighten monetary policy after a prolonged period of above-target inflation is keeping long-term bond yields at multiyear highs, posing a broader risk to markets, according to Citadel Securities.
