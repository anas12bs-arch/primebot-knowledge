# Fort Trump Will Come to Poland, Says President Nawrocki

Poland President Karol Nawrocki says it's just a matter of time before a permanent US military base is built in his country. Nawrocki says he hopes "Fort Trump" is completed before Trump's term ends in 2029. Nawrocki speaks exclusively to Bloomberg's Francine Lacqua on the sidelines of UN General Assembly meeting in New York. (Source: Bloomberg)
