# Optimal Byzantine-resilient Federated Learning with User-level Differential Privacy

Byzantine fault captures the worst-case behavior of adversarial clients, while differential privacy (DP) provides statistical guarantees against privacy loss. In particular, user-level DP guards a client's entire contribution and provides more realistic protection against information leakage.
