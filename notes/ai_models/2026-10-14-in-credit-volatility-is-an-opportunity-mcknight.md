Drew McKnight, Co-CEO and managing partner of Fortress Investment Group, joins Dani Burger on "Bloomberg Deals." They speak CAIS Summit in Beverly Hills, California about the immense capital needed for data centers, the performance of private credit investments, and the potential defaults and restructurings due to stresses in the economy.
