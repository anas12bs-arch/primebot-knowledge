# South African Bank Absa Expands to Dubai

South Africa’s Absa Group has expanded to Dubai’s DIFC. The bank aims to use the location to better support trade and investment flows between Gulf and African markets. Absa International Chief Executive Cheryl Buss spoke to Bloomberg's Chief Africa Correspondent Jennifer Zabasajja on the expansion. (Source: Bloomberg)
