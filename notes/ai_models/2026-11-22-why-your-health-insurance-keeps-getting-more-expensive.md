# Why Your Health Insurance Keeps Getting More Expensive

The average employer-sponsored family health plan now costs more than $28,000 a year, about the price of a new car, and premiums are expected to climb even faster in 2027. As costs rise, more of the bill is coming out of workers’ paychecks, and health care has become a top budget concern for CFOs.  (Source: Bloomberg)
