# UK Inflation Accelerates to Highest Level in a Year

Consumer prices rose 3.6% from a year earlier after a surge in energy bills and motor fuel costs, the Office for National Statistics said on Wednesday. It was in line with economists’ expectations. Bloomberg's Sam Unsted breaks down the numbers. (Source: Bloomberg)
