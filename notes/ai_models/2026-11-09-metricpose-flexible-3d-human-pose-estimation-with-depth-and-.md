# MetricPose: Flexible 3D Human Pose Estimation with Depth and Geometric Cues

In this paper, we present a 3D human pose estimator that predicts metrically accurate human joint positions in the camera coordinate frame. This task requires recovering the configuration of the body joints, scale, and metric camera-local placement. In monocular images, body size and camera distance are coupled through perspective projection and therefore cannot be determined uniquely from image alone.
