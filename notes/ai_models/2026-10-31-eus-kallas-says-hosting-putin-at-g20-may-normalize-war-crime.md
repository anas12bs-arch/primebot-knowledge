# EU’s Kallas Says Hosting Putin at G20 May Normalize War Crimes

Vladimir Putin’s possible attendance at the Group of 20 leaders’ summit in Florida in December would risk regularizing Russia’s return to international forums while its war in Ukraine continues, according to the European Union’s top diplomat Kaja Kallas.
