# Trafigura Snaps Up Copper in Asia as China Faces Supply Crunch

Trafigura Group has been buying large volumes of copper to boost sales into Asia as local supplies run low, adding to a scramble for metal that’s pushed regional premiums for spot cargoes to record highs.
