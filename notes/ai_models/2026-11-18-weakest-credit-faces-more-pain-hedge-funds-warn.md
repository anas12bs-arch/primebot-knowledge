# Weakest Credit Faces More Pain, Hedge Funds Warn

Hedge fund investors warned that stress is building across credit markets, with the weakest CCC borrowers particularly vulnerable as higher rates, loose documentation and deteriorating underwriting raise default risks. They also pointed to emerging strains in private credit and speculative AI financing, arguing that seemingly isolated pockets of weakness may be part of a broader repricing. (Source: Bloomberg)
