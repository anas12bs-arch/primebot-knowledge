# Banco BPM Says Paschi's Offer 'Does Not Recognize a Premium'

Banco BPM indicated skepticism toward the takeover bid it received from rival Banca Monte dei Paschi, saying the price offered was pretty much the same as its market valuation. Bloomberg's Tommaso Ebhardt reports from Milan. (Source: Bloomberg)
