# Ukraine Peace Push Turns to NATO and Donbas

Bloomberg Economics defense lead Becca Wasser tells Bloomberg This Weekend that a US proposal linking NATO membership to Ukrainian concessions in the Donbas represents movement in peace talks but faces significant political and security hurdles. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Wasser also examines Ukraine’s battlefield pressures and how the US could reshape its military presence in the Middle East after the war with Iran.
