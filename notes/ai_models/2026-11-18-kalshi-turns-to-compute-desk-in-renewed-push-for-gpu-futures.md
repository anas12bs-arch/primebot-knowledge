# Kalshi Turns to Compute Desk in Renewed Push for GPU Futures

Kalshi Inc. is working with index provider Compute Desk to launch futures contracts tied to the cost of computing power used for artificial intelligence, pushing into what exchanges see as a potentially lucrative new asset class, according to people familiar with the matter.
