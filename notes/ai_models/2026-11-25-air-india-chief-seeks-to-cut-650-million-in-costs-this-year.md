# Air India Chief Seeks to Cut $650 Million in Costs This Year

Air India Ltd. is looking to cut costs by 60 billion rupees ($650 million) this financial year as its new Chief Executive Officer outlined initiatives to revive the Tata Group-owned airline, which posted a record loss in the year ended March, people familiar with the matter said.
