# Treasury Hasn't Done Enough to Lower Yields, iCapital's Suzuki Says

Dan Suzuki, global investment strategist at iCapital, discusses the Treasury's recent attempts to lower long-term yields, arguing that the measures taken so far are insufficient and largely symbolic. Speaking on "Bloomberg Surveillance," Suzuki also says the Federal Reserve will have to start hiking rates at some point. (Source: Bloomberg)
