# The Entrepreneurs Behind Poppi Start Over

Poppi founders Allison and Stephen Ellsworth tell Bloomberg This Weekend how they turned a farmers market business into a nearly $2 billion beverage brand and what they learned from the decade-long journey. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Lisa Mateo, the couple explains why they are starting over with Jetty, a new hydration brand, and how lessons about social media, hiring and building a team are shaping their second act.
