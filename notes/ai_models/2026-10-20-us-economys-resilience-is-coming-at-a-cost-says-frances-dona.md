# US Economy's Resilience is 'Coming at a Cost,' Says Frances Donald

Treasuries are set to keep falling out of favor as an equity hedge amid a return to a persistently elevated inflation regime. 
Frances Donald, Chief Economist at RBC, breaks down this elevated inflation regime and what it means for wages, the labor market, and US economy as a whole. (Source: Bloomberg)
