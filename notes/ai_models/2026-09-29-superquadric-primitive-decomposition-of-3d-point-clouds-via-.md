# Superquadric Primitive Decomposition of 3D point clouds via Geometric-Aware Inlier Refinement

The decomposition of 3D point clouds into interpretable geometric primitives remains a longstanding challenge in Computer Vision and Computer Graphics. Among the available representations, superquadrics offer a compact and expressive model capable of capturing a wide range of shapes. However, their estimation is inherently challenging, as it requires solving a non-linear optimization problem and is particularly sensitive to noise, outliers, and overlapping structures.
