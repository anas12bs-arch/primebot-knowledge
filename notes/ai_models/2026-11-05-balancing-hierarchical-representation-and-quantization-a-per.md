# Balancing hierarchical representation and quantization: A performance trade-off in text embeddings

Hierarchical text embedding models offer variable-length representations, while quantization reduces numerical precision; both aim to improve computational efficiency. This work is an empirical benchmark study of how these two orthogonal, already-established techniques interact.
