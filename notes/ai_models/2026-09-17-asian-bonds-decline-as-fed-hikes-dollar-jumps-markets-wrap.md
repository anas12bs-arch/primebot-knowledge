# Asian Bonds Decline as Fed Hikes, Dollar Jumps: Markets Wrap

Shorter-dated Asian sovereign bonds fell, tracking moves in Treasuries after the Federal Reserve raised interest rates for the first time since 2023 and signaled further tightening to curb inflation. The dollar gained the most since June.
