# Kyiv Allies See War Going Into 2027 After Envoy Meetings

Melinda Haring, nonresident senior fellow at the Atlantic Council, said the war in Ukraine shows no signs of slowing and could drag on for another three to four years. Haring said that Putin is hoping to run out the clock on Ukraine by getting into complicated negotiations and undermining the idea of any Ukrainian sovereignty.   (Source: Bloomberg)
