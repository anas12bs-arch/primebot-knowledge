# Black Sea Chaos Is Forcing Russian Farmers to Plant Less Wheat

Some Russian farmers plan to plant less winter wheat as flows from the country’s crucial Black Sea ports remain stalled, creating a domestic glut and setting the stage for a smaller 2027 harvest from the world’s top shipper.
