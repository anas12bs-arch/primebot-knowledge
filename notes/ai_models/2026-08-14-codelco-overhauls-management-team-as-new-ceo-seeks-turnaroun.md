# Codelco Overhauls Management Team as New CEO Seeks Turnaround

Codelco is overhauling management and splitting oversight of its operations into northern and central-southern units as new Chief Executive Officer Jorge Gómez seeks to stabilize mines and finances at one of the world’s largest copper producers.
