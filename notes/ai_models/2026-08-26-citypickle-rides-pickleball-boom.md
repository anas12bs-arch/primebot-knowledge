# CityPickle Rides Pickleball Boom

CityPickle hosts the Major League Pickleball Championship finals at Wollman Rink in Central Park. CityPickle has been expanding rapidly this year, opening a flagship location in Times Square and another in Boca Raton, Florida, while serving more than 190,000 customers through its app. CityPickle Co-CEO Mary Cannon discusses the explosive growth of pickleball on Bloomberg Open Interest.
