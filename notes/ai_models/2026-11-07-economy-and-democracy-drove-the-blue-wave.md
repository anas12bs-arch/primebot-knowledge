# Economy and Democracy Drove the Blue Wave

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Rutgers University's Ashley Koning says affordability, concerns about democracy and government corruption helped drive voters toward Democrats, with significant shifts among Latino, young and independent voters. Koning tells hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini that Democrats now face pressure to deliver tangible results on affordability before the 2028 election cycle. (Source: Bloomberg)
