# ViRA: Toward Ultra-High-Resolution Visual Agents via Cross-Input Relative Policy Optimization

Visual agents answer questions about ultra-high-resolution photos by zooming into the relevant regions. However, existing agents often answer from common sense instead of reading the evidence. Reinforcement learning (RL) on the final answer cannot correct this, because a guess earns the same reward as reading.
