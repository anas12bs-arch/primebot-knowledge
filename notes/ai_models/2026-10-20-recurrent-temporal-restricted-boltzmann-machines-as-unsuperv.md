# Recurrent Temporal Restricted Boltzmann Machines as Unsupervised Encoders for Biomechanical Movement Sequences

Biomechanical movement recordings are continuous, high-dimensional time series, and analyzing them without manually labeling them requires representations that capture how movements evolve over time. Most unsupervised representation-learning pipelines, however, treat each observation frame independently and discard this temporal structure, while energy-based models that could capture it have not extensively been adapted to biomechanical signals.
