# India's NIIF Eyes $800M Data Center Investment

The National Investment and Infrastructure Fund (NIIF) is likely to draw as much as $3.2 billion in investments for one of its entities and between $700 million to $800 million of that will go to data centers, Chief Executive Officer Sanjiv Aggarwal told Bloomberg's Yvonne Man in an interview on the sidelines of the Bloomberg New Economy Forum in New Delhi. (Source: Bloomberg)
