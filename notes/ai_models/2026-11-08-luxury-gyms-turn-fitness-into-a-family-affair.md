# Luxury Gyms Turn Fitness Into a Family Affair

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg Pursuits writer Hannah Howard explains how high end fitness clubs are courting families with everything from child care and swimming lessons to homework help and activities for older kids. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Howard discusses why parents are willing to pay hundreds or even more than $1,000 a month for clubs that combine workouts, activities and family time under one roof.
