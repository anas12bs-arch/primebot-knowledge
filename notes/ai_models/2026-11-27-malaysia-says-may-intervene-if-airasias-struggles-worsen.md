# Malaysia Says May Intervene If AirAsia’s Struggles Worsen

Malaysia may consider stepping in to support AirAsia Group Bhd. if the airline’s financial difficulties worsen, Second Finance Minister Amir Hamzah Azizan said, as the government seeks to protect domestic air connectivity.
