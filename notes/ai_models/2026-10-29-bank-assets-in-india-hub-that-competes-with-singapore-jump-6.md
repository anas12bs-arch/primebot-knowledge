# Bank Assets in India Hub That Competes With Singapore Jump 60%

Banking assets in India’s international financial hub have surged about 60% since June, a top official said, as growth in the nation’s credit markets boosts GIFT City that’s competing with Singapore and Hong Kong in attracting foreign capital.
