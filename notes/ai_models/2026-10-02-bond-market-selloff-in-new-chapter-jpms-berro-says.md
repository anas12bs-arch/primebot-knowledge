# Bond Market Selloff in ‘New Chapter,’ JPM’s Berro Says

Kelsey Berro, fixed income portfolio manager at JPMorgan Asset Management, discusses recent changes in the bond market selloff, saying it now feels “very technically-driven, very momentum-driven.” Berro says on “Bloomberg Surveillance” that she expects investors to refocus soon on the fundamentals and valuations of the market.
