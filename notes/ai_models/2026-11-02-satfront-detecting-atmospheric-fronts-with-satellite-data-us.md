# SATFRONT: Detecting Atmospheric Fronts with Satellite Data using Machine Learning

Atmospheric fronts are associated with much of the high-impact weather in the mid-latitudes, yet their identification and classification remain largely manual and subjective. Existing deep learning approaches rely primarily on gridded reanalysis or forecast data, limiting their applicability to satellite observations available for time-critical applications. We introduce SATFRONT, a machine learning framework for detecting cold, warm, and occluded fronts over Europe from geostationary satellite
