# Thanksgiving Dinner Is Moving to Restaurants

Bloomberg Pursuits Food Editor Kate Krader tells Bloomberg This Weekend that more Americans are choosing restaurants for Thanksgiving as higher grocery costs, cooking stress and cleanup make dining out increasingly attractive. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Krader explains how restaurants are responding with traditional feasts and unconventional menus as Thanksgiving reservations continue to rise.
