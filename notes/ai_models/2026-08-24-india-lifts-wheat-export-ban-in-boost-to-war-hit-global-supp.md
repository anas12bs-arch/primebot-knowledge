# India Lifts Wheat Export Ban in Boost to War Hit Global Supply

India, the world’s second-biggest wheat producer, has scrapped a years-long ban on exports of the grain, a move that could ease global supplies that are under strain from escalating attacks between Russia and Ukraine.
