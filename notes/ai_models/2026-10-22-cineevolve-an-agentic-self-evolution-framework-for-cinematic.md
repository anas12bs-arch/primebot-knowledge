# CineEvolve: An Agentic Self-Evolution Framework for Cinematic Multi-Shot Video Generation

Cinematic multi-shot video generation requires both narrative planning and iterative refinement. Existing methods either lack narrative reasoning or rely on feed-forward workflows without policy updates. In this paper, we propose CineEvolve, an agentic self-evolution framework for cinematic multi-shot video generation that unifies a Director and a Critic within a single vision-language policy.
