# Blackstone, Apollo May Be Allowed to Buy NFL Minority Stakes

Bloomberg has learned that Blackstone Inc. and a unit of Apollo Global Management Inc. may be allowed to buy minority stakes in NFL teams at some point. Talks are preliminary and there's no deal yet. Bloomberg's Scarlet Fu reports.  (Source: Bloomberg)
