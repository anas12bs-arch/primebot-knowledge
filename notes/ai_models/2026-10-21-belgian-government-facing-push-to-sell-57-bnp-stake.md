# Belgian Government Facing Push to Sell 5.7% BNP Stake

Belgium is ramping up efforts to divest from stakes it amassed in banks during the global financial crisis, with some in the government pushing to prioritize the sale of its 5.7% stake in BNP Paribas SA.
