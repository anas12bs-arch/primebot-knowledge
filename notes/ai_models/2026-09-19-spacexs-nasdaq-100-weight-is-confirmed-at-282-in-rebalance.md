# SpaceX’s Nasdaq 100 Weight Is Confirmed at 2.82% in Rebalance

SpaceX will make up 2.82% of the Nasdaq 100 when the index’s quarterly rebalance takes effect Monday, according to data obtained by Bloomberg, locking in a sharp increase from its current weighting of about 1.28%.
