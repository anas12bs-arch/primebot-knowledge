# Rubio and Brazil Foreign Minister to Meet After Election Tension

Secretary of State Marco Rubio and his Brazilian counterpart agreed to meet in the coming weeks, according to people familiar with the matter, after President Donald Trump’s decision to grant the nation’s defeated right-wing candidate Flávio Bolsonaro refuge inflamed tensions.
