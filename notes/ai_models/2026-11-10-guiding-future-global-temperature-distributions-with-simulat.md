# Guiding future global temperature distributions with simulations and observations

Assessing climate risks requires projections of daily temperature distributions, including rare and spatially widespread extremes. Climate simulations are costly and biased, while correcting them toward observations risks suppressing inter-model differences. We train a single machine-learning emulator, a conditional diffusion model, on seventeen CMIP6 models and ERA5 reanalysis to generate spatially coherent daily temperature fields conditioned on model identity, date, and CO2-equivalent concent
