# JetBlue Promises It Will Return to Profitability

JetBlue Airways says it will turn a profit again next year despite losing 60 cents a share in the third quarter. The New York-based carrier hasn't had a profitable year since 2019. Meanwhile, Boeing is still hoping to turn around its commercial business. Bloomberg's Sid Philip reports.  (Source: Bloomberg)
