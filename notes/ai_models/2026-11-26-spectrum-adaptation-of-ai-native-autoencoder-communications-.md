# Spectrum Adaptation of AI-Native Autoencoder Communications with Transfer Learning

End-to-end physical-layer learning replaces separate modulation, coding, and detection operations with an autoencoder (AE) that consists of a jointly trained encoder-decoder pair at the transmitter and the receiver. This paper studies AI-native communications with AEs and quantifies the sensitivity to training-test mismatch in channel models.
