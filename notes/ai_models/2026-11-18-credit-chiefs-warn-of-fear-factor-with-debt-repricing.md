# Credit Chiefs Warn of ‘Fear Factor’ With Debt Repricing

Credit chiefs warned that a sharp repricing across fixed-income markets is likely to continue as interest rates remain elevated, putting greater pressure on weaker borrowers. Speaking at Bloomberg’s Global Credit Forum in London, investors point to growing fear in credit markets, rapidly shifting sentiment and the prospect of a higher-rate environment ahead.
