# Poland Probes Krakow Shooting as Terrorism

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg News reporter Piotr Skolimowski discusses the investigation into the deadly Krakow shooting and the far right ideology authorities say may have motivated the attack. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Skolimowski explains how the shooting comes amid heightened political tensions in Poland and growing divisions over the war in Ukraine.
