# Peltz’s Trian Offers $1.4 Billion to Take Wendy’s Private

Nelson Peltz’s Trian Fund Management offered to buy out Wendy’s Co. for about $1.4 billion, saying the burger chain will be better off attempting a multiyear turnaround bid as a private company.
