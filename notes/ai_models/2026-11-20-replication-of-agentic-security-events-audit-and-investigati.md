# Replication of Agentic Security Events: Audit and Investigation (ROGUE-AI)

News media reports routinely describe failures of these agents (e.g., Replit's coding agent deleting a production database), but a report often only says what went wrong and rarely (in)validates the incident or finds its root cause.
