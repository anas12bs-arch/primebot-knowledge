# NYU's Suzy Welch on Career Ambition & Gen Z's Work Paradox

NYU Stern professor Suzy Welch examines a growing contradiction among Gen Z: young workers increasingly value achievement and career success while also wanting to spend less of their lives at work. Welch discusses shifting attitudes toward ambition, work-life balance and what the trend means for employers. She speaks with Bloomberg's Tom Keene and Paul Sweeney. (Source: Bloomberg)
