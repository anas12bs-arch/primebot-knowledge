# Can Scotland’s Abundant Wind Power a Data Center Boom?

Scotland produces far more wind power than its grid can handle, forcing turbines to be shut down when supply exceeds demand or transmission capacity. Developers now see a possible solution: build data centers close to the surplus power, turning wasted wind into compute capacity for AI and the digital economy.  (Source: Bloomberg)
