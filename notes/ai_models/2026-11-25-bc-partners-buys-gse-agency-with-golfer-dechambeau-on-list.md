# BC Partners Buys GSE Agency With Golfer DeChambeau on List

BC Partners’ credit arm has agreed to buy a majority stake in GSE Worldwide, a sports marketing agency which represents athletes such as golf celebrity Bryson DeChambeau and former NFL and MLB star Bo Jackson.
