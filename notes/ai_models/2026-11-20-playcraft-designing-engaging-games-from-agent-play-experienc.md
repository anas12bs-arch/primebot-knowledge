# PlayCraft: Designing Engaging Games from Agent Play Experience

Recent AI agents can generate playable games, but these games are not necessarily engaging. Mastery is a key source of engagement, yet designing for it requires extensive human play and tuning. We propose PlayCraft, an automated framework that uses a vision-based agent to play games and draws on its detailed play experience to revise them for mastery.
