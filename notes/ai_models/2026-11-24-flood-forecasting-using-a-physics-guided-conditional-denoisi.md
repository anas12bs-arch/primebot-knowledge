# Flood forecasting using a physics-guided conditional denoising diffusion probabilistic model

High-resolution flood forecasting with full two-dimensional shallow water equation (SWE) solvers remains computationally expensive, even on GPUs. We develop a conditional denoising diffusion probabilistic model (DDPM) that forecasts the evolution of 30 m water-depth fields over a four-day horizon for the Des Plaines River watershed, Illinois.
