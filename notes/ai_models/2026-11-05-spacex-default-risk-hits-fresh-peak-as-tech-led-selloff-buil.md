# SpaceX Default Risk Hits Fresh Peak as Tech-Led Selloff Builds

The cost to protect SpaceX debt against default hit a record high Thursday, extending a move sparked by US election results that raised political risks for Elon Musk’s Texas-based companies. Shares in Musk’s SpaceX and Tesla Inc. tumbled amid a broad equity market selloff, led by a rout in chipmakers.
