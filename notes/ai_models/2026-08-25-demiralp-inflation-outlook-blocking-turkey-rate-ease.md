# Demiralp: Inflation Outlook Blocking Turkey Rate Ease

Turkey's central bank said it would resume weekly repo auctions at its 37% policy rate. The decision is a step towards normalizing funding conditions as the central bank bets the worst of the Iran War's economic fallout has passed. Selva Demiralp, Professor of Economics at Koç University spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on the move from the Central Bank & its impact on inflation in Turkey. (Source: Bloomberg)
