# Energy Driven Inflation Complicates Fed Rate Call

PNC Chief Investment Officer Amanda Agati is on Bloomberg This Weekend and says the Fed faces a difficult decision as energy-driven inflation pushes markets toward expectations for higher rates, warning that further tightening could risk a policy error. Agati also tells hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini investors are also adjusting to the prospect of a longer Iran war, while strong corporate earnings have pushed concerns about AI spending further into the background.
