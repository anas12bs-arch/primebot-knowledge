# A$^3$PreCore: Robust Rigid Registration for Native FLAIR-Space Brain-Mask Transfer

Reliable brain-mask transfer into native fluid-attenuated inversion recovery (FLAIR) space can fail when T1-to-FLAIR rigid registration is unstable. We developed A$^3$PreCore, a six-degree-of-freedom rigid-registration strategy that aligns the full bias-corrected T1-weighted image to native FLAIR using normalized mutual information while preserving the established T1-space anatomical mask.
