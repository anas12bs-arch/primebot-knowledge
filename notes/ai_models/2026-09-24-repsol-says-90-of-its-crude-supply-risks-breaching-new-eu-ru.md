# Repsol Says 90% of Its Crude Supply Risks Breaching New EU Rules

Repsol SA anticipates about 90% of its crude oil supplies falling short of new European Union methane requirements that are due to take effect in January, the latest in a series of warnings from the industry that the rules could threaten the region’s energy supplies.
