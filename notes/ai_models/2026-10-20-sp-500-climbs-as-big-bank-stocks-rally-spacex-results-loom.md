# S&P 500 Climbs as Big Bank Stocks Rally, SpaceX Results Loom

The tech-heavy Nasdaq 100 Index ended the day with a slight decline after South Korean neocloud company Elice Inc. tanked in its trading debut, spurring concern about the sustainability of the global AI boom.
