# Ed Yardeni Cuts S&P 500 View to 7,900 on Downturn Risks

Ed Yardeni, one of the biggest stock bulls on Wall Street, is slashing his year-end forecast for the S&amp;P 500 Index a month after raising it, citing increasing risks of a downturn in the next three to six months.
