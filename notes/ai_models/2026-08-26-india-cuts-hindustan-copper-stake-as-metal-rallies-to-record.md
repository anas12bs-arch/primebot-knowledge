# India Cuts Hindustan Copper Stake as Metal Rallies to Record

India’s government is selling about a 6% stake in Hindustan Copper Ltd., capitalizing on strong domestic liquidity and record global prices of the industrial metal as it seeks to meet its annual divestment target.
