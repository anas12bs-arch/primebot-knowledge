# US Futures Climb With SpaceX Results Helping Boost AI Sentiment

The S&amp;P 500 Index pared early-session gains spurred by solid earnings from SpaceX and Super Micro Computer Inc., which had bolstered bullish views of the artificial-intelligence trade. Healthcare and communcations stocks led declining sectors in Wednesday trading. Attention turns next to earnings from tech giants Meta Platforms Inc. and Tesla Inc., due after the bell.
