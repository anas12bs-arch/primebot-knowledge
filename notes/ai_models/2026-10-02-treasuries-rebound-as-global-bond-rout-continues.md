# Treasuries Rebound as Global Bond Rout Continues

US Treasuries rebounded from a global bond selloff as pressure in European markets fueled demand for haven assets, pulling 10-year yields down from a 24-year high. Bloomberg's Ruth Carson reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
