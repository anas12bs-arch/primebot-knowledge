# Kospi Rout Wipes $151 Billion From Korea Pension Fund’s Assets

South Korea’s National Pension Service saw its assets shrink by about 202 trillion won ($151.4 billion) in the third quarter as a stock-market retreat eroded some of the out-sized gains accumulated earlier this year.
