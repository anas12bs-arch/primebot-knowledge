# RBA Raised Key Rate to Counter Upside Price Risk, Remains Alert

Australia’s central bank raised its key interest rate two weeks ago because upside risks to inflation were coming to pass and waiting could prove costly, while adding the outlook would be heavily influenced by the ongoing conflict in the Middle East.
