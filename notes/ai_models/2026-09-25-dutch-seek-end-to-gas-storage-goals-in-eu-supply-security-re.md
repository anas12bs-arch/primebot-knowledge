# Dutch Seek End to Gas Storage Goals in EU Supply Security Revamp

The Netherlands wants to scrap mandatory gas storage targets under a revamp of the European Union’s energy supply security rules after spending nearly €1 billion ($1.1 billion) this year to fill the country’s reserves.
