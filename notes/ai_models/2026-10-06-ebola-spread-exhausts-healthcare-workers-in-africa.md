# Ebola Spread Exhausts Healthcare Workers in Africa

20 weeks into the Ebola virus outbreak, health workers have managed to tamp down some of the early hotspot but the virus is still spreading in the Democratic Republic of Congo, putting experts under mounting pressure. Jennifer Zabasajja, Bloomberg’s Chief Africa correspondent reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
