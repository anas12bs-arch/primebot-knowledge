# NYC Transit Agency Cuts Bond Refinancing 40% Amid Yield Spike

The Metropolitan Transportation Authority, which operates New York City’s subways, buses and commuter lines, cut a planned debt refinancing by about 40% as tax-exempt bond yields hovered near their highest levels in at least 15 years.
