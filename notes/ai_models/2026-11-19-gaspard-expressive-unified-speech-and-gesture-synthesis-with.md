# Gaspard: Expressive unified speech and gesture synthesis with continuous autoregressive flow-matching and decoupled classifier-free guidance

Human communication involves highly coordinated multimodal signals such as speech and co-speech gestures. Existing approaches for generating both modalities commonly follow a cascaded pipeline, where speech is generated first and subsequently used to drive gesture generation; speech generation is therefore independent of gestures.
