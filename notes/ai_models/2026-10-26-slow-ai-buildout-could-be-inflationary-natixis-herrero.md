# Slow AI Buildout Could Be Inflationary: Natixis' Herrero

Alicia Garcia Herrero, chief APAC economist at Natixis, tells Bloomberg TV that rapid AI adoption could be inflationary if data-center construction falls short of what is needed to meet demand for compute. (Source: Bloomberg)
