# BOE’s Mann on Hawkish Policy, Inflation, Reaction Functions

Bank of England Monetary Policy Committee member Catherine Mann discusses central bank reaction functions and managing inflation from supply shocks. She also points to research showing evidence of the immediate effect that central bank decisions can have on economic activity. Mann speaks on the sidelines of the Jackson Hole Economic Policy Symposium. (Source: Bloomberg)
