# Vietnam’s Leader Declares US Trade Deal Close

Vietnam’s top leader To Lam tells Bloomberg the nation is “very close” to a trade deal with the US. Vietnam wants to import more US items, Lam says in an exclusive interview on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly. (Source: Bloomberg)
