# Chiriseri: France’s Fiscal Picture Concern Bond Markets

Treasury yields edged higher as the recent bond rally lost some momentum. Bloomberg’s strategists say longer term, they see yields reaching fresher highs as strong US growth, higher borrowing costs and rising government debt supply will keep pressure on Treasuries. Rufaro Chiriseri, Head of Fixed Income for UK and Europe at RBC Wealth Management spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on Horizons Middle East & Africa. (Source: Bloomberg)
