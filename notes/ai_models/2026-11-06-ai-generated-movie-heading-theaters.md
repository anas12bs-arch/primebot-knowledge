# AI-Generated Movie Heading Theaters

Artificial intelligence is moving from experimental videos to the big screen. Fable Studio CEO Edward Saatchi says a team of just 30 people spent nine months creating Gods Don’t Give Gifts, an AI-generated animated feature that still relies on human directors, writers, voice actors and artists. He joins Bloomberg Businessweek Daily to discuss how AI changes the filmmaking process and what it means for Hollywood's traditional production model. (Source: Bloomberg)
