# Citadel Securities Urges SEC to Take Oversight of Some Wagers

Citadel Securities told regulators that prediction market contracts linked to publicly-traded companies should be overseen by the Securities and Exchange Commission instead of the agency that has taken responsibility for them so far, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission.
