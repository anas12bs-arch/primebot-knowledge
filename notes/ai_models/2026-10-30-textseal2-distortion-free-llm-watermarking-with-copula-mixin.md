# TextSeal2: Distortion-Free LLM Watermarking with Copula Mixing and Memoryless Races

We present TextSeal2, a distortion-free watermark for large language models that keeps its signal under speculative decoding: tokens that replace rejected drafts remain watermarked with the same keys, and the acceptance rate is unchanged. It also preserves output diversity and adds no measurable inference latency.
