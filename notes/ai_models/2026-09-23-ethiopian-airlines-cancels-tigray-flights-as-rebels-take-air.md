# Ethiopian Airlines Cancels Tigray Flights as Rebels Take Airport

Ethiopian Airlines has suspended flights to three destinations in Tigray, after reports that forces affiliated to the Tigray People’s Liberation Front had taken control of the airport in the regional capital.
