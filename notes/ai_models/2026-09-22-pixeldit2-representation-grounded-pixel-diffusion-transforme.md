# PixelDiT2: Representation-Grounded Pixel Diffusion Transformers

Recent advances in pixel-space diffusion models have narrowed the image quality gap with latent-space diffusion, but still converge more slowly and lag behind in final image quality. We argue that a key reason is the lack of an explicit representation prior: unlike latent diffusion, which usually denoises in a compact and structured latent space, pixel diffusion needs to learn denoising-friendly representations and pixel generation simultaneously from raw RGB space.
