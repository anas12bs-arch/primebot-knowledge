# JarvisGUI: Towards Cross-Device GUI Agents with Dynamic Task Composition

Real-world GUI usage frequently involves workflows that span multiple devices and platforms, requiring the transfer of intermediate results, maintenance of shared state, and coordination across heterogeneous environments.
