# China Water Environment Seeks $410 Million Loan Restructuring

China Water Environment Group, one of the nation’s largest sewage treatment firms, is seeking to restructure about $410 million of offshore loans, according to people familiar with the matter, adding to a growing number of Chinese state-backed firms facing debt challenges.
