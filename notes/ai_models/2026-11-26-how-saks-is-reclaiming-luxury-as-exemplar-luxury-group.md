# How Saks Is Reclaiming Luxury as Exemplar Luxury Group

Bloomberg’s Romaine Bostick reports on how Exemplar Luxury Group, formerly known as Saks Global, is reinventing itself for a fresh start in luxury since emerging from bankruptcy earlier this year. Exemplar Luxury Group CEO Geoffroy van Raemdonck, FIT Associate Professor Shawn Grain Carter, Telsey Advisory Group CEO Dana Telsey, and Exemplary Luxury Group President and CCO Darcy Penick share their thoughts.
