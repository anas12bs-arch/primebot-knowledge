# US Aid Cuts Drove Sharp HIV Service Drops in Africa, Study Finds

US funding cuts caused sharp and lasting declines in HIV prevention, testing and treatment services at organizations serving some of the people most vulnerable to the virus in Uganda and Zimbabwe, according to new research.
