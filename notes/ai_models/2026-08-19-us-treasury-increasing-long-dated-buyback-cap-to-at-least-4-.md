# US Treasury Increasing Long-Dated Buyback Cap to at Least $4 Billion

The US Department of the Treasury announced it will at least double the maximum size of its liquidity support buyback operations for longer-dated nominal coupon securities to at least $4 billion per operation, effective September 9, 2026. Michael McKee reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
