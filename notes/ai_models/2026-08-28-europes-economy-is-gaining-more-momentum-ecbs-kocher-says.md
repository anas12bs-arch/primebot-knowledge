# Europe’s Economy Is Gaining More Momentum, ECB’s Kocher Says

European Central Bank Governing Council member Martin Kocher says, “the European economy is more resilient than many people think, it is showing some momentum now, more momentum.” He discusses his takeaways from Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh’s speech and watching for potential signs of second-round effects in inflation over the coming months. He speaks with Lisa Abramowicz on the sidelines of the Fed’s annual economic symposium in Jackson Hole, Wyoming. (Source: Bloomberg)
