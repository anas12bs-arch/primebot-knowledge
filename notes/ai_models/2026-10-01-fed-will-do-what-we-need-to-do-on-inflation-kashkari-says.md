# Fed ‘Will Do What We Need to Do’ on Inflation, Kashkari Says

Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis President Neel Kashkari sees the US economy growing and is surprised by its resilience “in the face of tariffs and the conflict in Iran." He says on “Bloomberg Surveillance” that the central bank “will do what we need to do to get inflation back down to our target.” (Source: Bloomberg)
