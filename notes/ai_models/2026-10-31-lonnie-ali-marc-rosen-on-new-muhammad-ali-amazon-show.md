# Lonnie Ali & Marc Rosen on New Muhammad Ali Amazon Show

Lonnie Ali, Muhammad Ali's wife & Executive Producer of The Greatest, joins Marc Rosen of Authentic Brands to discuss the first authorized scripted series to take a more intimate look at Muhammad Ali beyond his boxing career. The two speak with Romaine Bostick on "The Close" on October 29, 2026. (Source: Bloomberg)
