# Sysco Draws Blowout Demand for $17 Billion Global Bond Offering

Sysco Corp. drew about $90 billion of orders for the US dollar portion of its global bond offering, underscoring strong investor appetite for both blue-chip and riskier debt to finance its purchase of cash-and-carry wholesaler Jetro Restaurant Depot LLC.
