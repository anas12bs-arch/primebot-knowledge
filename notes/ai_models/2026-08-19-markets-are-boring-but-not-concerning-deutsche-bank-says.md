# Markets Are ‘Boring’ But Not Concerning, Deutsche Bank Says

Deutsche Bank Head of European Equity Research Maximilian Uleer expresses a cautiously optimistic view on the current market environment. He highlights that volatility is at a 20-year low for this time of year, describing the market as "boring" but not concerning. Uleer speaks with Bloomberg's Lizzy Burden on "The Pulse."
