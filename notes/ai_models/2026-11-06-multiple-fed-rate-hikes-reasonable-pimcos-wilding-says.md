# Multiple Fed Rate Hikes Reasonable, Pimco's Wilding Says

Pimco Economist Tiffany Wilding says the US employment report does not change her outlook for a Federal Reserve rate hike in December. Speaking on "Bloomberg Open Interest," Wilding says another Fed rate increase in March is possible, describing the moves as a recalibration of policy. (Source: Bloomberg)
