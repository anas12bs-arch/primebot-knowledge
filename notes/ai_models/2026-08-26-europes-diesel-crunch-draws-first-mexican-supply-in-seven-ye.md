# Europe’s Diesel Crunch Draws First Mexican Supply in Seven Years

Europe’s diesel supply situation has gotten so tight that the region is drawing on imports from Mexico for the first time in seven years as the Iran war and a halt in Russian cargoes upends historical trade flows.
