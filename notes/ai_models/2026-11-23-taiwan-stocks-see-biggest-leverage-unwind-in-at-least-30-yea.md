# Taiwan Stocks See Biggest Leverage Unwind in at Least 30 Years

Taiwan stocks saw the biggest unwind of leveraged bets in at least three decades on Friday, helping to explain why the authorities’ efforts to stem the selloff in Asia’s most tech-heavy market are having a limited impact.
