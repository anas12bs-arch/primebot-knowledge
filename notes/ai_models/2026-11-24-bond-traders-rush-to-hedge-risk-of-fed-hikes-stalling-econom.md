# Bond Traders Rush to Hedge Risk of Fed Hikes Stalling Economy

Bond traders are racing to hedge the risk that the Federal Reserve’s interest-rate hikes and the stock market’s slide will significantly slow the US economy, forcing the central bank to shift back to easing monetary policy as soon as late next year.
