# Russia Agrees to Halt Recruitment of Ghanaians for Ukraine War

Russian President Vladimir Putin has agreed to halt the recruitment of Ghanaians to fight in Ukraine after President John Mahama raised concerns about the practice, according to Ghana’s foreign minister.
