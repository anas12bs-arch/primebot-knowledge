# IMF Approves Bolivia Loan Deal to Support Paz’s Economic Reforms

The International Monetary Fund approved a $1.9 billion financing program for Bolivia, including an immediate $214 million disbursement, in a package designed to help President Rodrigo Paz reverse a sharp economic slump.
