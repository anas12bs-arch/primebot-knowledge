# Robust Satisficing Ensemble: Scalable Model Aggregation Under Distribution Shifts

Standard ensemble methods can improve robustness by mitigating individual model stochasticity, but they frequently fail to generalize beyond the training distribution, leaving systems vulnerable to out-of-distribution (OOD) data. We introduce the Robust Satisficing Ensemble (RSE), a scalable probabilistic aggregation framework designed to secure model combinations by minimizing their fragility against distributional changes.
