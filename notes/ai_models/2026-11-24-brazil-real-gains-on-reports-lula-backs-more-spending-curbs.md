# Brazil Real Gains on Reports Lula Backs More Spending Curbs

The Brazilian real gained on Tuesday on reports that President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva is backing moves to curb fiscal spending, easing the concerns about the budget deficit that have dragged the currency down since his surprise re-election.
