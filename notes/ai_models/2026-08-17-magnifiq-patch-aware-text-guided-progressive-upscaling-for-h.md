# MagnifiQ: Patch-aware Text Guided Progressive Upscaling for High-Resolution Image Restoration

High-resolution image restoration from degraded inputs is challenging because it must preserve global structural consistency while recovering fine-grained local details, especially at 4K resolution where direct diffusion-based restoration is computationally expensive and prone to repeated or inconsistent textures.
