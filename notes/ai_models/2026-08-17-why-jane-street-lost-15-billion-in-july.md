# Why Jane Street Lost $15 Billion in July

Jane Street posted roughly $15 billion of losses in July, its first monthly loss in about a decade, due to its investment in Situational Awareness and wrong-way bets in Asian equity markets. Katherine Doherty reports on "Bloomberg Open Interest." (Source: Bloomberg)
