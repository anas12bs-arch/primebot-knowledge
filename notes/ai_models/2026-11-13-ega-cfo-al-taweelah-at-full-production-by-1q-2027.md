# EGA CFO: Al Taweelah At Full Production by 1Q 2027

Emirates Global Aluminium says it’s making “strong progress” on restoring production at its main smelter, which was damaged in an Iranian strike earlier this year. It comes as the firm reported 9m revenue of $5.3 billion compared to $6.3 billion y/y. EGA’s CFO, Pal Kildemo spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on Horizons Middle East & Africa. (Source: Bloomberg)
