# Data Center Buildout to Continue, Kirkland & Ellis Says

"There's a lot of rumor, but for now we hear activity continuing," says Kirkland & Ellis Partner Roald Nashi as Bloomberg's annual Global Credit Forum commences in London. He speaks with Bloomberg's Anna Edwards and Guy Johnson on "The Opening Trade." Despite the "in flux" political environment, "at the end of the day, I think these are very, very stable assets that in most cases are under long-term contracts." (Source: Bloomberg)
