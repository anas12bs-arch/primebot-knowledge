# New Zealand’s Year-End Inflation Surge Points to More Rate Hikes

New Zealand inflation likely eased slightly in the third quarter, but the respite is set to be short-lived as a weaker currency and surging oil prices drive an acceleration in the final three months of the year, building the case for rate hikes.
