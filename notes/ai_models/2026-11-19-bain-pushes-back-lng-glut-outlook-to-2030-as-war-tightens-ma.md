# Bain Pushes Back LNG Glut Outlook to 2030 as War Tightens Market

A looming global glut of liquefied natural gas has been pushed back by more than a year as the conflict in the Middle East disrupts supplies and delays projects, according to Bain &amp; Co., extending a period of tightness that’s delivering higher profits for traders.
