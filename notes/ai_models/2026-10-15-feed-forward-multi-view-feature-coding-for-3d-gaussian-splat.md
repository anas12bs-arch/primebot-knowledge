# Feed-Forward Multi-View Feature Coding for 3D Gaussian Splatting

Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) methods enable efficient reconstruction of 3D scenes from sparse multi-view images in a single forward pass, without scene-specific optimization. However, existing feed-forward scene-coding pipelines first generate a complete Gaussian representation and then encode its geometry and appearance attributes separately, often involving non-differentiable operations.
