# Gold Isn't Driven by US Inflation Says Jan van Eck

Jan van Eck, CEO of VanEck Funds joined Bloomberg Businessweek Daily from Future Proof to discuss the state of the global economy. He says he isn't worried about the $40 Trillion US debt and that Bessent and Trump are 'toying' with the market when it comes to the idea of sending $5,000 checks to Americans if they keep control of Congress.
