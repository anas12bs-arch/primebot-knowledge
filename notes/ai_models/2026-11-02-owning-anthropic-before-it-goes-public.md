# Owning Anthropic Before It Goes Public

John Cole Scott, president and & CIO of CEF Advisors, joins Scarlet Fu and Eric Balchunas on "Bloomberg ETF IQ." Anthropic filed on Oct. 26 for an IPO that’s set to take place before Thanksgiving. Anthropic is seeking to meet or exceed the amount SpaceX raised in its $86.2 billion debut, people familiar with the matter have said.
