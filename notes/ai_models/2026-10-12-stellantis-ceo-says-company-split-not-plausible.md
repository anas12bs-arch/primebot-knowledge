# Stellantis CEO Says Company Split 'Not Plausible'

Stellantis Chief Executive Officer Antonio Filosa says a Europe-US split of the company is "not plausible at all." Speaking with Bloomberg's Caroline Connan at the Paris Motor Show, he adds:  "Our strategy is based on having all the brands playing their unique role, but all of them enjoy transversal technology." (Source: Bloomberg)
