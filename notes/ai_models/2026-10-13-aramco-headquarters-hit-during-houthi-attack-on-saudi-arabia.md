# Aramco Headquarters Hit During Houthi Attack on Saudi Arabia

Saudi Aramco’s headquarters in Dhahran in eastern Saudi Arabia was hit during a missile attack by Yemen’s Houthi militants on Tuesday, causing a fire in a part of the complex, according to people with knowledge of the matter.
