# What RL on Expert-Built Tasks Teaches Nemotron 3.5 Super

We post-train NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Super (120B total, 12B active parameters) with reinforcement learning (RL) on five expert-built task datasets, one at a time: real-world software engineering, complex constraints, long-horizon tool use, professional chart understanding and enterprise document reasoning.
