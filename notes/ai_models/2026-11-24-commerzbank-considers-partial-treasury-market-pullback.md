# Commerzbank Considers Partial Treasury Market Pullback

Commerzbank is reportedly weighing whether to pare back its involvement in US Treasury trading. The German bank is weighing options including an exit from the US repo market and partnering with a broker dealer on its broader involvement in trading cash as the US prepares to introduce new Treasury clearing requirements. Arno Schuetze reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
