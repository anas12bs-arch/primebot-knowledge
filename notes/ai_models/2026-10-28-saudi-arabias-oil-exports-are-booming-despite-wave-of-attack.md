# Saudi Arabia’s Oil Exports Are Booming Despite Wave of Attacks

Saudi Arabia’s oil exports are holding near wartime highs this month despite a wave of attacks on the kingdom’s energy infrastructure and an increase in reported Iranian strikes on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, according to preliminary data from ship-tracking firms.
