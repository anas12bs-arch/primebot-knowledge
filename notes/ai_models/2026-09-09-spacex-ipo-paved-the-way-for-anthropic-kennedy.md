# SpaceX IPO Paved The Way For Anthropic: Kennedy

Lise Buyer, partner and founder at Class V Group, Ajay Shah, chairman of global technology investment banking at Deutsche Bank Securities, and Matt Kennedy, senior strategist at Renaissance Capital, joins Scarlet Fu on "Bloomberg Deals." Anthropic is set to finalize an expansion of its revolving credit facility to $15 billion, according to people familiar with the matter, clearing a hurdle before the AI firm’s public filing for its highly anticipated IPO.
