# OC-GS: Gaussian Splatting for Irregular Turntable Capture

Uneven rotation and dropped frames make equal-angle assumptions unreliable for turntable reconstruction. We present OC-GS, an object-centric Gaussian splatting that refines each image's angle while maintaining a shared camera, rotation axis, and pivot. This orbit-consistent refinement jointly optimizes image-derived geometry and angles to reconstruct objects from sparse, irregular captures. On rendered objects with 12, 8, and 6 irregularly spaced views, OC-GS achieves mean foreground PSNR scores
