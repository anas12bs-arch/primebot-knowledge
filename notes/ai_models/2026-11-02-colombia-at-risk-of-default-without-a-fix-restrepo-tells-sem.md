# Colombia at Risk of Default Without a Fix, Restrepo Tells Semana

Colombia is at risk of default if Congress fails to approve a fiscal adjustment bill, Vice President José Manuel Restrepo said in an interview with local magazine Semana, warning that the country faces a severe economic crisis without the proposed measures.
