# Transforming the New York Liberty Into WNBA Champions

Clara Wu Tsai sits down with David Rubenstein to discuss New York Liberty's transformation. She talks about moving the team from Westchester to the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, investing in players, facilities and management, and how it all led to winning the WNBA championship. Wu Tsai joins David Rubenstein for the premiere episode of The David Rubenstein Show: Peer to Peer Conversations, Season 12. This interview was recorded June 4, in Brooklyn, New York.
