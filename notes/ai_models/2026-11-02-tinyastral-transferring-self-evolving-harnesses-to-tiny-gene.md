# TinyAstral: Transferring Self-Evolving Harnesses to Tiny Generalist Manipulation Agents

Robot agents built on vision-language models depend on both the planner and its harness: the tools, feedback, memory, and recovery logic around it. Can improvements to a large teacher's harness be carried into a compact agent with lower inference compute cost? We present TinyAstral, a two-stage approach.
