# US, Iran Exchange Strikes for First Time in About a Month

The US and Iran exchanged attacks for the first time in about a month with American forces striking an island in the Strait of Hormuz and the Islamic Republic launching attacks on the United Arab Emirates and Jordan. Tyler Kendall reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
