# Bellman Language Models: Solving the Recurring Decisions of LLM Agents

A general observation in agentic workflows, especially bounded ones, is that the decision space of the LLM, when we separate a decision from generation, starts approaching saturation: the agent meets the same states again and again, and after enough conversations it stops meeting new ones. LLMs generally don't treat these decisions as an optimization problem and instead re-derive them from their context/prompt on every turn.
