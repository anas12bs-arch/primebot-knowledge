# Oasis Urges Kakaku.com to Withdraw Support for EQT’s Buyout Bid

Kakaku.com Inc. shareholder Oasis Management Co. has asked the company to withdraw its support for EQT AB’s buyout bid on grounds that the offer is too low, escalating a bidding war for the Japanese price comparison site.
