# Philippine October Inflation Accelerates 7.8%, Close to Four-Year High

Philippine inflation accelerated to its fastest pace in almost four years in October as the prolonged Middle East conflict drove up the cost of fuel, electricity and transport in a nation heavily reliant on the region for its energy supplies.
