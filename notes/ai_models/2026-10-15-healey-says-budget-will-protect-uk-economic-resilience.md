# Healey Says Budget Will Protect UK Economic 'Resilience'

Chancellor of the Exchequer John Healey discusses the International Monetary Fund's comments that the UK is the “country that basically stands out” for plotting a path to bring down borrowing. Speaking to Bloomberg's Lisa Abramowicz in Bangkok, Healey also discusses the importance of banks to the British economy, declining to comment on potential tax measures in the government's Oct. 28 budget.
