# Qatar Has Boosted LNG Exports to Their Highest Since February

Qatar’s liquefied natural gas exports have risen to the highest since the start of the US-Iran war, overcoming disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz and boosting supply to Europe and Asia as the regions enter the peak winter demand season.
