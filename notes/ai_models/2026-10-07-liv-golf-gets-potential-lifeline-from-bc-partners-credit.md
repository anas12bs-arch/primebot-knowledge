# LIV Golf Gets Potential Lifeline From BC Partners Credit

Bloomberg's Randall Williams joins Dani Burger on "Bloomberg Deals." LIV Golf, a bankrupt golf tour backed by Saudi Arabia’s sovereign wealth fund, is receiving a financing commitment of as much as $300 million from the credit arm of BC Partners, potentially obtaining the funding needed to launch a 2027 season. (Source: Bloomberg)
