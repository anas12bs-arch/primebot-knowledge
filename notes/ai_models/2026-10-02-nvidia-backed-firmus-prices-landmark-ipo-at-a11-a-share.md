# Nvidia-Backed Firmus Prices Landmark IPO at A$11 a Share

Data center operator Firmus Grid Ltd. has priced its initial public offering at A$11 a share, seeking to raise at least $5 billion in its Australian initial public offering this month, according to terms of the deal seen by Bloomberg, ahead of one of Australia’s largest-ever IPOs.
