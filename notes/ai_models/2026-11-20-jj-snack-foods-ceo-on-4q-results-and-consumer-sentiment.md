# J&J Snack Foods CEO on 4Q Results and Consumer Sentiment

J&amp;J Snack Foods CEO Dan Fachner said the company’s turnaround is on track after a strong year, citing Project Apollo’s more than $25 million in durable savings. A second phase, Apollo 2, is expected to generate another $5 million by 2027 through distribution and manufacturing improvements. Fachner speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close."
