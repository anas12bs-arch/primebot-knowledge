# Kganyago: SARB Expects S. Africa CPI to Decline in 2027

South African Reserve Bank Governor Lesetja Kganyago says they will make their policy decisions meeting by meeting. He spoke to Bloomberg’s Avril Hong on the sidelines of the IMF-World Bank meeting in Bangkok ahead of the SARB’s final policy meeting of the year, next month. (Source: Bloomberg)
