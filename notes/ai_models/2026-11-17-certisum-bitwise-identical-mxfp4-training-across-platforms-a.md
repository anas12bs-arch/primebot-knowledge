# CertiSum: Bitwise-Identical MXFP4 Training Across Platforms and Parallelism Layouts

Training with MXFP4, a 4-bit microscaling format, is non-deterministic: identical inputs can produce different results across parallelism layouts and GPU vendors. These differences complicate debugging and training audits and can cause training and inference engines to disagree. We present CertiSum to make MXFP4 training bitwise invariant across runs, parallelism layouts, and GPU vendors.
