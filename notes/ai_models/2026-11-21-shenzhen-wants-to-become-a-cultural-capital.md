# Shenzhen Wants to Become a Cultural Capital

Bloomberg’s Tiffany Ap tells Bloomberg This Weekend how Shenzhen is spending billions on museums as the technology and manufacturing hub tries to establish itself as a cultural destination and attract talent and tourism. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Ap discusses whether a city known for Huawei, Tencent and BYD can build a lasting cultural identity while rapid wealth creation and a softer broader economy deepen economic divides.
