# Dubai Weighs Consolidating Lenders in Islamic Banking Push

Officials in Dubai have held preliminary talks on potentially combining Dubai Islamic Bank with Emirates Islamic Bank to create a larger Sharia-compliant lender better positioned to compete globally, people familiar with the matter said.
