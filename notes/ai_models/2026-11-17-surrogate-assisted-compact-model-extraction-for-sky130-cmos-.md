# Surrogate-Assisted Compact-Model Extraction for SKY130 CMOS at 77 K

Cryogenic circuit design requires compact transistor models that reproduce measured low-temperature behavior and run in standard circuit simulators. We fit BSIM4 parameters to measured I-V curves of SkyWater 130 nm (SKY130) transistors at 77 K using a neural surrogate of NGSpice. The surrogate maps compact-model parameters and device geometry to the complete set of output and transfer characteristics.
