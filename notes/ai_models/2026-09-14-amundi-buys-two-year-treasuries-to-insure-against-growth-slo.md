# Amundi Buys Two-Year Treasuries To Insure Against Growth Slowdown

Amundi SA, Europe’s largest asset manager, is looking for opportunities in global bond markets after the recent selloff, and is buying two-year US Treasuries to protect against the risk of economic growth slowing due to elevated oil prices.
