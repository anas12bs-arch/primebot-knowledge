# Short-End Treasuries Become Popular Bet on Fed Inflation Win

Investors in the US Treasury market are shifting their focus to owning shorter-dated government bonds, a bet that the Federal Reserve will eventually emerge victorious in its fight against inflation. 
Gennadiy Goldberg, Head of US Rates Strategy at TD Securities, discusses how the current rate hiking cycle might evolve and where the 10-year could finish the year. (Source: Bloomberg)
