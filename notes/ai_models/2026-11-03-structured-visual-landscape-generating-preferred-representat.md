# Structured Visual Landscape: Generating Preferred Representations in Biological and Artificial Neural Networks

Understanding how neurons respond to visual stimulus is an important question in both deep learning and neuroscience. It has significant implications for enhancing the interpretability of black-box artificial neural networks and for understanding the visual representation in the human brain. We propose a structured visual representations landscape and design an activation-based prior that allows us to effectively regularize the landscape with activations from brain regions or neural network unit
