# Exemplar Luxury Group CEO on the Company’s Second Life

Geoffroy van Raemdonck said Exemplar Luxury Group is financially stronger after Saks emerged from bankruptcy. He emphasized reaching that goal through customer loyalty, full-price sales and profitable growth that supports reinvestment and strengthens relationships with brands. Geoffroy van Raemdonck speaks with Romaine Bostick on “The Close.” (Source: Bloomberg)
