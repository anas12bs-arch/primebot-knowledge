# Malaysia Budget Sees Consumer Names Emerge Winners, Analysts Say

Consumer stocks are among the biggest beneficiaries of Malaysia’s spending plan for 2027, boosted by higher allocations to improve households’ purchasing power, according to analysts. Malaysian stocks rose as much as 0.4% on Monday, extending Friday’s gains.
