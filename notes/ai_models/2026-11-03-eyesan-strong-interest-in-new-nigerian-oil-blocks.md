# Eyesan: Strong Interest in New Nigerian Oil Blocks

Nigeria is offering 40 deepwater, shallow water and onshore blocks as it seeks to attract investment in its oil industry. Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission Chief Executive Oritsemeyiwa Eyesan spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar about plans to accelerate operations and development. (Source: Bloomberg)
