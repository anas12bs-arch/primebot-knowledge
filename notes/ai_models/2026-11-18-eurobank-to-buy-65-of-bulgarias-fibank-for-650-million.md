# Eurobank to Buy 65% of Bulgaria’s Fibank for €650 Million

Greece’s Eurobank agreed to buy a majority stake in First Investment Bank in Bulgaria, setting in motion a mandatory offer for the rest in a deal that would create the Balkan country’s biggest lender by assets.
