# Ex-Taula Trader Matteo Sardo to Join Millennium

Portfolio Manager Matteo Sardo, who specializes in rates trading reneged on an offer he accepted to go to Cohen’s Point72 Asset Management, according to people with knowledge of the matter. He is returning to his former employer Millennium Management, which is run by Izzy Englander, the people said, asking not to be identified discussing private information. Hema Parmar has more on "Bloomberg Open Interest." (Source: Bloomberg)
