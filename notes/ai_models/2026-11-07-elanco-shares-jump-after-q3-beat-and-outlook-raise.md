# Elanco Shares Jump After Q3 Beat and Outlook Raise

Elanco Animal Health shares rose nearly 8% after the company reported third-quarter earnings above expectations and raised its full-year outlook. Jeff Simmons, Elanco’s President and CEO, called it a high-quality quarter, citing 11% reported growth and broad strength across markets and product lines. He speaks with Romaine Bostick & Kailey Leinz on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
