# Rwanda Wins F1 Hosting Rights in Bet on Tourism

Rwanda has been chosen to host Formula 1’s return to Africa, beating competition from South Africa and Morocco. With the first Grand Prix planned for the early 2030s, the country is betting on a new circuit, airport and hotels to drive tourism, create jobs and boost its economy.
