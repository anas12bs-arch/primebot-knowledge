# S2PD: Serial-to-Parallel Diffusion for Physically and Logically Consistent Video Generation

Bidirectional video diffusion models denoise entire videos in parallel, yet when trained on effectively unlimited in-distribution data from procedural generators, continue to violate physical laws and simple symbolic rules. We introduce Serial-to-Parallel Diffusion (S2PD), which performs autoregressive diffusion at high noise before switching to parallel diffusion at low noise.
