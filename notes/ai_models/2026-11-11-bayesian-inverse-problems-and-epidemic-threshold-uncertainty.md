# Bayesian inverse problems and epidemic-threshold uncertainty for a heterogeneous SIS reaction-diffusion model

We study Bayesian inference of persistence from early observations of a heterogeneous susceptible--infected--susceptible (SIS) reaction--diffusion model with standard incidence. We show that the leading prevalence dynamics depend on the unknown coefficients only through the difference between the transmission and recovery rates and the infected diffusivity.
