# US Natural Gas Futures Fall as Offshore Production Recovers

US natural gas futures settled down, falling to the lowest in two weeks, as offshore production came back online following Hurricane Isaias. The quick recovery in supply weighed on prices as traders priced in more gas being injected into storage sites ahead of the winter heating season.
