# Walmart Posts Slowest US Sales Growth in Six Years

Walmart saw second-quarter sales at US stores open at least a year, excluding fuel, rise 2.6%, the retailer’s slowest rate of growth in more than six years. David Bellinger, senior equity analyst at Mizuho examines the results. (Source: Bloomberg)
