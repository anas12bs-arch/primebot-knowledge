# Scaling Online Optimal Transport Minibatches for Flow Matching

A central challenge in scaling flow models is maintaining stability in training and integration. In flow matching, this stability depends critically on how source samples are paired with data. Independent random pairing introduces substantial stochasticity, motivating minibatch optimal transport (OT) to construct online source-target couplings that reduce this randomness (Tong et al., 2024).
