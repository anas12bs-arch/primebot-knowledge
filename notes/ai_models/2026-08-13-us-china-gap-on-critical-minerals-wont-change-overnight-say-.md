# US, China Gap on Critical Minerals 'Won't Change Overnight,' Say REalloys CEO

Lipi Sternheim, REalloys CEO, said that his company is working closely with the US Army to get a critical mining operation underway on US military base and that the US government's interest in expanding critical minerals mining in the US has not slowed. Sternheim said that expanding US capacity to the level of its competitors like China remains years away.  (Source: Bloomberg)
