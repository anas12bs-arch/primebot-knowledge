# Musalem Says Clear Framework Helps Fed Policy Transmission

Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis President Alberto Musalem says providing clear communication about how a central bank will adjust interest rates based on incoming data can improve the transmission of monetary policy and lead to better economic outcomes during an event in London.
