# SymAnt: Grounding Long-Horizon Action Anticipation with Adaptable Symbolic Priors

As robots and intelligent systems increasingly interact with humans, they must anticipate long sequences of future actions from only brief observations. While purely neural models typically require extensive observations to predict future actions and remain difficult to interpret and adjust, symbolic priors offer a structured alternative.
