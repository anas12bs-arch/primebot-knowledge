# Asymptotic Margins and Feature Confinement in Conic Classification Heads under Softmax Cross-Entropy

Classification heads built on conic functions give each class a bounded region of the feature space, and such regions are used to reject samples of unseen classes. We ask whether these regions also keep the features bounded when the head is trained with softmax cross-entropy. For a head whose class scores combine a linear term with a Euclidean norm, we first show that the region of each class is bounded by a quadric of revolution, whose eccentricity is the reciprocal of one learned ratio.
