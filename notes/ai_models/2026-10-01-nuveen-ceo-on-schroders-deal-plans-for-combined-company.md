# Nuveen CEO on Schroders Deal, Plans for Combined Company

Nuveen has completed its acquisition of Schroders, forming a combined asset manager with $2.6 trillion in assets under management and operations in more than 40 markets. In an exclusive interview, Bill Huffman said the deal brings together two strong firms to create a global platform that can serve clients across a wider range of market conditions. He speaks to Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
