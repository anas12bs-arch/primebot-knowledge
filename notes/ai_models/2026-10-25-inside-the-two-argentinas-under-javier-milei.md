# Inside The Two Argentinas Under Javier Milei

President Javier Milei has brought inflation down and opened Argentina to more investment, helping fuel a boom in energy and other export industries. But manufacturing, retail and small businesses are struggling, leaving many Argentines feeling that the recovery is passing them by. One year before the election, Milei’s biggest test may be unifying an increasingly fragmented economy.
