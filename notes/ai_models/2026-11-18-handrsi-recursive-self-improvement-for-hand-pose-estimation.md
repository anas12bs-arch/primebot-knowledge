# HandRSI: Recursive Self-Improvement for Hand Pose Estimation

Egocentric 3D hand pose estimators still miss hands at the image border, misplace occluded hands and jitter. Such failures are usually fixed by hand-written inference refinements and by fine-tuning, two interdependent steps that are tuned separately. We present HandRSI, a recursive self-improvement framework for a hand pose system that consists of a video estimator and its executable inference harness.
