# Singer Vehicle Founder on Louis Vuitton Partnership

In a groundbreaking collaboration, luxury fashion house Louis Vuitton and high-end automotive restoration company Singer have teamed up to create custom Porsche cars. This marks a first for both brands, as Louis Vuitton has never engaged in co-branding before, and Singer has not previously partnered on such a project. Rob Dickinson & Mazen Fawaz speak with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
