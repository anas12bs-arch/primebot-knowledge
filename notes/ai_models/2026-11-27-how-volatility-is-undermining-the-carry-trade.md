The carry trade, in which investors borrow in currencies like the yen, euro, and dollar, is becoming harder to navigate after delivering strong returns earlier in the year.  Bloomberg's Garfield Reynolds discusses how market changes are impacting the strategy's appeal. (Source: Bloomberg)
