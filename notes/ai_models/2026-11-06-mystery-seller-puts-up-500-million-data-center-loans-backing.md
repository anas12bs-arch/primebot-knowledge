# Mystery Seller Puts Up $500 Million Data Center Loans Backing Stargate, CyrusOne

Dutch bank ING is putting up for sale a roughly $500 million portfolio of data center loans all at once, according to people familiar with the matter. The rare auction comes just days after a Democratic sweep of the US midterm elections raised doubts about the future of many of the projects.
