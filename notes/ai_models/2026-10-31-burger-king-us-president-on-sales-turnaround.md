# Burger King US President on Sales Turnaround

Burger King US and Canada President Tom Curtis discussed the chain’s comparable-sales growth, cited in the conversation as 8.4%, against an estimate of 6.2%. Curtis said growth is being driven largely by customers who had stopped visiting and are now returning, drawn by a renewed connection to the brand. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
