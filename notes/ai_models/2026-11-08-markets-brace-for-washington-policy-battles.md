# Markets Brace for Washington Policy Battles

On Bloomberg This Weekend, SMBC Nikko Securities Chief Economist Joe Lavorgna says affordability helped drive the midterm results but argues the underlying US economy remains healthy and major policy changes may be limited by divided government. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Lavorgna discusses the outlook for AI investment, inflation and markets as Washington prepares for two years of potential policy.
