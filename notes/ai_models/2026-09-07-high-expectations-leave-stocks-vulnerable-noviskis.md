# High Expectations Leave Stocks Vulnerable: Noviskis

Jessica Noviskis, OCIO portfolio strategist at Marquette Associates, says strong earnings continue to support US stocks, but elevated expectations and higher yields leave the market vulnerable if results disappoint. “When we do see those hiccups, we can see a bigger correction,” she says on “Bloomberg Markets.” (Source: Bloomberg)
