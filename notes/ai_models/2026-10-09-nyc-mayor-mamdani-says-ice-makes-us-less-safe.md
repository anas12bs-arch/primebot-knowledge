# NYC Mayor Mamdani Says ICE 'Makes Us Less Safe'

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani repeats his call for the abolishment of the Immigration and Customs Enforcement enforcement agency after an officer shot and wounded a man in front of a child on Thursday in the Bronx. Mamdani says ICE makes the city "less safe." ICE says the shooting happened as part of a targeted operation. The child was unharmed, the victim is in stable condition at a local hosptial.
