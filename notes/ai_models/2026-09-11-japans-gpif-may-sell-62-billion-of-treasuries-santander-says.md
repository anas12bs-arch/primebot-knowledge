# Japan’s GPIF May Sell $62 Billion of Treasuries, Santander Says

Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund could sell as much as $62 billion of US Treasuries without a formal revamp of its asset-allocation policy, according to analysts at Banco Santander SA.
