# 'Surveillance Pricing' Comes Under Scrutiny

Bloomberg's Max Chafkin, David Gura, and Jessica Karl join Scarlet Fu and Tom Keene on "Bloomberg Money." They discuss the ongoing fight over 'surveillance pricing,' the use of consumer data to set prices by retailers, grocers and others. Walmart strenuously denies engaging in this tactic.
