# Altria, Japan Tobacco Explore Takeover of Imperial Brands

Altria Group Inc. and Japan Tobacco Inc. are exploring a potential takeover of British cigarette maker Imperial Brands Plc, accelerating consolidation in the declining cigarette market in what would be one of the biggest UK acquisitions in a decade.
