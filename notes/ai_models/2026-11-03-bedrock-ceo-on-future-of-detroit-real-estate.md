# Bedrock CEO on Future of Detroit Real Estate

Bedrock CEO Jared Fleisher discussed Detroit’s redevelopment, pointing to Dan Gilbert’s investment in restoring and filling 140 buildings across Detroit and Cleveland, totaling more than 21 million square feet. Fleisher highlighted the Hudson’s site, where a new tower is rising on the former department store location and is expected to be completed in 2027. He speaks to Romaine Bostick on "The Close."
