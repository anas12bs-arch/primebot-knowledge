Stephanie Roth, chief economist at Wolfe Research, and Guneet Dhingra, head of US rates strategy at BNP Paribas join Emily Graffeo on "Bloomberg Real Yield." US Treasuries rebounded from the global bond selloff that raced through European markets on Thursday. Earlier in the day, US 10-year yield surged to the highest level since 2002.
