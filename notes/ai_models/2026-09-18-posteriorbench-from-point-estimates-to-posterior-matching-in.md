# PosteriorBench: From Point Estimates to Posterior Matching in Evaluating Generative Inverse Solvers

Generative models are increasingly used to solve scientific inverse problems, but existing evaluations still focus primarily on whether a method can produce a single plausible reconstruction. This is insufficient for ill-posed problems, where multiple solutions may be consistent with the same sparse or noisy observations.
