# BLIP-Live: Balancing Timeliness & Firing Precision in Proactive Streaming Video Agents

Streaming video understanding is becoming increasingly important for real-world interactive systems. Such systems must be proactive for natural and useful human interaction: they should respond as soon as sufficient evidence becomes available while avoiding premature, redundant, or incorrect responses.
