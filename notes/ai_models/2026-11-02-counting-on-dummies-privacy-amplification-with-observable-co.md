# Counting on Dummies: Privacy Amplification with Observable Counts and Truncated Batches

In many practical deployments of Differentially Private Stochastic Gradient Descent (DP-SGD), the server processes data in batches. Randomly sampling these batches allows for better privacy bounds via privacy amplification results. Most sampling schemes with provable privacy amplification, including Poisson sampling and random allocation, result in variable batch sizes.
