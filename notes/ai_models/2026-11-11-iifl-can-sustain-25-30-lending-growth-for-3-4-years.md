# IIFL: Can Sustain 25-30% Lending Growth For 3-4 Years

IIFL Finance Founder & MD Nirmal Jain believes his company can sustain 25 percent to 30 percent lending growth for three to four years. IIFL Finance offers gold, home and business loans, as well as financing against property and to small and medium-sized enterprises. He speaks with Haslinda Amin on "Insight with Haslinda Amin."
