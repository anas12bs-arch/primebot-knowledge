# Beyond the Keyword: A Human-Annotated Benchmark for Arabic Anxiety-Related Discourse

Keyword retrieval returns expressions of personal anxiety together with many other uses of anxiety-related language. We present a benchmark of 1,000 Arabic Twitter/X posts retrieved with the keyword qalaq ("anxiety/worry") and annotated for six discourse functions. Two native Arabic-speaking annotators labeled every post independently, reaching 51.1% raw agreement (Cohen's $κ= 0.378$).
