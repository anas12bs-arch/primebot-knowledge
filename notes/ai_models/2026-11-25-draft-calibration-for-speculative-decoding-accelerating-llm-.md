# Draft Calibration for Speculative Decoding: Accelerating LLM Serving by Matching Draft and Target Distributions

Speculative decoding accelerates large language model (LLM) inference by employing a lightweight draft model to propose draft tokens and allowing the target model to verify the new tokens in parallel to improve inference-time compute utilization. The speedup relies on the acceptance probability of draft tokens in target verification, which is directly related to the gap $\|p-q\|_1$ between the target distribution $p$ and the draft distribution $q$.
