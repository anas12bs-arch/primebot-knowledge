# Few Places to Hide as Bond Selloffs Continue

AMP economist My Bui projects US 10-year Treasury yields could reach 5% as structural inflation drivers remain intact. She notes that sustained market pressures may keep Federal Reserve interest rates elevated as equities come under pressure, creating a situation where investors lack safe havens amidst market turmoil.
