# UniVOE: A Removal-Driven Framework for Interaction-Aware Universal Video Object Editing

Video object removal and injection are reciprocal editing operations between object-present and object-absent video states, yet they differ substantially in training difficulty. Removal can be effectively bootstrapped from video inpainting and further adapted to erase object-related interaction effects.
