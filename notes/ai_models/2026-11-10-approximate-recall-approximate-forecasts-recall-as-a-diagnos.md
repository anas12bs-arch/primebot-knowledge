# Approximate Recall, Approximate Forecasts: Recall as a Diagnostic for LLM Forecasting Errors

Large Language Models (LLMs) exhibit approximate recall: they retain coarse associations about past events while losing fidelity on specific details. Past research on LLMs has quantified different models' forecasting ability in real-world events using prediction markets, but has not studied how losing said fidelity impacts their forecasting abilities.
