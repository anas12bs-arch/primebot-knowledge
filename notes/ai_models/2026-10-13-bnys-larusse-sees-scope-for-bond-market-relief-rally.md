# BNY's LaRusse Sees Scope for Bond Market Relief Rally

April LaRusse, co-head of global client portfolio management at BNY Investments, says it is too early to confidently call a peak in Treasury yields. Speaking on "The Asia Trade," LaRusse says current market pricing for roughly three rate hikes looks excessive, leaving room for a relief rally if the Middle East conflict eases or labor market data weakens.
