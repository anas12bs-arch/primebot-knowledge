# UAV-Based Computer Vision for Cattle Detection and Feed Bunk Spatial Analysis

Beef cattle production is central to Brazilian agribusiness and faces growing demands for efficiency, sustainability, and animal welfare. Precision Livestock Farming (PLF) offers tools to address these challenges, particularly through monitoring feeding-related spatial patterns in confined systems. Feeding management plays a decisive role in animal performance, yet its evaluation still relies largely on subjective, labor-intensive visual inspections that are hard to scale and prone to bias.
