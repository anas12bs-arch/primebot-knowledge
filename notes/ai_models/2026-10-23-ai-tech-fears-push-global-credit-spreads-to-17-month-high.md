# AI Tech Fears Push Global Credit Spreads to 17-Month High

Metrics across global bond markets are signaling their biggest risk-off sentiment in more than a year, as a selloff in AI stocks and growing geopolitical tensions caused investors to seek out safe-haven assets.
Katy Kaminski, Chief Research Strategist at AlphaSimplex, discusses the equities AI trade test and why fixed income is still under pressure. (Source: Bloomberg)
