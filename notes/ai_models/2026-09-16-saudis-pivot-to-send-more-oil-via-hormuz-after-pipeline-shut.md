# Saudis Pivot to Send More Oil Via Hormuz After Pipeline Shut

Saudi Arabia is ramping up prompt sales of crude from outside the Strait of Hormuz following the shutdown of the kingdom’s East-West pipeline, which halted cross-country flows to its Red Sea coast.
