# Deere Sees Headwinds Ahead for Farmers in 2027

Deere & Co. says it expects higher sales of its biggest machines in the 12 months through October, but also sees net income of $4.5 billion to $5.25 billion, which trails the $6.1 billion average of analysts’ estimates compiled by Bloomberg. Chris Ciolino has more on "Bloomberg Open Interest." (Source: Bloomberg)
