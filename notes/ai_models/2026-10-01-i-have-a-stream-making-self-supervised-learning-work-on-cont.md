# I Have a Stream: Making Self-Supervised Learning Work on Continuous Video

Self-supervised learning draws inspiration from infant visual development, yet standard training pipelines bear little resemblance to it: images are independently sampled and globally shuffled across epochs. We study self-supervised learning from continuous video streams, where frames are consumed in temporal order using strict sliding-window batches, without global reshuffling or multi-epoch replay.
