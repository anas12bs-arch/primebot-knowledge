# Seeking in Space: Benchmarking Purposeful Active Exploration of Vision-Language Models for Spatial Reasoning

Humans explore unfamiliar spaces with purpose, deciding where to look until they have gathered the evidence a spatial question requires. However, can vision-language models (VLMs), which reason increasingly well over curated images and videos, also seek in space when the evidence is not handed to them?
