# Fed's Collins Says Additional Policy Tightening Is Appropriate

Federal Reserve Bank of Boston President Susan Collins says recent data show inflation is too high and broadening. Speaking with Michael McKee on "Bloomberg Open Interest," Collins adds that because of the increasing inflation risks, more rate tightening is needed. (Source: Bloomberg)
