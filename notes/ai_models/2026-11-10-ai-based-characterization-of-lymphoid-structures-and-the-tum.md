# AI-based characterization of lymphoid structures and the tumor microenvironment reveals lower mature tertiary lymphoid structures, lymph-node follicles, and plasma cells after neoadjuvant therapy in lung squamous cell carcinoma

Mature, germinal centre-bearing tertiary lymphoid structures (TLS) and mature lymph node follicles (secondary lymphoid structures, SLS) sustain the plasma cell response that shapes outcome and immunotherapy benefit in lung cancer. How this loco-regional axis differs between neoadjuvant-treated and therapy-naive tumors is poorly defined.
