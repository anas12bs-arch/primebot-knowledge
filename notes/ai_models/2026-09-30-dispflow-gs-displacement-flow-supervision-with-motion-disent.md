Accurate dynamic scene reconstruction is important for robotic perception, where temporally consistent representations of dynamic environments are essential. Deformable 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) models dynamic scenes through deformation fields, and recent methods incorporate motion supervision by aligning rendered Gaussian flow with optical flow.
