# Daly Says More Fed Rates Hikes Likely Needed

Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco President Mary Daly says further interest-rate increases will likely be needed to bring down inflation, adding that businesses are increasingly contemplating raising prices. She speaks at the Stanford Institute for Economic Policy Research. (Source: Bloomberg)
