# Chinese Chipmaker Longsys Seeks $800 Million in HK Listing

Shenzhen Longsys Electronics Co. is seeking to raise as much as HK$6.28 billion ($801 million) in a Hong Kong listing, extending the wave of Chinese companies along the artificial intelligence supply chain coming to the market after its onshore shares have gained about 50% this year.
