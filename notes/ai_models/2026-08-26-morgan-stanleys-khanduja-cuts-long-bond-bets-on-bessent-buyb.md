# Morgan Stanley’s Khanduja Cuts Long-Bond Bets on Bessent Buyback Move

Morgan Stanley Investment Management’s Vishal Khanduja, a top-performing fixed-income investor, is scaling back bets against long-term US bonds, saying Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is prepared to do “whatever it takes” to keep yields from rising.
