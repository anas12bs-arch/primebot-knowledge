# Lincoln Center Kicks Off Fall Season

The Lincoln Center in NYC has welcomed 600,000 new audience members in the years since the COVID-19 pandemic, offering free of low-cost performances to attract new audiences. Chief Artistic Officer Shanta Thake shares what is on schedule for the Lincoln Center's fall programming, and why she thinks the institution has thrived in the live performance and arts space for so many decades.
