# Getty Images Files for Chapter 11 at a ~$910M Run-Rate: 27% EBITDA Margins, 25% Fewer Subscribers, and $0 for Public Shareholders

Getty Images filed for Chapter 11 today in Delaware. Affiliates of the Getty family and Koch Equity Development will put in up to $533M in new equity and will own the whole company once the process ends, which Getty expects by the end of the year.
