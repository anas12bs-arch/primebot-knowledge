# Why India’s Stock Markets Hold a Session on Diwali: Explainer

India’s stock markets will open for a special shortened session on Sunday as the country celebrates Diwali, its biggest festival, keeping alive a tradition that has survived the shift from handwritten ledgers and bustling trading floors to smartphones and algorithms.
