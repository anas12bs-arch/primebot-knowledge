# ITU: Focus On Ensuring Syria Becomes a Digital Hub

The UN’s digital technology agency is meeting in Doha, with AI, connectivity and space-based technologies high on the agenda. According to the International Telecommunication Union, more than two billion people remain offline as governments grapple with the rapid development of AI and other digital technologies. Doreen Bogdan-Martin, Secretary-General of the ITU spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar on Horizons Middle East & Africa. (Source: Bloomberg)
