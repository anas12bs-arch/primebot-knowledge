# Own, Don't Earn: 'Everywhere Millionaire' Author

Owen Zidar, author of 'The Everywhere Millionaire', joins Scarlet Fu and Tom Keene discuss their must-read book on this week's edition of "Bloomberg Money." They discuss Zidar's book, which lays out how wealth often comes from businesses outside of Wall Street. They also discuss 'Think and Grow Rich: The Landmark Bestseller Now Revised and Updated for the 21st Century' by Napoleon Hill. (Source: Bloomberg)
