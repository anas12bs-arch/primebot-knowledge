# Dangote Refinery Set to Start Trading in Lagos on Dec. 1

Dangote Petroleum Refinery and Petrochemicals FZE will rank as the biggest company with a primary listing in Africa once it makes its trading debut in Lagos. The operation owned by tycoon Aliko Dangote is valued at the equivalent of about $48 billion through its initial public offering. Bloomberg's Jennifer Zabasajja reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
