# BofA’s Hartnett Sees Pressure on Risk Assets If Bond Plan Fails

A failure in the US Treasury’s plan to tame long-term bond yields would pressure the dollar and spur short bets against riskier assets in the lead-up to November midterm elections, according to Bank of America Corp. strategist Michael Hartnett.
