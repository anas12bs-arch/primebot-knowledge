# Dangote Refinery Set to Become Africa’s Biggest Listed Company

Dangote Petroleum Refinery and Petrochemicals FZE will rank as the biggest company with a primary listing in Africa once it makes its trading debut in Lagos, far exceeding the size of the next-largest business on the continent.
