# NIB CEO Says Defense Project Pipeline Has Increased Rapidly

Nordic Investment Bank Chief Executive Officer André Küüsvek speaks on Bloomberg Television as the Helsinki-based multilateral lender owned by the five Nordic and three Baltic countries formally added resilience, security and defense into its strategy. "We are in discussions with around 30 other potential borrowers and counterparts with a gross pipeline of about €1.2 billion worth of projects today on the table," Küüsvek says. (Source: Bloomberg)
