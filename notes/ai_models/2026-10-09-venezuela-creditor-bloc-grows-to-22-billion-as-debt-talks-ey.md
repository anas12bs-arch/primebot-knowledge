# Venezuela Creditor Bloc Grows to $22 Billion as Debt Talks Eyed

Venezuela’s creditor group has been expanding and now accounts for nearly 40% of all the debt outstanding, putting it in a stronger position as fresh optimism builds that the nation’s long-delayed restructuring is high on the agenda in Washington and Caracas.
