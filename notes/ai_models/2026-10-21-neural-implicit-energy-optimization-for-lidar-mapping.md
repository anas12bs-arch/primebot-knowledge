# Neural Implicit Energy Optimization for LiDAR Mapping

Neural implicit representations provide memory-efficient signed distance field (SDF) parameterizations for large-scale LiDAR scenes, but the geometry recovered from sparse and often noisy inputs carries jitter and artifacts. Classical geometric optimization techniques resolve such artifacts with surface energy functionals, whose minimizers correspond to smooth surfaces.
