# Partners Group CEO Rejects Redemption Concerns

Partners Group CEO David Layton joined Bloomberg Open Interest to push back on concerns that his move to CIO signals trouble at the private-markets giant. He explains why the leadership shake-up isn’t tied to redemption pressure, says the firm is gaining fundraising share despite an industry slowdown, and reveals why elevated evergreen-fund redemptions could continue for another 12 to 18 months.
