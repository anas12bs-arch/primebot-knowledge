# Dave Lee: Anthropic Brings ‘Anti-Sell’ Strategy to Its IPO

Dave Lee, Bloomberg Opinion US Technology Columnist, joins Bloomberg Businessweek Daily to discuss his piece about Anthropic's IPO announcement. Lee writes about the firm's S-1 containing warnings about the company and prospects for humanity as AI technology continues to advance. Lee adds that the filing is described as “a pitch deliberately designed to be unappealing to those who are a poor fit … while appealing to the type of person we want to hire.” Lee speaks with Carol Massar and Tim Stenov
