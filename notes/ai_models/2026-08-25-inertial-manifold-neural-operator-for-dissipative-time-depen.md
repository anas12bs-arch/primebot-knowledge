# Inertial Manifold Neural Operator for Dissipative Time-Dependent Partial Differential Equations

In this paper, we introduce the Inertial Manifold Neural Operator (IMNO) for solving dissipative time-dependent partial differential equations (PDEs). The long-time dynamics of such systems often exhibit an effective low-dimensional structure due to dissipation.
