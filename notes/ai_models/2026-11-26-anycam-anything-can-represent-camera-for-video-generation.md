# AnyCam: Anything Can Represent Camera for Video Generation

Camera-controlled video generation re-renders a video along a target camera trajectory, yet moving the camera around an object, the most natural way to change the viewpoint of an object-centric scene, remains difficult. Existing methods condition either on camera parameters, which are scale-ambiguous in monocular video, or on explicit geometry such as warped point clouds or depth, which breaks down under large viewpoint changes.
