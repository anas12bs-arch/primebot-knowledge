# Covenant Plans Mass Production of Cruise Missiles in Germany

Defense startup Covenant says their new heavy-payload, long-range missile called Anthem will become key to Europe’s deterrence. The company plans to mass produce the missile at a new factory in eastern Germany, as NATO allies look for ways to refill depleted stocks of precision-strike weapons.
Covenant President Abby Denburg spoke with Bloomberg’s Stephen Carroll from Berlin, on October 7, 2026. (Source: Bloomberg)
