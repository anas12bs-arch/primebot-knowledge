# Multimodal Masked Diffusion Autoencoder for Modeling Impaired Driving

Human cognitive state (e.g., distraction, stress), gaze, and behavior interact with the driving environment in subtle ways that AI could use to assist drivers, but ground-truth labels for human cognitive state levels are costly to obtain at scale. We propose a multimodal masked-diffusion autoencoder, ID-MAE, that adds a self-supervised objective to impairment prediction: alongside predicting the impairment label as a masked token, it reconstructs masked gaze and trajectory.
