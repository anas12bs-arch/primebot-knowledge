# Investing in Financial Security: Masters in Business with Salim Ramji

Barry speaks with Vanguard CEO Salim Ramji. They discuss his early career journey in law before transitioning to BlackRock as Head of Corporate Strategy and later taking over as the first "outsider" CEO of Vanguard. They also discuss Vanguard's recent acquisition of Altruist and what it means for the future of financial advice. (Source: Bloomberg)
