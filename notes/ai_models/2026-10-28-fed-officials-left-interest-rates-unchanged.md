# Fed Officials Left Interest Rates Unchanged

Jon Ferro, Lisa Abramowicz and Tom Keene host a special edition of "Bloomberg Surveillance" as the Federal Open Market Committee voted 10-2 to hold its benchmark federal funds rate in a range of 3.75% to 4% and leave interest rates unchanged. (Source: Bloomberg)
