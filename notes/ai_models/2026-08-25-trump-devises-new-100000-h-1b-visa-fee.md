# Trump Devises New $100,000 H-1B Visa Fee

The Trump administration is proposing a regulatory process that would charge companies roughly $103,000 for each candidate they sponsor for a visa under the H-1B program for foreign-born professionals. Bloomberg's Alicia Caldwell breaks down the news. (Source: Bloomberg)
