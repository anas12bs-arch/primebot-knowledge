# Brazil Assets Surge as Investor Favorite Bolsonaro Leads in Race

Brazilian assets surged on expectations that Flávio Bolsonaro will address the country’s mounting fiscal problems and pave the way for interest-rate cuts after he became the surprise favorite to win the presidency in an Oct. 25 runoff against Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.
