# A Mesh for Every Moment

We present MEMO (a Mesh for Every MOment), a method that reconstructs the geometry of complete dynamic scenes, captured by multiple synchronized cameras, and extracts a mesh at every frame. Existing dynamic Gaussian splatting methods either model appearance alone or target isolated objects segmented by foreground masks, and all of them recover meshes by TSDF fusion or Poisson reconstruction, whose fixed grids limit the detail they can capture.
