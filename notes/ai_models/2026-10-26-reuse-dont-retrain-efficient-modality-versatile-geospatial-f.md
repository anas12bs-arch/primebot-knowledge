# Reuse, Don't Retrain: Efficient, Modality-Versatile Geospatial Foundation Models through Feature Distillation

Geospatial foundation models (GeoFMs) are costly to pretrain, yet model families often repeat this process across backbone scales and sensing modalities. We investigate feature distillation as a more efficient way to reuse existing representations. First, we distill frozen CROMA and TerraMind teachers into smaller Vision Transformers through patch-level feature matching.
