# Saks, Neiman Marcus Parent Company CEO Says Growth Plan Is on Track

Exemplar Luxury Group CEO Geoffroy van Raemdonck says the Saks Fifth Avenue and Neiman Marcus parent company is profitable and aims to grow revenue from $7 billion to $9 billion. He talks about the demand for luxury goods, strengthening relationships with vendors and how the company is preparing for the holiday shopping season. He speaks with Bloomberg’s Romaine Bostick.
