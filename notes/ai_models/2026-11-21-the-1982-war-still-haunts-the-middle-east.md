# The 1982 War Still Haunts the Middle East

Journalist and author Kim Ghattas tells Bloomberg This Weekend how the violence that engulfed Lebanon in 1982 helped set the US, Israel, Iran and the wider Middle East on a trajectory that continues to shape conflicts in the region today. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini about her new book, The Best Kind of American, Ghattas examines Hezbollah’s enduring influence, Lebanon’s struggle to assert state authority and the risks of trying to remake the region through war.
