# A Chosen Future Can Still Be Rewritten: Causal Writability in Video Models

When a video model generates physically incorrect motion, did it fail to learn the correct motion, or did it learn it but fail to use it? We show the latter: the correct motion remains available inside the model and can still be made to control the generated video. We train on videos where red masses oscillate slowly and blue masses oscillate quickly, then test a red mass with fast observed motion.
