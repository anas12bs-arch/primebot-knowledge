# El Niño Causes Panama Canal to Restrict Traffic

Ilya Espino de Marotta is the new head of the Panama Canal Authority and she is already tackling some tough challenges. She says a strong El Niño has led to restrictions on ship transits and talks about how this weather pattern is impacting the business. She speaks to Bloomberg's Romaine Bostick. (Source: Bloomberg)
