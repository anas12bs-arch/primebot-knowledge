# Bond Yields About Fundamentals, Not Bessent, Says HSBC’s Kettner

Max Kettner, chief multi-asset strategist at HSBC Bank, points to US growth and exceptionalism as he makes the case for higher US yields. Kettner says, “at the end of the day, this is fundamentals, this is not about what Scott Bessent wants.” He speaks on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
