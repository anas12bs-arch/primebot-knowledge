# Li Ka-shing Keeps $23 Billion Ask for Ports Despite Panama Loss

Li Ka-shing’s CK Hutchison Holdings Ltd. expects to sell what remains of its global ports portfolio for its original $22.8 billion value, even after losing two Panama terminals that had been included, according to people familiar with the matter.
