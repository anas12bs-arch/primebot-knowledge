# Learning Positive Linear Combinations of ReLUs in Fully Polynomial Time

We study the fundamental problem of PAC learning positive linear combinations of $k$ arbitrary ReLU activations on $\mathbb{R}^d$ with respect to the Gaussian distribution, in $L_2$ loss. We give the first fully-polynomial time algorithm for this learning task. Specifically, our algorithm draws $\mathrm{poly}(d, k, 1/ε)$ samples, runs in $\mathrm{poly}(d, k, 1/ε)$ time, and outputs a hypothesis that is $ε$-close to the target concept with high probability.
