# MCB CEO: Growth Fueled by Current Oil Market Demand

Mauritius Commercial Bank reported an increase in net interest income for the full year that’s as geopolitical tensions are driving up oil & gas prices. Jennifer Zabasajja, Bloomberg's Chief Africa Correspondent spoke to Thierry Hebraud, CEO of Mauritius Commercial Bank on the earnings and the formation of the new Africa Credit Ratings agency.
