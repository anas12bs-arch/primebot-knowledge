# Gotham Greens CEO: Not Good To Have an Outbreak Like This

Craig Stevenson, CEO of Gotham Greens, addressed the ongoing concerns over food safety amid recent recalls linked to salmonella in jalapenos and the widespread Cyclospora outbreak affecting thousands in the U.S. He noted a temporary decline in consumer demand for greens as news of outbreaks circulates, but emphasized that consumer interest in salads remains strong. He speaks with David Gura & Isabelle Lee on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
