# A Positional Realizability Theory of Length Generalization in Single-Head Attention

Why does length generalization (LG) depend so sharply on the positional encoding (PE)? Relative schemes extrapolate fixed offsets, absolute schemes fix positions, and neither handles growing offsets. We give a realizability theory for a deliberately controlled abstraction, a single positional-only attention head, that derives this well-documented empirical pattern and says precisely where it holds and breaks.
