# Shipping Oil Through Hormuz Costs $20 Million, Total CEO Says

The cost of shipping oil cargoes on a supertanker through the Strait of Hormuz is about $20 million, the boss of TotalEnergies SE said, underscoring the wide margins to be made by traders and shipowners.
