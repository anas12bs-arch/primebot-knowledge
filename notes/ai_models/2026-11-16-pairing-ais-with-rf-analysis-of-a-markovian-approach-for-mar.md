# Pairing AIS with RF: Analysis of a Markovian Approach for Maritime Data Fusion

Monitoring maritime activities is important for safety and security, particularly for detecting illegal activities, but remains challenging due to the size and complexity of the maritime domain. The Automatic Identification System (AIS) is commonly used for this purpose, despite its known vulnerabilities.
