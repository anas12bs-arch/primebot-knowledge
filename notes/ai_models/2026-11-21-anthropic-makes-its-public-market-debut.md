# Anthropic Makes Its Public Market Debut

Bloomberg’s Ed Ludlow tells Bloomberg This Weekend that Anthropic’s landmark IPO tested investor appetite for frontier AI, giving the company fresh capital for its enormous computing needs as OpenAI considers its own path to the public markets. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Ludlow also examines the turmoil at Nvidia following Jensen Huang’s departure and the uncertainty surrounding the chipmaker’s search for new leadership.
