# How Short Is Too Short? Length-Conditional Calibration and Abstention for Detecting AI-Generated Text

Detectors of AI-generated text are usually tuned and tested on passages of several hundred tokens, yet many real cases are short. We study score-based detection as a function of passage length. We give a closed form for how the false-positive rate of a threshold set on long text changes as length falls, and a formula for the number of tokens a detector needs to reach a target true-positive rate.
