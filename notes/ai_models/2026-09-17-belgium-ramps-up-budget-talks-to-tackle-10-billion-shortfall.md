# Belgium Ramps Up Budget Talks to Tackle €10 Billion Shortfall

The Belgian government is intensifying budget talks in a bid to find around €10 billion ($11.5 billion) in savings by 2029, the latest effort by the country to reassure investors ahead of a deadline next month.
