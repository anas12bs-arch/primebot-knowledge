# Keeneland CEO on Annual Yearling Sale

Shannon Arvin says Keeneland’s yearling sale is running ahead of last year’s pace and is on track to top $550 million, making it the largest horse sale in the world. She said the auction is already up 3% to 4% this year after increasing by 24% last year, reflecting strong demand and continued interest in horse racing. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
