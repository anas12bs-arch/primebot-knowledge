# David Booth on Investing, Dimensional and Staying Calm | The David Rubenstein Show

David Booth started Dimensional Fund Advisors out of a Brooklyn brownstone in 1981 with an idea that challenged conventional investing: investors should have exposure to smaller companies as part of a diversified portfolio. Today, Dimensional manages more than $1 trillion. Booth sits down with David Rubenstein to discuss his new book, "Stay Calm," which applies his investment philosophy of staying calm during periods of market volatility, to navigating the uncertainties of life.
