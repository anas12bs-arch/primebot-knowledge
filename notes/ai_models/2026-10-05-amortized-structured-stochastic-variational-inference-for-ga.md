# Amortized Structured Stochastic Variational Inference for Gaussian Process Latent Variable Models

Many machine learning methods aim to approximate the lower-dimensional manifold on which the data lives. A desirable feature of such methods is that they should capture the epistemic uncertainty of this learned manifold. One model that achieves this is the Gaussian Process Latent Variable Model, in which a Gaussian Process (GP) mapping from the latent space provides an estimate of the uncertainty of the manifold.
