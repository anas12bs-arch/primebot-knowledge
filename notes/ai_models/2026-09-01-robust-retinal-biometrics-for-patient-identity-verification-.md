# Robust retinal biometrics for patient identity verification and retrieval across age and imaging devices

Patient identity errors can compromise longitudinal medical records, research databases, and downstream clinical decisions. We present a retinal biometric system for verifying claimed identities and retrieving the correct identity from color fundus images.
