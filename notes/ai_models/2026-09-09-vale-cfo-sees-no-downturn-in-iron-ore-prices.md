# Vale CFO Sees No Downturn in Iron Ore Prices

Vale SA Chief Financial Officer Marcelo Bacci says iron ore is a resilient commodity and says demand is stable. He also says the mining giant may tap the Chinese bond market in the near future. He speaks on "Bloomberg Open Interest."
