# Porky Ends Peru’s Election Year Nearly Empty-Handed

Rafael López Aliaga, Lima’s brash ex-mayor, ran for office three times this year, trying to become Peru’s president, a senator and finally, the capital’s mayor once again. The right-wing businessman came close to all three and challenged electoral authorities along the way, but ended up with only a seat on the city council.
