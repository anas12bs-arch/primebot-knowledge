# Capital Economics' MacAdam on ECB, Kevin Warsh, Bessent

Simon MacAdam, deputy chief global economist at Capital Economics, joins Bloomberg's Lizzy Burden on "The Pulse" to discuss his reaction to ECB Governing Council member Isabel Schnabel emphasizing the need for further interest rate increases due to upside inflation risks. He also talks about what he'd like to hear from US Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh at Jackson Hole. (Source: Bloomberg)
