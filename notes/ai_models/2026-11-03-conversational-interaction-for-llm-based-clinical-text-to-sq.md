# Conversational Interaction for LLM-Based Clinical Text-to-SQL: A User Study with Healthcare Professionals

Conversational text-to-SQL interfaces could give healthcare professionals natural-language access to electronic health record (EHR) data, but evidence for their real-world usability remains limited: prior evaluations have relied largely on curated benchmarks with fully specified questions or on simulated users.
