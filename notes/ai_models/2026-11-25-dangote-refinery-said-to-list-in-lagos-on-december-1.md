# Dangote Refinery Said to List in Lagos on December 1

Bloomberg understands billionaire Aliko Dangote’s oil refinery will start trading in Lagos as soon as next week. Dangote Petroleum Refinery & Petrochemicals debut is being set for December 1, in what will be Africa’s biggest IPO. Chief Africa correspondent, Jennifer Zabasajja reports from Johannesburg.
