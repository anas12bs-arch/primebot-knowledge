# Leakage- and Label-Aware Benchmarking of Class-Imbalance Strategies for Multimodal Chest X-Ray and EHR Classification

Benchmarks of class-imbalance strategies for multimodal chest X-ray (CXR) and electronic health record (EHR) prediction are often evaluated on record-level splits of cohorts in which one patient contributes several radiographs. We audit such a benchmark on 29,083 MIMIC-CXR radiographs of 10,479 patients with eight diagnosis targets.
