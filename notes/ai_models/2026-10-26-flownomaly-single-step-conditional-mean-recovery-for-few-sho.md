# Flownomaly: Single-Step Conditional-Mean Recovery for Few-Shot Anomaly Detection

Few-shot industrial anomaly detection requires modeling category-specific normality from only a handful of defect-free images. Common approaches tie inference to stored exemplars, impose rigid linear structure, or risk reproducing anomalous content. Flownomaly instead casts the problem as normal-counterpart recovery in frozen DINOv2 feature space.
