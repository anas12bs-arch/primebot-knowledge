# Hertz Lenders Team Up With Advisers as Possible Debt Fight Looms

Creditors to Hertz Global Holdings Inc. are organizing across at least two groups, bracing for potential negotiations with the car rental company over its billions of dollars of debt that comes due over the next few years, according to people familiar with the matter.
