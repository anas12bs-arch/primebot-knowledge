# Local AI Agents in Power Electronics Simulation: Deployment, Measurements and Costs

Agentic artificial intelligence (AI) is emerging in power electronics, where it drives the simulation loop that engineers used to close largely by hand. In this loop, a converter design, or a study of the network it connects to, is built, run, measured and revised until it satisfies its specification.
