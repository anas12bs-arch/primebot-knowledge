# A Case Against the "Conventional Wisdom" of Investing

In his new book 'The Awesome Portfolio', Jared Dillian makes the argument against the conventional wisdom to invest your cash as "you buy the index, you dollar cost average, you hold, you ride out the volatility." He argues "cash can also be your friend." He joins Businessweek Daily to discuss he new book and latest market headlines. (Source: Bloomberg)
