# Dollar’s Reliance on Equity Inflows Seen Weighing on Currency

The dollar’s increasing dependence on equity inflows could erode the diversification benefit of leaving US assets unhedged, according to Deutsche Bank AG strategists, potentially putting downward pressure on the currency over time.
