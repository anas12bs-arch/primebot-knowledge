# Ukraine Peace Push Returns to Abu Dhabi

US, Ukrainian and Russian delegations are scheduled to meet in Abu Dhabi on November 10th and 11th, with Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner expected to represent the US. Joining Bloomberg This Weekend former Deputy Secretary of State Stephen Biegun discusses how a Democratic-controlled Congress could bring greater scrutiny to President Donald Trump’s foreign policy as the US renews efforts to negotiate an end to Russia’s war in Ukraine.
