# Compose the Horizon: Parameter-Shared Blockwise Forecasting under a Sequential-Compute Trade-off

Most long-horizon forecasters emit the complete future in one pass. We ask whether a compact operator can instead be reused to emit contiguous future blocks, trading stored parameters for sequential computation. Recursive Functional Decomposition (RFD) factorizes the horizon mapping into three parameter-shared block predictions. The blocks occupy disjoint future intervals and are concatenated, not added as trend, seasonal, or residual components, and each emitted block joins the rolling context
