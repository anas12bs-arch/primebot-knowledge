# Complementarity without ground truth?

Human-AI complementarity captures the idea that a human assisted by an AI system can outperform both the human and the AI acting alone. In prediction tasks, this improvement is assessed against ground truth labels produced through a reference construction procedure and intended to represent the state of affairs to be predicted.
