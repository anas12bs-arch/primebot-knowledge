# Systems Like Supply Chains are Being ‘Weaponized’: Modi

Indian PM Narendra Modi warned that the interconnected systems that once united the world like supply chains, trade and tech have become instruments of coercion.

He said at the Bloomberg New Economy Forum that India stands apart with a different approach. (Source: Bloomberg)
