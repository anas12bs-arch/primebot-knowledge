# Quenched Ensemble Sampling

Some of the sharpest challenges in sampling from the energy functions of physical systems arise at phase transitions, where the density of states changes abruptly and many sampling algorithms stall. Nested sampling is a particle method that traverses the density of states under a hard energy constraint and is known to be robust to such transitions, but its application in high dimension is limited by the difficulty of sampling under that constraint.
