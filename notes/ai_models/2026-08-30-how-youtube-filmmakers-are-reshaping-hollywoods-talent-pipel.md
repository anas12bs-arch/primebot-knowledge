# How YouTube Filmmakers Are Reshaping Hollywood’s Talent Pipeline

YouTube creators are turning online audiences into big-screen opportunities, with filmmakers like Alex Kister moving from DIY horror videos to studio deals. As platforms, distributors and FAST services open new paths to money and viewers, the creator boom is also flooding the market with content, making it easier to start but harder to win.
