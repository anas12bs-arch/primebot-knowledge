# IAEA's Grossi Says Iran Nuclear Inspections Can Resume Fast

IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi joins Bloomberg's David Gura at the United Nations General Assembly to discuss the nuclear situation in Iran. He says there is still a fairly important amount of nuclear material in Iran and that the IAEA can get on the ground and assess pretty fast if there is the political will for it. (Source: Bloomberg)
