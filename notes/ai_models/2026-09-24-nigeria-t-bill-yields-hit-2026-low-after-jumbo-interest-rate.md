# Nigeria T-Bill Yields Hit 2026 Low After Jumbo Interest-Rate Cut

Nigeria’s central bank sold short-dated debt at the lowest yields this year as investors rushed to lock in returns after the biggest interest-rate cut in almost two decades stoked expectations that future offerings will pay less.
