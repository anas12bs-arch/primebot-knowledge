# What a Drop Reveals: Sim-to-Real Material Estimation for Deformable Objects from Video

How an object deforms when it hits the ground reveals what it is made of. From a single real video of a drop, we estimate a deformable object's density-normalized stiffness $E/ρ$, and its yield stress $σ_Y/ρ$ when the impact causes plastic deformation, with a feed-forward predictor trained only in simulation.
