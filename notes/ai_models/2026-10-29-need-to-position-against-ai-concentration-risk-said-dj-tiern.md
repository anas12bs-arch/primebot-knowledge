# Need to Position Against AI Concentration Risk, Said D.J. Tierney

D.J. Tierney, senior investment portfolio strategist at Schwab Asset Management, said that 'we don't know' when the party is over for the AI trade and that Schwab is advising investors to start making changes at the margins to position themselves to insure against concentration risk.
