# Temporal Conditioning for Human Behavioral Cloning in Chess

Behavioral cloning models in chess typically predict human moves from board states while ignoring temporal inputs such as the remaining clock. However, time pressure is a major determinant of human decision-making quality, and temporal state is a missing dimension of human behavioral modeling.
