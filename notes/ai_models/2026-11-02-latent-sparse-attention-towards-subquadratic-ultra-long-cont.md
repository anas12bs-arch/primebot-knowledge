# Latent Sparse Attention: Towards Subquadratic Ultra-Long Context Attention

Agentic workflows are increasingly shifting towards long-horizon tasks, such as open-ended scientific work, where models benefit from long context windows. However, at ultra-long context (above 1M tokens), full attention is prohibitively expensive. A promising approach is sparse attention, in which a dedicated indexer selects a subset of keys for the attention.
