# Ayala Corp. CEO Consing on Strategic Deal With Mitsubishi

Cezar Consing, President and CEO of Ayala Corp., says the company will cut debt and acquire additional shares in undervalued units using proceeds from its $700 million share sale to Mitsubishi. He speaks on "Bloomberg: The China Show." (Source: Bloomberg)
