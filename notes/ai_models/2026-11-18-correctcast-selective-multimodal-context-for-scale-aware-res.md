# CorrectCast: Selective Multimodal Context for Scale-Aware Residual Correction in Precipitation Nowcasting

Precipitation nowcasting models suffer from errors in intensity, location, and spatial structure. Addressing these errors by retraining nowcaster backbones can be computationally expensive and requires architecture-specific modifications. To overcome these limitations, we propose CorrectCast, a decoupled residual correction framework that refines completed nowcasts while keeping the base nowcaster frozen.
