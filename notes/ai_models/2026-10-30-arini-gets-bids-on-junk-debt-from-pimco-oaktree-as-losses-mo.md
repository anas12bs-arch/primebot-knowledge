# Arini Gets Bids on Junk Debt From Pimco, Oaktree as Losses Mount

Arini Capital Management, which has been beset with losses this month, has gotten bids on some of its high-yield debt holdings from asset managers including Pacific Investment Management Co. and Oaktree Capital Management, according to people familiar with the matter.
