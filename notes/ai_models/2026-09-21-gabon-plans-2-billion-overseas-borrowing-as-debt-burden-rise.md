# Gabon Plans $2 Billion Overseas Borrowing as Debt Burden Rises

Gabon plans to raise about $2 billion on international markets next year to help finance its budget and refinance maturing debt, underscoring the government’s continued reliance on external borrowing even after a recent audit lowered its estimate of outstanding debt.
