# A Scaling Theory of Multi-Epoch Pretraining: Late-Time Overfitting and Ensembling

In the near future language model pretraining will become bound by total data rather than total compute. Such a regime requires revisiting scaling laws and scaling law theory. Motivated by prior works that have found utility in ensembling, we study how the number of training epochs and ensemble size jointly shape generalization when training data are fixed.
