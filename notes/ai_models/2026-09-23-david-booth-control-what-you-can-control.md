# David Booth: Control What You Can Control

Dimensional Fund Advisors Founder and Chairman David Booth explains his investment philosophy of staying calm during periods of market volatility. He explains why he didn't panic during the 2020 pandemic, when markets collapsed. He's on this week's episode of The David Rubenstein Show: Peer to Peer Conversations. This interview was recorded August 10 in New York.
