# NYC Airports Disrupted by Air Traffic Control Outage

Flights at New York’s major airports were disrupted after a technical problem at an air traffic control facility in Philadelphia. A so-called ground stop was in effect at John F. Kennedy International, LaGuardia, and Newark Liberty International. Sid Philip reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
