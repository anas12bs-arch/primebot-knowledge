# CueClash: Frontier Vision-Language Models Judge 3-D Relations Close to the Picture Plane

A vision-language model (VLM) can judge 3-D relations from the picture or from the ground layout. In photographs the two usually agree. We separate them with CueClash, rendered scenes in which the 2-D cue for each of five relations is right on exactly half the statements. The shortcut gap is accuracy when the cue is right minus accuracy when it is wrong. For Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.6 Flash at low reasoning effort it is +18 to +37 percentage points.
