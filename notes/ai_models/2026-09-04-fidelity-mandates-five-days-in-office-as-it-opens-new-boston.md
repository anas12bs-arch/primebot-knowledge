# Fidelity Mandates Five Days in Office as it Opens New Boston Home

Fidelity is opening a new 735,000-square-foot headquarters in Boston, complete with harbor views, a private bar and an in-office concierge. The move comes as the investment giant ends hybrid work for its Boston employees, requiring them to come into the office five days a week. Greg Ryan reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
