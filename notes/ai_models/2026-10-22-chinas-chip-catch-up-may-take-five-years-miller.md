# China’s Chip Catch-Up May Take Five Years: Miller

“Chip War” author Chris Miller says China may need five years or longer to catch up in advanced chips. Speaking on the sidelines of the Barclays Asia Forum in Singapore, he said restrictions on access to cutting-edge chipmaking equipment constrain the country’s capabilities. He appeared on Bloomberg’s Insight with Haslinda Amin. (Source: Bloomberg)
