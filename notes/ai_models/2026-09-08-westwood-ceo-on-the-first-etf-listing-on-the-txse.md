# Westwood CEO on the First ETF Listing on the TXSE

Brian Casey of Westwood appears on the program as the segment tees up Westwood Salient Enhanced Power & Infrastructure ETF, ticker PWRX, as the Texas Stock Exchange’s first new listing. The actively managed ETF is designed to capitalize on accelerating power needs, pairing an income strategy with exposure to high-growth energy and infrastructure themes. He speaks with Romaine Bostick & Tyler Kendall on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
