# Text-Aware, Vision-Blind: How Multimodal Image Editing Systems Handle Unsafe Inputs

Multimodal image generation systems have made rapid progress and increasingly emphasize content safety. However, their safety mechanisms tend to rely on a single modality and lack joint consideration of both visual content and text prompts, particularly in instruction-guided image editing, where the uploaded image may itself carry risk.
