# Soybeans Hit Two-Month Low as Lula Win Lifts China Trade Outlook

Soybean futures fell to a two-month low in Chicago after Lula’s election win in Brazil raised expectations of closer ties with China, potentially eroding the US share of the world’s biggest soybean import market.
