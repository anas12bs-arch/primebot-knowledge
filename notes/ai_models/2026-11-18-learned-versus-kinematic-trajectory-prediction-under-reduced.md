# Learned versus Kinematic Trajectory Prediction under Reduced Observation Frequency

Trajectory prediction models for automated driving are usually trained on data with a fixed sampling frequency, while positions received via vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication arrive at 1-10 Hz. We study whether a learned predictor that uses only the observation history keeps its advantage over constant velocity (CV) and constant acceleration (CA) models when the history frequency drops.
