# Beyond Truncation? Entailment-Licensed Elision and the Limits of Semantic Preservation in LLM Decoding

Instruction-tuned large language models (LLMs) answer at greater length than a request needs. We ask whether a training-free controller inside the decoding loop can remove that surplus while preserving content, judged against the simplest possible baseline: truncating the model's own answer to the same length.
