# GEOHunter: Mapping a Generative Engine Optimization Network in the Wild

Generative engines synthesize retrieved web pages into answers, recommendations, and rankings. Generative engine optimization (GEO) services sell visibility in these answers by publishing promotional articles on user-generated-content (UGC) platforms for paying clients. Such promotion raises concerns in medical and financial domains, where misleading advice can cause harm.
