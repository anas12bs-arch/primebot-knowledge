# Russian Satellite Raises Space Warfare Fears

Clayton Swope, deputy director of the CSIS Aerospace Security Project, said that we shouldn't look at movement in Space as separate from what countries do on on earth, after a Russian satellite approached an American satellite used by Ukraine. Swope said that the move should be evaluated as an 'example of Russia doing hybrid warfare zone activities that threaten US interests and European interests' and is all part of the same geopolitical problem as the Russia Ukraine war.  (Source: Bloomberg)
