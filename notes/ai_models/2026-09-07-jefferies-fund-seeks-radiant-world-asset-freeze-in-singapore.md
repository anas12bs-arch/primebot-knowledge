# Jefferies Fund Seeks Radiant World Asset Freeze in Singapore

A fund run by a Jefferies Financial Group Inc. subsidiary is seeking a freezing injunction against iron ore trader Radiant World and its founder Pinkesh Nahar in Singapore, days after securing a worldwide freezing order against them from a UK court.
