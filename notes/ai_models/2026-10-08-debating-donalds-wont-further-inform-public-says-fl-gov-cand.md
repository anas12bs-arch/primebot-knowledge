# Debating Donalds Won't Further Inform Public, Says FL Gov Candidate Jolly

David Jolly, the Democrat candidate for Governor in Florida, said that debating Republican Byron Donalds won't further inform the public and would just platform division and vitriol in the race. Jolly, who served in Congress as a Republican, said that he is honest with voters about the ideological differences between himself and Democrat Senate candidate Angie Nixon and that he is less worried about the rise of socialism in America than he is 'right-wing authoritarianism.'
