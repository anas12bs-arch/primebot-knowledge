# Torani CEO Melanie Dulbecco on Manufacturing, Company Culture

Torani CEO Melanie Dulbecco joins Businessweek Daily in celebration of National Coffee Day. The 101-year-old flavor company has surpassed $800 million in annual revenue, continues to grow more than 20% year over year, and is approaching the $1 billion mark faster than anticipated, as changing beverage culture drives continued investment in U.S. manufacturing and its workforce. Dulbecco discusses the company's evolution with Bloomberg's Carol Massar and Tim Stenovec. (Source: Bloomberg)
