# US-China Have Time to Discuss Trade Truce Extension, Greer Says

Trade Representative Jamieson Greer indicated China and the US remain divided on the terms of a trade truce extension. Asked if a three-to six-month extension was under discussion, Greer said “that’s probably the right kind of range” though it’s unlikely to be announced this week. He spoke on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
