# Announced Activity In Market Is Slower: Friedland

David Friedland, co-head of North America investment banking at Citi, joins Dani Burger on "Bloomberg Deals." Citigroup is slashing the time its investment-banking analysts need to wait to advance to associate level, part of a push by the bank to attract and retain top talent in a fierce job market that’s seen buyout firms poach aggressively from Wall Street.
