# Citi Makes It Easier for Junior Bankers to Advance

Citigroup Inc. will now advance some investment banking analysts to associates in two years instead of three. The move gives bankers a faster path to more responsibility and higher pay, while the bank gets to keep top talent. Bloomberg's Sridhar Natarajan reports.  (Source: Bloomberg)
