# Exchanges Urge CFTC to Block Kalshi’s Perpetual Oil Futures Plan

Commodity exchanges are urging US regulators to withhold approval of Kalshi Inc.’s proposed perpetual oil futures contract and close a loophole that gives retail traders access to offshore platforms.
