# SpaceX Pushes Starship Reusability to New Test

Space Network co-founder Ross Hamilton tells Bloomberg This Weekend why SpaceX’s attempt to catch Starship after its return from orbit could mark a major step toward making the rocket system fully reusable. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Hamilton explains how more frequent and less expensive launches could accelerate Starlink expansion, orbital data centers and SpaceX’s broader ambitions in space.
