# Attribution Through the Decoder: Prediction-Space Explanations for Structured Outputs

Networks that predict angles, gaze directions, and rotations do not output these quantities directly: they emit raw coordinates that a fixed decoder, such as normalization, maps to the final prediction. Attribution methods are usually applied to the raw coordinates, so they can credit inputs for changes the decoder discards, such as rescaling a vector when only its direction matters.
