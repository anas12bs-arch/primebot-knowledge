# Russian Oil Uncertainty Hangs Over India’s Largest Buyers

Indian state-owned refiners are facing the most significant supply uncertainty in months, according to people with direct knowledge of the trade, as the impact of a drawn-out conflict in the Middle East is compounded by the prospect of tighter US restrictions on Russian purchases.
