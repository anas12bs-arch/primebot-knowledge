# Typhoon Champi Shuts Taiwan’s $5 Trillion Stock Market

Taiwan’s stock exchange will shut Friday as Typhoon Champi approaches the island, bringing heavy rainfall and strong winds that prompted authorities to shut offices and schools in the capital city of Taipei.
