# Fortescue Says Iron Ore Deal With China Must Protect Australia

Fortescue Ltd.’s top executives said the miner won’t rush into a deal with China’s state-backed iron ore buyer, and that any agreement must protect the company’s long-term interests as well as Australia’s.
