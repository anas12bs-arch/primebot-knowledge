# Trump, Xi and Putin Set for Rare APEC Trilateral Talks

US President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin are set to hold a rare trilateral meeting Thursday on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen. Reporting from Shenzhen, Bloomberg’s Stephen Engle says that while the White House is framing the talks around denuclearization, the conflicts in Ukraine and Iran are likely to come up in discussions.
