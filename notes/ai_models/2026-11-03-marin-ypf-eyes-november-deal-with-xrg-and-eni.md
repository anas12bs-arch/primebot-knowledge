# Marin: YPF Eyes November Deal with XRG and Eni

YPF Chairman and CEO Horacio Marin says the company is working with Eni and XRG and aims to sign an agreement by the end of November. Marin spoke to Bloomberg’s Abeer Abu Omar about the project and the need for more energy supply amid conflicts in the Middle East and the Russia-Ukraine war. (Source: Bloomberg)
