# Conformal Uncertainty Quantification Guarantees for Neural Operators

Neural operators provide fast surrogate models for approximating operators between function spaces, but their predictions often lack uncertainty quantification. We develop a split conformal framework to guarantee that a calibrated pointwise band around the neural operator output contains the true solution on at least a $1-γ$ fraction of the evaluation domain, with probability at least $1-α$ over test and calibration inputs, where $α,γ\in(0,1)$.
