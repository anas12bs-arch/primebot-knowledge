# Jackson Hole a Bigger Market Risk Than Nvidia for Allspring’s Miletti

Ann Miletti, head of equity investments and chief diversity officer at Allspring Global Investments, says she is keeping expectations low heading into the Jackson Hole Symposium and sees it posing a greater near-term market risk than Nvidia earnings. She speaks with Dani Burger on "Bloomberg Open Interest." (Source: Bloomberg)
