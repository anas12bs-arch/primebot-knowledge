# US Formally Concludes Operation Inherent Resolve in Iraq

The US has formally concluded Operation Inherent Resolve in Iraq with the departure of coalition forces and equipment, according to a Pentagon statement. Lt. General Karen Gibson (Retired), Former-Deputy Director of National Intelligence joined to discuss this as well as the latest in the Middle East.  (Source: Bloomberg)
