# Montage Intl. CEO: Future Bookings Are Higher Into 2027

Alan Fuerstman, Founder and CEO of Montage International, said luxury travel demand has remained resilient despite higher costs and geopolitical uncertainty. He described the business as coming off a record year and said future bookings are up going into next year, with record holiday-period bookings at resorts across the U.S. and Mexico. He speaks with Scarlet Fu & Kristine Aquino on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
