# Is AI a Bubble? History Offers Some Clues

Bloomberg Opinion columnist Justin Fox tells Bloomberg This Weekend that history offers warning signs for financial bubbles, but little reliable guidance about exactly when they will burst, making debt and investor sentiment important risks to watch amid the AI boom. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Fox explains why AI can be a transformative technology while its companies and investments are still vulnerable to inflated expectations and a painful market correction.
