# Click3R: Interactive Stereo 3D Reconstruction with Sparse Correspondence Clicks

Despite impressive progress, existing feed-forward large 3D reconstruction models produce a single, irrevocable prediction. When these models fail, there is no mechanism to correct their mistakes. To address this limitation, we introduce Click3R, a framework for interactive geometric correction of feed-forward models using sparse human correspondence clicks.
