# Indonesia Holds Rates in ‘Policy Mix’ to Support FX and Growth

Bank Indonesia kept its benchmark interest rate unchanged, while cutting reserve requirements and raising deposit rates, a mix of policies designed to pursue competing objectives following the market volatility triggered by MSCI Inc.’s downgrade threat.
