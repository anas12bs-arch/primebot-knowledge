# Bilt CEO, Equinox Exec. Chairman on New Partnership

The segment features a discussion with Ankur Jain, CEO of BILT, and Harvey Spevak, Equinox Group Executive Chairman, about the newly launched exclusive partnership between Bilt, a membership and hospitality platform, and Equinox, a luxury fitness brand. The collaboration aims to connect the audiences of both brands by allowing Bilt members to redeem Bilt cash for credits that can be used toward Equinox experiences.
