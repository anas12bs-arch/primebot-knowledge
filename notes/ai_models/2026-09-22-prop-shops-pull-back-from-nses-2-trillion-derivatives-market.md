# Prop Shops Pull Back From NSE’s $2 Trillion Derivatives Market

Proprietary traders’ share of equity derivatives turnover has fallen to the lowest level in almost four years at the National Stock Exchange of India Ltd., as problems with the closing auction mechanism add to an increasingly restrictive trading environment.
