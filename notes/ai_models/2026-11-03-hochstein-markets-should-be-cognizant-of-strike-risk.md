# Hochstein: Markets Should Be Cognizant of Strike Risk

Oil is steadying after a sharp drop as hopes build that the US and Iran could be moving closer to a negotiated settlement. Amos Hochstein, managing partner at TWG Global and a former adviser to President Joe Biden, says markets should remain prepared for the possibility of further strikes even as talks continue.
