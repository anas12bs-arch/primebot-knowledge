# Athleisure Faces Price Pressures

Regis Schultz, CEO of JD Sports, discusses the state of sports fashion amid the popularity of running. He says the US is "the most resilient customer" and that JD stores haven't felt much tariff impact because the brands they carry are taking most of the hit. Schultz gives his expectations for the holiday season and which brands will interest the sports fashion consumer. He joins Paul Sweeney and Avalon Pernell on Bloomberg Intelligence.
