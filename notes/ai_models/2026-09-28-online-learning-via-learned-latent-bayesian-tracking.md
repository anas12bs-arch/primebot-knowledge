# Online Learning via Learned Latent Bayesian Tracking

Online learning in non-stationary environments requires models to adapt rapidly from streaming data under strict computational constraints. A principled approach casts online learning as Bayesian state tracking, where model parameters are updated sequentially via Bayesian filtering.
