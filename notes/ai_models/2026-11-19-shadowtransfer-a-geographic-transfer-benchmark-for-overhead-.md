# ShadowTransfer: A Geographic Transfer Benchmark for Overhead Shadow Detection

Overhead aerial imagery feeds dense-prediction pipelines that are routinely trained in one geographic region and deployed in another, yet no benchmark measures how shadow detection models transfer across regions. Existing overhead shadow datasets either lack a geographic transfer protocol or rely on automatically generated labels. We introduce ShadowTransfer, a benchmark of 4,500 human-verified shadow masks across three U.S. cities at two native resolutions, paired with a leave-one-city-out eval
