# Why High Yields on Government Bonds Are Causing Alarm

Government borrowing costs have been surging around the world as investors demand more compensation to entice them to hold longer-maturity debt. A Bloomberg gauge tracking government debt across the G7 countries now yields its highest on average since September 2000. Michael MacKenzie reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
