# Vingroup Among Vietnam Stocks to Debut on FTSE’s EM Index

Vingroup JSC is among over 100 Vietnamese companies added to FTSE Russell’s Global Equity Indexes late Friday, marking a key step in the country’s long-awaited transition from frontier to secondary emerging market status.
