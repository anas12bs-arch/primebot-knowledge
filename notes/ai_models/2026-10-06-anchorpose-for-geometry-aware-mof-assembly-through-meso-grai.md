# AnchorPose for Geometry-Aware MOF Assembly through Meso-Grained Pose Generation

Predicting metal-organic framework (MOF) structures from given building blocks requires recovering their positions and orientations in a periodic crystal. The spatial effects of rotation errors are geometry-dependent and anisotropic. The same angular error can produce different atomic displacements depending on block size, shape, and rotation axis.
