# Composable Causality: A Toolkit for Systematic Time-Series Causal Discovery and Treatment-Effect Benchmarking

Real-world multivariate time series rarely come with verified ground-truth causal structure, so research on time-series causal discovery and treatment-effect estimation depends on synthetic data. Existing tools are either fixed datasets that cannot be modified after release or script-based generators in which each script fixes one combination of mechanism, confounding, and missingness.
