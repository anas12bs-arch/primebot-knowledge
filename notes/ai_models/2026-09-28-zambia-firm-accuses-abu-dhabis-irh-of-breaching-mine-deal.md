# Zambia Firm Accuses Abu Dhabi’s IRH of Breaching Mine Deal

A Zambian state firm has accused Abu Dhabi’s International Resources Holding of breaching agreements under which the Emirati company acquired control of the Mopani copper mining complex in early 2024.
