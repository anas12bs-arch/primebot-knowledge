# Universal neural network approximation of symmetric polyconvex energies and envelopes

Symmetric polyconvexity provides a sufficient condition for weak lower semicontinuity in small-strain elasticity and a tractable lower bound for the relaxation of non-(quasi)convex energies. Since classical algorithms for computing such functions can be computationally expensive, neural network approximations have recently emerged as an alternative.
