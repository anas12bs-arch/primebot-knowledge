# Pole or Vector? Encoders Capture Bangla Compound Verbs, but LLMs Read Them Literally

In Bangla, a main verb is often followed by one of a small set of vector verbs. These verbs lose their own meaning and add a shade of meaning instead, such as completion. For example, likhe phello means that someone finished writing, not that they wrote and threw something. The same two verbs can also describe two separate events, so only the context shows which meaning is meant. This makes compound verbs a hard test of how language models handle multiword expressions.
