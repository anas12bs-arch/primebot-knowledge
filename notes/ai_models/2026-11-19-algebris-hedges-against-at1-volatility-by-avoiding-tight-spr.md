# Algebris Hedges Against AT1 Volatility by Avoiding Tight Spreads

Algebris Investments is taking a cautious approach to AT1 bonds as spreads remain historically tight, avoiding the most expensive parts of the market and keeping buffers against a potential downturn. Other investors remain positive on the asset class, arguing that banks are well capitalized and that even French lenders remain fundamentally sound despite political and sovereign-market volatility.
