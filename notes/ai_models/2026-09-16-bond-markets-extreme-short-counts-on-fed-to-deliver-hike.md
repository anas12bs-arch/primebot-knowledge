# Bond Market’s ‘Extreme’ Short Counts on Fed to Deliver Hike

Bond traders have piled into bearish positions ahead of Wednesday’s Federal Reserve meeting, betting that the Treasury selloff driving yields to their highest in almost two decades will continue.
