# ProtoDiffusion: Token-per-Layer Generative Modeling of Multi-Layer Images

Graphic designs are ordered compositions of RGBA layers whose separation enables selection, movement, and reuse. Existing layered models represent each layer with a full-canvas or region-sized spatial latent grid, so representation size grows with the cumulative spatial extent of the layers. We present ProtoDiffusion, a latent-diffusion framework built on ProtoVAE, a multi-layer variational autoencoder that encodes a design into one shared spatial prototype and one candidate vector per layer.
