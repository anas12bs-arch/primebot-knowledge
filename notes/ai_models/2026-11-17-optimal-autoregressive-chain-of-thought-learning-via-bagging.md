# Optimal Autoregressive Chain-of-Thought Learning via Bagging

We study how to learn an autoregressive next-token generator $f\colonΣ^\star\toΣ$ from a class $\mathcal{F}$, given chain-of-thought (CoT) demonstrations of length $T$ generated by $f$. We present a simple algorithm based on bagging, which outputs a majority vote of generators that are trained to be CoT-consistent on bootstrap samples of the training data.
