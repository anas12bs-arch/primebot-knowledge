# Blue-Collar Workers Are Training AI To Take Their Jobs

Before robots can take over our jobs, they first need to learn how humans move.

With cameras strapped to their foreheads, blue-collar workers in India are training humanoid robots by filming how they use their hands in what might be AI's latest gold rush. (Source: Bloomberg)
