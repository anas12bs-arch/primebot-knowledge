# Goldman Warns of Hidden Credit Stress

Goldman Sachs’ chief credit strategist Amanda Lynam says today’s credit-market pressure is about dispersion, not widespread disruption. She joined Bloomberg Open Interest to explain why software borrowers face growing maturity risks, which private-credit warning signs matter most and how debt markets could help finance the massive AI buildout.
