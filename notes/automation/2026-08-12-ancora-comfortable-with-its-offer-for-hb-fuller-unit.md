# Ancora Comfortable With Its Offer for H.B. Fuller Unit

Ancora Holdings Group is pushing to acquire H.B. Fuller Co.'s building adhesives unit for as much as $1.2 billion. James Chadwick is the president of Ancora's alternatives arm. He says the activist investor is comfortable with the offer and it would be a "win-win" for shareholders. He speaks on "Bloomberg Deals." (Source: Bloomberg)
