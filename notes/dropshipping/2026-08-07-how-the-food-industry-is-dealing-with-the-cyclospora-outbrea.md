# How the Food Industry Is Dealing With the Cyclospora Outbreak

Since Taco Bell was linked to the cyclospora outbreak, the fast food chain removed the allegedly implicated lettuce from its supply chain and, in a bid to win back hesitant diners, began offering special discounts on a concoction known as the Enchirito.

Bloomberg Businessweek's Deena Shanker joins Max Chafkin and Stacey Vanek Smith on the Everybody's Business podcast to discuss how the food industry is dealing with the growing health crisis. (Source: Bloomberg)
