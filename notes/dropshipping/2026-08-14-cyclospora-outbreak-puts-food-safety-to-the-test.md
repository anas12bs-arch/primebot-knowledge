# Cyclospora Outbreak Puts Food Safety to the Test

A cyclospora outbreak and salmonella flare up are putting food safety and restaurants to the test in the US. It also caused consumers to cut back on eating out and food brands to take a closer look at their supply chains. Bloomberg's Romaine Bostick reports.  (Source: Bloomberg)
