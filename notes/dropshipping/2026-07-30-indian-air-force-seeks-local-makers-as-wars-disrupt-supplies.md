# Indian Air Force Seeks Local Makers as Wars Disrupt Supplies

Indian Air Force identified dozens of critical components it wants domestic manufacturers to produce after wars in Ukraine and the Middle East disrupted defense supply chains, as part of a broader effort to boost self-reliance in military production.
